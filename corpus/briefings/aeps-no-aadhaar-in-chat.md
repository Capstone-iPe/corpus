iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether AePS onboarding requires collecting Aadhaar in chat or authenticating with UIDAI.

## Facts

Applicant offered Aadhaar for AePS enablement during onboarding chat.

## Rule

AePS briefing: do not collect or print Aadhaar in chat. Not a pack slot. No UIDAI call.

## Application

Owner ID may still be an Aadhaar letter as a file. Chat collection of the number for AePS is out of product. Do not print. Do not collect OTP. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether AePS onboarding requires collecting Aadhaar in chat or authenticating with UIDAI. On these facts (Applicant offered Aadhaar for AePS enablement during onboarding chat.) the held is: AePS is not a slot and not a UIDAI path. No reprint. Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

AePS is not a slot and not a UIDAI path. No reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print Aadhaar. Do not collect OTP.
