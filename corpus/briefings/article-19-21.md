iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether freedom of trade waives documents, or whether privacy requires reading numbers back.

## Facts

The merchant cited Article 19 to skip files, or Article 21 to demand read-back, or both.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-002: constitutional citations do not rewrite §§2–4. Article 21 supports ONB-POL-004 rather than read-back.

## Application

Trade freedom does not compel a private (fictional) acquirer to drop CDD or to onboard gambling. Privacy supports not echoing identity and account numbers, not the opposite. The Copilot shall not waive, shall not print, and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether freedom of trade waives documents, or whether privacy requires reading numbers back. On these facts (The merchant cited Article 19 to skip files, or Article 21 to demand read-back, or both.) the held is: No waiver from Article 19. No read-back from Article 21. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No waiver from Article 19. No read-back from Article 21.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not waive. Do not print numbers.
