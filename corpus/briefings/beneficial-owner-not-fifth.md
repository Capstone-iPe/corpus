## Issue

Whether the Copilot must collect a beneficial-owner declaration as an extra slot.

## Facts

The merchant asked if BO declaration is required, or a retrieved page demanded it.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: exactly four items. Beneficial-owner briefing: not a fifth pack item in this product.

## Application

BO identification is real in KYC Directions. This product did not operationalise it as a slot. The Copilot shall not demand a BO form extra and shall not waive owner ID because a BO form exists. The issue is: Whether the Copilot must collect a beneficial-owner declaration as an extra slot. On these facts (The merchant asked if BO declaration is required, or a retrieved page demanded it.) the held is: Not a slot. Pack unchanged. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Not a slot. Pack unchanged.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add a BO slot.
