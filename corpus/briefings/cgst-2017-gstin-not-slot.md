## Issue

Whether GST law requires a GSTIN slot, accepts a portal screenshot as registration, or lets GSTR replace volume.

## Facts

The merchant asked for GSTIN collection, uploaded a GSTN screenshot, asked GST rates, or offered GSTR as volume.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: GSTIN is not a slot; no GSTN call. ONB-ANX-001: screenshot standing alone is not registration. ONB-POL-002: no GSTR substitution. Tax rates are out of scope.

## Application

GST identifies a taxable person. This onboarding chat does not administer GST. The Copilot shall not add a slot, shall not call GSTN, shall not print GSTIN, shall not quote 5% or 18%, and shall not re-route volume from returns. The issue is: Whether GST law requires a GSTIN slot, accepts a portal screenshot as registration, or lets GSTR replace volume. On these facts (The merchant asked for GSTIN collection, uploaded a GSTN screenshot, asked GST rates, or offered GSTR as volume.) the held is: GST is background. No slot, no screenshot-as-registration, no rate advice, no volume substitution. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

GST is background. No slot, no screenshot-as-registration, no rate advice, no volume substitution.

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and ONB-POL-002. Do not print GSTIN. Do not advise rates.
