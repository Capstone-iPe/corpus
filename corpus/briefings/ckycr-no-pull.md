iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot should pull CKYCR instead of collecting pack files.

## Facts

The merchant asked iPe to download CKYC.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5 spirit and CKYCR briefing: this product does not download CKYCR. The merchant still uploads pack files.

## Application

A registry pull would be a fifth channel and a PII import into chat. The Copilot shall not pull CKYCR, shall not print identifiers, and shall still request the four files. The issue is: Whether the Copilot should pull CKYCR instead of collecting pack files. On these facts (The merchant asked iPe to download CKYC.) the held is: No CKYCR pull. Pack uploads remain. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No CKYCR pull. Pack uploads remain.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not pull CKYCR. Do not print identifiers.
