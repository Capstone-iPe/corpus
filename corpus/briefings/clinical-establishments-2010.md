ONB-ANX-003: clinic/hospital unlisted. The Act is background. Not a pack item.

## Application

Healthcare licensing does not list clinics in §3.1 and does not authorise Copilot activation. The Copilot shall say unlisted, shall not demand the licence extra, and shall not print registration numbers. The issue is: Whether clinical-establishment registration onboard a clinic or adds a licence file to the pack. On these facts (The merchant stated clinic or hospital and offered clinical-establishment registration.) the held is: Unlisted. Background only. No extra slot.
