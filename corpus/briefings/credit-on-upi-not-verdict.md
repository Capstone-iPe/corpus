iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether credit-on-UPI product news changes pack, type, or activation.

## Facts

The merchant asked if credit-on-UPI means they are live or need fewer files.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: product features are not onboarding verdict inputs.

## Application

Rails innovation is not a reduced pack and not Copilot activation. Say the onboarding policies do not contain that product fact. Do not invent eligibility. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether credit-on-UPI product news changes pack, type, or activation. On these facts (The merchant asked if credit-on-UPI means they are live or need fewer files.) the held is: Out of onboarding verdict. Pack and type unchanged. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-004; NPCI briefing.

## Held

Out of onboarding verdict. Pack and type unchanged. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not invent UPI-credit rules. Do not activate.
