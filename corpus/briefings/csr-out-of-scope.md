## Issue

Whether CSR spend, Schedule VII, or a CSR registration is a pack item or a type.

## Facts

Applicant asked to record CSR or offered CSR registration as KYC.

## Rule

CSR briefing: out of scope. Not a pack item.

## Application

CSR is corporate social spend. It does not fill registration, bank proof, or shopfront. Do not add a slot. Do not print CIN. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether CSR spend, Schedule VII, or a CSR registration is a pack item or a type. On these facts (Applicant asked to record CSR or offered CSR registration as KYC.) the held is: CSR is out of onboarding verdict. No extra slot. Cite ONB-POL-004.

## Held

CSR is out of onboarding verdict. No extra slot.
