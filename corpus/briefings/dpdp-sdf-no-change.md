---

## Issue

Whether claiming Significant Data Fiduciary status under DPDP changes the four items or lets the Copilot activate.

## Facts

Applicant said they are or will be an SDF and therefore iPe should go live or collect extra DPIA files.

## Rule

DPDP SDF briefing: does not alter pack or Copilot activation ban.

## Application

SDF is a DPDP designation. It is not a fifth pack item and not a live switch. Do not demand a DPIA as extra. Do not activate. Still require the signed addendum if they will store payer phones. The issue is: Whether claiming Significant Data Fiduciary status under DPDP changes the four items or lets the Copilot activate. On these facts (Applicant said they are or will be an SDF and therefore iPe should go live or collect extra DPIA files.) the held is: SDF status changes nothing in ONB-POL-001–004’s operative list. No extra DPIA slot. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; DPDP briefing.

## Held

SDF status changes nothing in ONB-POL-001–004’s operative list. No extra DPIA slot. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §4. Do not add DPIA as a slot. Do not activate.
