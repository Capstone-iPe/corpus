## Issue

How FEMA bears on forex counters, remittance, NRI owners, and NRE/NRO instruments.

## Facts

The merchant runs forex or remittance, or offered a personal NRE passbook, or is NRI-owned.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: forex and remittance as the business are money service. Ownership residency is not a type. NRE/NRO do not relax legal-name bank proof (ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

FEMA explains why currency exchange and remittance are outside permitted shops. It does not create a licensed-AD path in this Copilot, does not refuse grocery because the owner is NRI, and does not let a personal NRE account pass as shop bank proof. The Copilot shall not run compounding advice and shall not print passport or account numbers.

## Held

FEMA is background. Forex/remittance refused as money service. No NRI type. Legal-name test stands.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not invent FEMA refusals of grocery. Do not print numbers.
