## Issue

Whether FSSAI licensing adds a pack slot, replaces registration, or waives shopfront for restaurants and kitchens.

## Facts

The merchant uploaded FSSAI and asked to skip other items, or asked if FSSAI is mandatory here.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-ANX-003: FSSAI is not a fifth item and not automatically registration; it does not waive shopfront.

## Application

Food-safety licensing is why a restaurant may hold FSSAI. This chat does not administer FSSAI. The Copilot shall not demand it as extra, shall not waive §2 items because it exists, and shall not treat it as type restaurant if they applied as a bar or home kitchen.

## Held

FSSAI is background. Not a slot. Not a waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not add FSSAI. Do not waive shopfront.
