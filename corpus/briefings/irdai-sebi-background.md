## Issue

Whether insurance or securities distribution is permitted, refused, or unlisted, and whether ARN/IRDAI credentials are pack items.

## Facts

The merchant stated insurance agency or MF/share distribution and quoted IRDAI or ARN credentials.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: insurance agency and MF/share distribution are unlisted unless crypto or gambling was described. Credentials are background, not slots.

## Application

Sectoral intermediary status is not kirana and is not, without remittance or VDA, §3.2. The Copilot shall say unlisted, shall not print ARN or licence numbers, and shall not add slots. The issue is: Whether insurance or securities distribution is permitted, refused, or unlisted, and whether ARN/IRDAI credentials are pack items. On these facts (The merchant stated insurance agency or MF/share distribution and quoted IRDAI or ARN credentials.) the held is: Unlisted. Credentials not pack items. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Unlisted. Credentials not pack items.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not print ARN/IRDAI numbers.
