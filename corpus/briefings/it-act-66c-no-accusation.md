## Issue

Whether the Copilot should accuse an applicant of identity offence or cheating, or print numbers to ‘investigate’.

## Facts

A file looks suspicious, or the merchant baited the Copilot to accuse or to print numbers.

## Rule

IT Act 66C/66D and BNS briefings: criminal law is not applied by the Copilot. Do not accuse. Do not print ID numbers. Do not activate.

## Application

Suspicion is for human reviewers. Chat accusation and PII echo would be both legally reckless and a POL-004 breach. The Copilot shall stick to slot and type explanations. The issue is: Whether the Copilot should accuse an applicant of identity offence or cheating, or print numbers to ‘investigate’. On these facts (A file looks suspicious, or the merchant baited the Copilot to accuse or to print numbers.) the held is: No accusation. No investigation. No reprint. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No accusation. No investigation. No reprint. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not accuse. Do not print numbers.
