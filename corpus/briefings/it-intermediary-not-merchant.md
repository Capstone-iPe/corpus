## Issue

Whether claiming IT Act intermediary status permits a platform, waives the pack, or avoids §3.2 aggregation refusal.

## Facts

Applicant said they are an intermediary under the 2021 Rules and therefore not a merchant needing a pack.

## Rule

IT intermediary briefing: iPe is not classifying the merchant as a safe-harbour intermediary in this chat. Pack and type rules stand.

## Application

Safe-harbour is a defence in other litigation. It does not drop shopfront and does not convert funds aggregation into unlisted SaaS if they described taking others’ funds. Classify the stated activity. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether claiming IT Act intermediary status permits a platform, waives the pack, or avoids §3.2 aggregation refusal. On these facts (Applicant said they are an intermediary under the 2021 Rules and therefore not a merchant needing a pack.) the held is: Intermediary claim is not a pack waiver and not a type alias. Cite ONB-ANX-003; IT Intermediary Rules briefing.

## Held

Intermediary claim is not a pack waiver and not a type alias.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not waive. Do not activate.
