## Issue

Whether reasonable-security practice requires the Copilot to store payer phones in the transcript for the reviewer.

## Facts

Applicant pasted customer phones ‘for security audit’ in chat.

## Rule

IT reasonable-security briefing: do not echo secrets. Do not store payer phones in the transcript.

## Application

A transcript dump is the opposite of reasonable security. Do not keep the list. Do not print it. Point to ONB-POL-003 if they will store phones operationally. The issue is: Whether reasonable-security practice requires the Copilot to store payer phones in the transcript for the reviewer. On these facts (Applicant pasted customer phones ‘for security audit’ in chat.) the held is: Do not retain phones in chat. Security talk is not a dump duty. Cite ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Do not retain phones in chat. Security talk is not a dump duty.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not echo the list.
