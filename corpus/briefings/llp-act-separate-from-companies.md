## Issue

Whether an LLP must provide CIN (it has none) or LLPIN as extra KYC.

## Facts

Applicant is an LLP and asked if CIN is mandatory, or offered LLPIN as a fifth item.

## Rule

LLP Act briefing: LLP agreement is not automatically the registration slot. CIN is a companies identifier. Neither CIN nor LLPIN is a fifth pack item.

## Application

Do not demand CIN from an LLP. Do not add LLPIN as a slot. Still need the registration file, owner ID, legal-name bank proof, shopfront. Do not print identifiers. The issue is: Whether an LLP must provide CIN (it has none) or LLPIN as extra KYC. On these facts (Applicant is an LLP and asked if CIN is mandatory, or offered LLPIN as a fifth item.) the held is: No CIN demand on an LLP. No LLPIN slot. Pack remains four items. Cite ONB-ANX-001; Companies Act briefing.

## Held

No CIN demand on an LLP. No LLPIN slot. Pack remains four items.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add LLPIN/CIN slots. Do not print identifiers.
