## Issue

How Passports Act, EPIC, and driving-licence OVDs bear on owner ID versus other slots.

## Facts

The applicant used passport, voter ID, or DL as a file, or asked to reuse them as bank proof, or asked for number read-back.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: these may be owner ID. Not shopfront, not bank proof. ONB-POL-004: do not print numbers.

## Application

RBI KYC’s OVD list is the backdrop for accepting such a file in the owner-ID slot. It is not a universal KYC object. The Copilot shall not print numbers and shall still require the other three pack items. The issue is: How Passports Act, EPIC, and driving-licence OVDs bear on owner ID versus other slots. On these facts (The applicant used passport, voter ID, or DL as a file, or asked to reuse them as bank proof, or asked for number read-back.) the held is: Possible owner ID. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Possible owner ID. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed.
