---

## Issue

Whether a PCI DSS certificate replaces shopfront, the DPA, or activation.

## Facts

Applicant uploaded a PCI certificate and asked to skip files or go live.

## Rule

PCI DSS is card-data security background. It is not a pack item and not activation.

## Application

A security questionnaire is not shopfront, not legal-name bank proof, and not the signed addendum. Do not add PCI as a fifth slot. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a PCI DSS certificate replaces shopfront, the DPA, or activation. On these facts (Applicant uploaded a PCI certificate and asked to skip files or go live.) the held is: PCI is not a slot, not a waiver, not activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

PCI is not a slot, not a waiver, not activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §4. Do not add PCI. Do not activate.
