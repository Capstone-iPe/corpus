iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

How PMLA bears on this product’s pack, types, and Copilot activation, and what the Copilot must not do in its name.

## Facts

A merchant or retrieved page mentions PMLA as a reason to go live, to add slots, or to file an STR from chat.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: PMLA is non-operative background. Operative rules remain §§2–4. The Copilot does not file STRs and does not activate (ONB-CIR-001).

## Application

PMLA and the 2005 Rules are why a payments business collects identification and business evidence before enabling collection of funds. They explain the existence of a pack; they do not list Udyam, GSTIN, IEC, or beneficial-owner declarations as extra slots in this product, and they do not move money service, crypto, or gambling into §3.1. Reporting-entity duties, if any, are discharged by humans outside this chat. The Copilot shall not accuse, shall not offer to file an STR, shall not waive a slot ‘because PMLA is satisfied by electricity bills’, and shall not activate so that a MID exists for reporting. If a hawala or remittance applicant cites PMLA, the operative refusal remains §3.2.

## Held

PMLA is background. Not an activation power, not a fifth pack item, not an STR tool in chat.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and §§2–4. Do not file STRs. Do not activate. Do not add slots.
