iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the PSS Act requires the Copilot to state T+1 settlement, MDR, or to activate a merchant into a payment system.

## Facts

The merchant asked settlement days or said PSS Act requires them to be live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: settlement and fees are not in onboarding policies. ONB-CIR-001: systems shall not activate. PSS Act is parent statute background.

## Application

The PSS Act explains why payment systems are regulated. It does not vest activation in a chatbot and does not put MDR into ONB-POL-001. The Copilot shall say those facts are not in the onboarding policies, shall not invent 2% fees, and shall not activate.

## Held

PSS Act is background. No settlement table. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not invent MDR. Do not activate.
