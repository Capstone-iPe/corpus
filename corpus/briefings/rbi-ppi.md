iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether PPI regulation makes a wallet screen into bank proof or creates a permitted wallet-issuer type.

## Facts

The merchant uploaded a wallet screenshot, or stated prepaid wallet issuance as the business.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: wallet screenshots fail bank proof. ONB-ANX-003: prepaid wallet issuer is money service.

## Application

PPI rules govern stored-value instruments. They do not turn a screenshot into a legal-name bank letter, and they do not list wallet issuance in §3.1. The Copilot shall request proper bank proof for a permitted shop, and shall refuse a wallet-issuer type without an id.

## Held

PPI is background. Screenshot ≠ bank proof. Issuer type refused.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 or ONB-ANX-003 as applicable. Do not print wallet numbers.
