## Issue

Whether RERA registration permits a broker as retail or must be collected as KYC.

## Facts

The merchant stated real estate broker and quoted a RERA number.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: brokers unlisted. RERA registration is not a pack slot. ONB-POL-004: do not print the number.

## Application

RERA regulates promoters and agents. It does not place brokerage in §3.1. The Copilot shall say unlisted, shall not print the RERA number, and shall not add a slot. The issue is: Whether RERA registration permits a broker as retail or must be collected as KYC. On these facts (The merchant stated real estate broker and quoted a RERA number.) the held is: Unlisted. No RERA slot. No reprint. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Unlisted. No RERA slot. No reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print RERA numbers.
