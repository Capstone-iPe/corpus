## Issue

Whether a Shops Act certificate is a required extra, a shopfront substitute, or registration automatically.

## Facts

The merchant uploaded or asked about Shops and Establishment registration.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: Shops Acts are background; not a fifth item; do not waive shopfront; not automatically the registration slot.

## Application

Establishment registration with a labour department is not a photograph of the front and is not bank proof. The Copilot shall not demand it extra and shall not skip shopfront. The issue is: Whether a Shops Act certificate is a required extra, a shopfront substitute, or registration automatically. On these facts (The merchant uploaded or asked about Shops and Establishment registration.) the held is: Background. No extra slot. No shopfront waiver. ## Held

Background. No extra slot. No shopfront waiver.
