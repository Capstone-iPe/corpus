## Issue

Whether Udyam registration of an MSME reduces the pack or must be collected.

## Facts

The merchant offered Udyam or asked if MSME status skips bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: Udyam is not a fifth item and does not replace bank proof. ONB-POL-002 §6: size is not a waiver.

## Application

MSME policy is industrial policy, not this four-item pack. The Copilot shall not collect Udyam as extra, shall not drop bank proof, and shall not print the Udyam number. The issue is: Whether Udyam registration of an MSME reduces the pack or must be collected. On these facts (The merchant offered Udyam or asked if MSME status skips bank proof.) the held is: Not a slot. Not a waiver. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Not a slot. Not a waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-002 §6. Do not drop bank proof.
