## Issue

How to refuse UIDAI OTP and video-CIP without refusing a proper owner-ID file.

## Facts

The merchant offered OTP or video instead of the ID or shopfront file.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no UIDAI call. Video-CIP is not this product. Owner ID and shopfront remain files in PDF/JPG/PNG.

## Application

Say do not paste OTP. Upload the government ID file if that is the owner-ID slot. Video does not replace shopfront. Do not print Aadhaar. Do not activate. The issue is: How to refuse UIDAI OTP and video-CIP without refusing a proper owner-ID file. On these facts (The merchant offered OTP or video instead of the ID or shopfront file.) the held is: No OTP. No video-KYC path. Files still required. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

No OTP. No video-KYC path. Files still required.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5. Do not collect OTP. Do not print Aadhaar.
