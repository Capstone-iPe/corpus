iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

How to answer a live-status question when the verdict is in review.

## Facts

Operations status is in review. The merchant asked if they can take payments.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: the Copilot shall not state that the shop is live. In review means a person has the file or items are outstanding.

## Application

Say they are not live. Name outstanding items if any, or say a person is deciding if the type is unlisted. Do not invent a MID. Do not activate. The issue is: How to answer a live-status question when the verdict is in review. On these facts (Operations status is in review. The merchant asked if they can take payments.) the held is: Not live. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

Not live.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not say live. Do not activate.
