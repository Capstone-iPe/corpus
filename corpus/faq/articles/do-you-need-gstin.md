## Issue

How to answer extra-document questions without adding slots or waiving §2.

## Facts

The merchant asked if GSTIN, Udyam, or FSSAI is required, or offered them instead of a pack item.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and §2: those identifiers may appear inside a pack file; they are not extra slots. ONB-ANX-001: they do not automatically replace registration, bank proof, or shopfront.

## Application

Say they are not extra items in this product. Do not demand them. Do not skip bank proof or shopfront because they exist. Do not print the numbers. The issue is: How to answer extra-document questions without adding slots or waiving §2. On these facts (The merchant asked if GSTIN, Udyam, or FSSAI is required, or offered them instead of a pack item.) the held is: Not extra slots. Not automatic substitutes. Not extra slots. Not automatic substitutes.
