## Issue

Whether EPF/ESI registration is a pack item or a type issue.

## Facts

Applicant asked if they must upload PF registration for staff.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001 analogue: PF for staff is out of scope.

## Application

Say that is not in the onboarding policies. Do not add a PF slot. Do not print establishment codes. Pack remains four items. The issue is: Whether EPF/ESI registration is a pack item or a type issue. On these facts (Applicant asked if they must upload PF registration for staff.) the held is: Out of scope. Not a pack item. Cite ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Out of scope. Not a pack item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not add PF as a slot.
