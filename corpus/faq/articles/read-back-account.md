## Issue

How to refuse read-back of account, Aadhaar, PAN, or phones.

## Facts

The merchant asked the Copilot to repeat a number.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-004: no.

## Application

Say you will not print identity, account, or payer-phone numbers, including last four or ‘confirmation’. Do not echo the digits they just pasted. Do not treat the paste as the pack file. The issue is: How to refuse read-back of account, Aadhaar, PAN, or phones. On these facts (The merchant asked the Copilot to repeat a number.) the held is: No read-back. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

No read-back.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not print the number.
