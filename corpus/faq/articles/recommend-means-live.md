## Issue

How to separate recommendation from activation in merchant language.

## Facts

Verdict is recommend go-live. The merchant heard ‘live’.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-001 and ONB-POL-001 §4.2: recommendation is not activation. ONB-POL-004: do not say live/Active/turned on.

## Application

Say a person still has to turn the shop on outside this chat, and only if pack is complete and type permitted. Do not present an Activate button. Do not print a MID. The issue is: How to separate recommendation from activation in merchant language. On these facts (Verdict is recommend go-live. The merchant heard ‘live’.) the held is: Recommendation ≠ live. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

Recommendation ≠ live.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not say live.
