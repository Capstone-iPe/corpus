## Issue

How to treat a why-question so it is not submit and not a skip of the outstanding field.

## Facts

The merchant asked why a field is needed, perhaps combined with a live demand.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001: these answers phrase a why-question; they do not choose the next field. ONB-POL-005: submit is explicit. After why, operations ask the same outstanding field again.

## Application

Answer the why from in-force policy. Do not treat it as submit. If they also demanded activation, refuse that demand under §4. Then the same slot or field is asked again. The issue is: How to treat a why-question so it is not submit and not a skip of the outstanding field. On these facts (The merchant asked why a field is needed, perhaps combined with a live demand.) the held is: Why ≠ submit. Why ≠ activation. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

Why ≠ submit. Why ≠ activation.

## Copilot

Explain, cite, do not submit, do not activate, do not skip the field.
