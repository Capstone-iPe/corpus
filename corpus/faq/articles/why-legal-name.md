iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

How should the Copilot answer a merchant who asks why iPe collects the shop’s legal name.

## Facts

The merchant asked why legal name is required, before or after offering personal-name bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001 and ONB-POL-001 §2: bank proof has to be in that name. ONB-ANX-001: the legal name is the name collected on the application.

## Application

Tell them the name is the string against which the passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter is read. It is not decoration and not a prompt to edit the name so a personal account passes. After the explanation, onboarding operations will ask any outstanding field again; this article does not choose the next field. Do not print account numbers while explaining.

## Held

Approved explanation: legal name exists so bank proof can match it. Do not rewrite the name. Do not print numbers.

## Copilot

Use this phrasing. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not treat the why as submit. Do not activate.
