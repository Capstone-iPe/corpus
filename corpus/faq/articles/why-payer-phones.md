## Issue

How to explain payer-phone collection and the signed addendum without demanding a DPA when phones will not be stored.

## Facts

The merchant asked why phones are mentioned, or said they agree in the note.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-003 §2: if they will store customer phone numbers, a signed addendum file is required; a sentence in the note is not that file. §2.3: do not request the addendum if they will not store phones.

## Application

Explain the if-store-then-signed-file rule in merchant language. Do not accept GDPR/ISO/URL substitutes. Do not print numbers they paste. Do not activate. The issue is: How to explain payer-phone collection and the signed addendum without demanding a DPA when phones will not be stored. On these facts (The merchant asked why phones are mentioned, or said they agree in the note.) the held is: Signed file if phones will be stored. Note is not the file. No reprint. ## Held

Signed file if phones will be stored. Note is not the file. No reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not treat the note as the DPA. Do not print phones.
