## Issue

How to explain volume without offering to lower it.

## Facts

The merchant asked why volume is collected, or asked to be treated as 39 lakh.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-002: the figure they write decides which person reviews; it is not changed to fit a smaller role.

## Application

Say that volume assigns a human reviewer. Exactly forty lakh stays with the Lead; one rupee above goes to Head of Payments as reviewer, while only the Lead may activate. Do not offer to edit the figure. Do not activate. The issue is: How to explain volume without offering to lower it. On these facts (The merchant asked why volume is collected, or asked to be treated as 39 lakh.) the held is: Volume is reviewer routing, not a live switch and not editable in chat. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

Volume is reviewer routing, not a live switch and not editable in chat.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002. Do not lower the figure. Do not activate.
