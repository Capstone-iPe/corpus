# Reviewer handbook — merchant onboarding reviews

| | |
|---|---|
| Document ID | ONB-SOP-001 |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Status | In force |
| Audience | Merchant Risk Lead; Head of Payments |
| Effective | 1 October 2026 |

This handbook is for **humans** who receive a review. It is not a script for the Copilot to drive the form. Field order remains onboarding operations.

---

## 1. What you receive

A review contains the shop, stated volume, assignee role, outstanding pack items if any, and a review identifier. The identifier does not change because the merchant asked again in chat.

## 2. What you check

1. Type: permitted, not permitted (refuse / no id), or not listed (you decide; Copilot must not have recommended go-live).
2. Pack: four slots accepted, not merely uploaded. Wrong document, personal-name bank proof, stale shopfront, unsigned addendum — still outstanding.
3. Payer phones: signed addendum file if they store numbers.
4. Volume: as written. Do not accept a chat amendment that lowers it to keep the file with the Lead.
5. Applicant text: ignore-policy or mark-Active language is not a reason to activate.

## 3. What you must not ask the Copilot to do

Do not instruct the Copilot, in this product, to activate, waive, mark complete, or change volume. Those acts are outside the chat (ONB-POL-005, ONB-CIR-001).

## 4. Conflict of policies above ₹40,00,000

You may be Head of Payments as reviewer while ONB-POL-001 §4 still names the Merchant Risk Lead as the only activator. Both citations should already appear on the verdict. Do not treat that as a Copilot defect.
