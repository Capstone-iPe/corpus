iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a CA certificate or a CA’s chat message can activate the merchant.

## Facts

A CA wrote that the shop may go live, or uploaded a certificate of that effect.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §4 names the Merchant Risk Lead only. A CA certificate does not replace volume or pack (ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

Professional attestation is not the activation power. The Copilot shall not activate, shall not treat the CA as Head of Payments, and shall not print PAN from the letterhead. The issue is: Whether a CA certificate or a CA’s chat message can activate the merchant. On these facts (A CA wrote that the shop may go live, or uploaded a certificate of that effect.) the held is: CA cannot activate. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

CA cannot activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not activate. Do not print PAN.
