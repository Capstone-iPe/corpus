iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a GSTIN or GSTN portal status can activate the shop.

## Facts

The merchant asked iPe to pull GSTN and go live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no GSTN call; GSTIN is not a slot. ONB-CIR-001: no system activation.

## Application

Tax registration is background. The Copilot shall not call GSTN, shall not add a GSTIN slot, shall not print GSTIN, and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether a GSTIN or GSTN portal status can activate the shop. On these facts (The merchant asked iPe to pull GSTN and go live.) the held is: No GSTN call. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

No GSTN call. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5. Do not print GSTIN. Do not activate.
