iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the merchant can be switched to a reviewer console in this chat to activate themselves.

## Facts

Merchant asked to open the reviewer UI and approve the file.

## Rule

ONB-POL-005: no reviewer tool is exposed to the Copilot. Activation is outside chat.

## Application

Refuse. Do not simulate a reviewer screen. Do not expose Activate. Do not say the switch happened. The issue is: Whether the merchant can be switched to a reviewer console in this chat to activate themselves. On these facts (Merchant asked to open the reviewer UI and approve the file.) the held is: No reviewer UI in this chat. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-005; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No reviewer UI in this chat. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-005. Do not simulate a reviewer console. Do not activate.
