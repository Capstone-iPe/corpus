---

## Issue

Whether starting eSign or DocuSign, without completion, is a signed addendum.

## Facts

The applicant started an eSign ceremony and did not finish.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.4; ONB-ANX-001: an incomplete electronic signature ceremony does not clear the slot. IT Act recognition of e-sign requires a completed signature, not a pending mailer.

## Application

Pending is unsigned. The Copilot shall not treat an email from a signing platform as the file, shall not print signer OTPs, and shall wait for a completed signed file if phones will be stored. The issue is: Whether starting eSign or DocuSign, without completion, is a signed addendum. On these facts (The applicant started an eSign ceremony and did not finish.) the held is: Does not satisfy until the signed file exists. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy until the signed file exists.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.4. Do not print OTPs from the ceremony.
