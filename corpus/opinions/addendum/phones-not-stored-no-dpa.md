iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot should still collect a DPA when the merchant states they will not store payer telephone numbers.

## Facts

The merchant stated they will not store payer phones.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.3: if the shop will not store payer telephone numbers, the Copilot shall not request the addendum.

## Application

Demanding a DPA anyway would add a fifth-like obstacle this policy forbade. The Copilot shall not request the addendum on these facts, shall not print phones, and still shall not activate. Pack items in ONB-POL-001 §2 remain. The issue is: Whether the Copilot should still collect a DPA when the merchant states they will not store payer telephone numbers. On these facts (The merchant stated they will not store payer phones.) the held is: Do not request the addendum if phones will not be stored. Still no Copilot activation. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Do not request the addendum if phones will not be stored. Still no Copilot activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.3. Do not invent a DPA slot. Do not activate.
