## Issue

Whether a website privacy policy or terms URL satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2.

## Facts

The applicant pasted a privacy URL and said they are DPDP/GDPR compliant.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: a privacy-policy URL is not the addendum. GDPR compliance talk is not the Indian signed file this product requires.

## Application

Browsewrap is not the file. The Copilot shall not fetch the URL, shall not treat GDPR as a substitute for DPDP-informed ONB-POL-003, and shall request the signed addendum if phones will be stored. The issue is: Whether a website privacy policy or terms URL satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2. On these facts (The applicant pasted a privacy URL and said they are DPDP/GDPR compliant.) the held is: URL does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

URL does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not fetch the URL.
