---

## Issue

Whether a SOC 2 report can replace the signed addendum.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a SOC 2 report.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: SOC 2 is not the addendum.

## Application

An auditor’s report on a different company’s controls is not this shop’s signed file. The Copilot shall not accept it, shall not print customer names from the report, and shall request the addendum. The issue is: Whether a SOC 2 report can replace the signed addendum. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a SOC 2 report.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not dump the report into chat.
