---

## Issue

Whether an oral WhatsApp note can be the addendum.

## Facts

The applicant sent a voice note saying they agree to the DPA.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: oral statements are not the file. IT Act e-sign, if used, still requires a completed ceremony on the addendum (briefing), not a voice memo.

## Application

Oral agreement theories under the Contract Act do not override this product’s signed-file rule. The Copilot shall not transcribe the voice note into PII and shall request the signed file. The issue is: Whether an oral WhatsApp note can be the addendum. On these facts (The applicant sent a voice note saying they agree to the DPA.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not transcribe secrets.
