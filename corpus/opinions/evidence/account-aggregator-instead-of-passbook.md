iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an Account Aggregator token can replace passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter.

## Facts

The applicant offered AA consent instead of bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 and the Account Aggregator briefing: AA is not a substitute for bank-proof upload in this product.

## Application

AA is a different rail. This pack wants a specific instrument in the legal name. The Copilot shall not start an AA journey, shall not print balances, and shall request passbook, cheque, or bank letter. The issue is: Whether an Account Aggregator token can replace passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. On these facts (The applicant offered AA consent instead of bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not start AA.
