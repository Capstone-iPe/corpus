## Issue

Whether a chartered accountant’s turnover certificate can replace the volume the shop wrote, or can stand as bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a CA certificate and asked to use it as volume and as bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002: do not substitute a CA certificate for the stated figure. ONB-ANX-001: CA certificate is not bank proof. Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 is background.

## Application

A CA certificate is professional work-product. It is not the application’s volume field and not a bank instrument in the legal name. The Copilot shall not lower or raise volume from the certificate, shall not accept it as bank proof, and shall not treat the CA as an activator.

## Held

Does not amend volume. Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not let a CA activate.
