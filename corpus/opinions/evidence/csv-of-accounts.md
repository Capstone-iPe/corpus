---

## Issue

Whether a CSV export of accounts can satisfy bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a CSV of accounts.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a spreadsheet is not bank proof in the legal name.

## Application

A merchant-made table is not a bank letter, passbook, or cancelled cheque. The Copilot shall not print the rows and shall request a proper instrument. The issue is: Whether a CSV export of accounts can satisfy bank proof. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a CSV of accounts.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not dump the CSV into chat.
