## Issue

Whether a photograph of the front that happens to include customers’ faces fails shopfront, and what the Copilot may say about those persons.

## Facts

The file is a photograph of this shop’s front within date. Passers-by are visible.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: still a shopfront file if it is the front; do not describe or print those persons (ONB-POL-004 spirit). Acceptance remains operational.

## Application

The slot tests subject (front) and age and format, not emptiness of the pavement. The Copilot shall not reject in words because faces appear, shall not describe or identify those persons, and shall not print anything from the image. Operations accept or reject the file.

## Held

Faces do not by themselves make a front into a non-front. Do not describe persons. Acceptance is operational.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not describe the persons.
