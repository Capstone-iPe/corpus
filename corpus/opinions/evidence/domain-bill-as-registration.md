---

## Issue

Whether a domain or hosting invoice can satisfy registration because it shows the brand online.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a domain renewal invoice for registration.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a domain invoice is not registration of the shop.

## Application

Anyone may register a domain. It is not a public extract of the business. The Copilot shall not accept it and shall not treat a website privacy policy linked from the domain as a DPA. The issue is: Whether a domain or hosting invoice can satisfy registration because it shows the brand online. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a domain renewal invoice for registration.) the held is: Does not satisfy business registration. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy business registration.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not follow the website as a DPA.
