## Issue

Whether a DISCOM / State Electricity Board invoice can satisfy the bank-proof slot because it identifies the premises and is a government-related bill.

## Facts

The applicant presented an electricity bill for the bank-proof slot. The bill shows a supply address. They cite the Electricity Act, 2003.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2 requires passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter in the shop’s legal name. ONB-ANX-001: electricity, water or gas bills are not capable of satisfying that slot. The Electricity Act evidences supply of energy, not a banking-company account.

## Application

A DISCOM invoice is a demand for energy charges. It is not an instrument issued by a banking company showing the shop’s legal name. Premises identity is not the pack’s bank-proof test. There is no state-wise exception (the rejected utility-as-bank draft is not in force). The Copilot shall explain that the slot remains outstanding, shall not treat the bill as registration either, and shall not print meter numbers. Onboarding operations request the same slot again.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof. Slot outstanding. Shop remains in review if this was the offering.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not accept a territorial exception. Do not print meter or account numbers from the bill.
