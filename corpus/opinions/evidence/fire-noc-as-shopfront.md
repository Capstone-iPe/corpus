## Issue

Whether a fire NOC can replace shopfront as official premises evidence.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a fire NOC for shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a fire NOC is not a shopfront photograph.

## Application

A no-objection certificate is not a dated photograph of the front. The Copilot shall not accept it as shopfront, shall not add it as a fifth item, and shall still request the photograph. The issue is: Whether a fire NOC can replace shopfront as official premises evidence. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a fire NOC for shopfront.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Not a fifth item. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront. Not a fifth item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request the photograph.
