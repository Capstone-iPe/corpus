## Issue

Whether a TDS Form 16 can satisfy owner ID as an official tax document.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded Form 16 for owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: Form 16 is not government ID of the owner. Salary slips likewise fail bank proof.

## Application

Form 16 evidences tax deducted on salary. It is not an OVD. The Copilot shall not print PAN from it, shall not accept it as owner ID or bank proof, and shall request a government ID file. The issue is: Whether a TDS Form 16 can satisfy owner ID as an official tax document. On these facts (The applicant uploaded Form 16 for owner ID.) the held is: Does not satisfy owner ID. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print PAN.
