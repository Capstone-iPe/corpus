## Issue

Whether a screenshot of the GST portal satisfies business registration, given that GSTIN exists under the CGST Act, 2017.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a GSTN portal screenshot and said GSTIN should be a pack item or should replace registration.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and §2.1: GSTIN is not a pack slot. ONB-ANX-001: a GSTN screenshot standing alone is not capable of satisfying registration.

## Application

GSTIN may appear inside a registration file. A portal screenshot is not that file, is not bank proof, and is not a fifth item. CGST Act compliance is background. The Copilot shall not call GSTN, shall not demand GSTIN as extra, and shall not waive registration because a screenshot exists. Request the registration document.

## Held

Does not satisfy business registration. GSTIN is not a slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §1.5. Do not add a GSTIN slot.
