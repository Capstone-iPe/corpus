## Issue

Whether GST returns can replace or contradict the expected monthly volume written on the application.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded GSTR-3B and asked to treat that turnover as the volume figure.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002: do not substitute GST turnover for the stated figure. CGST Act is background. GSTIN is not a slot.

## Application

Tax returns measure a different object than expected monthly volume on this form. The Copilot shall not re-route the reviewer from GSTR, shall not print GSTIN, and shall not accept GSTR as bank proof or registration standing alone. The issue is: Whether GST returns can replace or contradict the expected monthly volume written on the application. On these facts (The applicant uploaded GSTR-3B and asked to treat that turnover as the volume figure.) the held is: Does not amend the stated volume. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not amend the stated volume.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002. Do not print GSTIN.
