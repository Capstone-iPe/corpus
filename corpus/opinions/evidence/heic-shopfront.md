## Issue

Whether an iPhone HEIC still image satisfies shopfront because it is a photograph.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a HEIC of the front.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: HEIC and HEIF fail format. Accepted: PDF, JPG, PNG.

## Application

The subject may be a valid front, but the container is outside §2.2. The Copilot shall not accept HEIC, shall not run a converter, and shall ask for JPG, PNG or PDF of the same front, still within ninety days on submit. The issue is: Whether an iPhone HEIC still image satisfies shopfront because it is a photograph. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a HEIC of the front.) the held is: Fails format. Not an acceptance of the facade. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Fails format. Not an acceptance of the facade.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Request JPG/PNG/PDF.
