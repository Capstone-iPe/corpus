## Issue

Whether an IEC under the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992 must be collected or can waive registration.

## Facts

The applicant asked whether IEC is required, or offered IEC instead of registration.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: exactly four items. ONB-ANX-001: IEC is not a fifth item and not automatically registration.

## Application

IEC is a trade identifier. This product does not collect it as a slot and does not waive registration because it exists. The Copilot shall not demand IEC, shall not call DGFT, and shall not print the code. The issue is: Whether an IEC under the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992 must be collected or can waive registration. On these facts (The applicant asked whether IEC is required, or offered IEC instead of registration.) the held is: Not a slot. Not a waiver of registration. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Not a slot. Not a waiver of registration.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add IEC.
