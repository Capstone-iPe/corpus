---

## Issue

Whether typing IFSC and account number in chat can satisfy bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant typed IFSC and an account number and said that is bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: chat is not the pack file. ONB-POL-004: do not repeat the account number.

## Application

Bank proof is a passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter uploaded in format. A chat string is not that instrument and must not be echoed. Request the file. Do not validate IFSC via an external call. The issue is: Whether typing IFSC and account number in chat can satisfy bank proof. On these facts (The applicant typed IFSC and an account number and said that is bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Do not reprint the number. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof. Do not reprint the number.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not echo the account.
