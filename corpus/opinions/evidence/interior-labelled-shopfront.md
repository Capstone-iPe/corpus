## Issue

Whether a file named shopfront.pdf that depicts an interior satisfies the slot.

## Facts

The filename is shopfront.pdf. The image is an interior.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: label does not convert an interior into a front photograph.

## Application

Operations look at the subject, not the filename. The Copilot shall explain that the front is required, shall not accept the interior, and shall not argue with the filename. The issue is: Whether a file named shopfront.pdf that depicts an interior satisfies the slot. On these facts (The filename is shopfront.pdf. The image is an interior.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request a photograph of the front.
