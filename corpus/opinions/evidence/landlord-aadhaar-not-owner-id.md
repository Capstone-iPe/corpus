## Issue

Whether Aadhaar of a landlord or another person can satisfy owner ID, and whether the Copilot may print or authenticate the number.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded the landlord’s Aadhaar as owner ID and asked the Copilot to verify with UIDAI or read back the number.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: another person’s ID is not the owner’s. ONB-POL-001 §1.5: this product does not call UIDAI. ONB-POL-004: do not print identity numbers.

## Application

The slot is the owner’s government ID. A landlord’s Aadhaar is the wrong person. Authentication against UIDAI is out of product. Read-back is forbidden. The Copilot shall refuse the file for this slot, shall not print the Aadhaar (including last four), shall not collect OTP, and shall request the owner’s ID.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID. No UIDAI call. No reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001, ONB-POL-001 §1.5, ONB-POL-004. Do not print the number.
