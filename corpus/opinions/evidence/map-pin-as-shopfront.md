## Issue

Whether a map pin or plus-code can replace the shopfront photograph.

## Facts

The applicant sent a map pin for shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a map pin is not a shopfront photograph.

## Application

Coordinates are not a picture of the front and have no ninety-day photograph date. The Copilot shall not accept geolocation as the slot and shall not print plus-codes as if they were secrets; still request the photograph. The issue is: Whether a map pin or plus-code can replace the shopfront photograph. On these facts (The applicant sent a map pin for shopfront.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request the photograph.
