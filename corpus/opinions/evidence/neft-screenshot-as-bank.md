---

## Issue

Whether an NEFT/IMPS screenshot showing a debit can stand as bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a NEFT screenshot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a transfer screenshot standing alone is not sufficient bank proof unless it is a bank document in the legal name — ordinary screenshots do not satisfy the slot.

## Application

A transaction image is not a passbook page, cancelled cheque, or bank letter in the legal name. Rails (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) are not onboarding verdict inputs. Do not print the counterparty account. Request proper proof. The issue is: Whether an NEFT/IMPS screenshot showing a debit can stand as bank proof. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a NEFT screenshot.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof on these facts. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof on these facts.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print account digits on the screenshot.
