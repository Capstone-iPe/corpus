## Issue

Whether FEMA account categories (NRE/NRO) allow a personal-name instrument to pass as shop bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a personal NRE passbook and cited FEMA.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: personal NRE/NRO fails the legal-name test. FEMA categories do not relax it.

## Application

NRE/NRO classification answers a FEMA question about a resident’s or non-resident’s account, not this product’s legal-name test. If the shop’s legal name is not on the instrument, it fails. The Copilot shall not run a FEMA seminar, shall not print account numbers, and shall request an instrument in the shop name.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof where the legal name is absent.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print account numbers. Do not invent an NRI waiver.
