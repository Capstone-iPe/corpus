## Issue

Whether a PAN card can satisfy bank proof as an official financial identifier.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded PAN for bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: PAN is not bank proof. It may appear on owner ID but shall not be printed (ONB-POL-004).

## Application

PAN identifies a taxpayer. It does not show a bank account in the shop’s legal name. The Copilot shall not accept it as bank proof, shall not print the PAN, and shall request a bank instrument. The issue is: Whether a PAN card can satisfy bank proof as an official financial identifier. On these facts (The applicant uploaded PAN for bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print PAN.
