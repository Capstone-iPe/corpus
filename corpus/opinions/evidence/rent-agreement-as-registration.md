## Issue

Whether a rent or leave-and-licence agreement under the Transfer of Property Act / state rent control satisfies the business-registration slot.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a lease for business registration, saying they are allowed to occupy the shop.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: rent and leave-and-licence agreements are not capable of satisfying business registration. Occupation is not public registration of the business.

## Application

A lease answers ‘may they sit in this room?’. The pack’s first item asks whether the shop is registered as a business. Collapsing those questions would onboard unregistered traders whenever a landlord signed. Stamp duty on the lease (Indian Stamp Act, 1899) does not cure the miss. Request the registration file. Do not treat the lease as shopfront either.

## Held

Does not satisfy business registration.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not treat stamping as registration.
