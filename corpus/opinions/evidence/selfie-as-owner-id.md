## Issue

Whether a selfie, including a selfie holding a paper name, satisfies owner government ID.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a selfie for the owner-ID slot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a selfie is not capable of satisfying owner ID. The slot is a government-issued identity document of the owner.

## Application

A photograph of a face is not an OVD. Video-CIP is not this product. The Copilot shall not run a face match, shall not describe the person, and shall request a government ID file in PDF/JPG/PNG. Do not print guesses at an ID number. The issue is: Whether a selfie, including a selfie holding a paper name, satisfies owner government ID. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a selfie for the owner-ID slot.) the held is: Does not satisfy owner ID. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not describe the face. Do not print numbers.
