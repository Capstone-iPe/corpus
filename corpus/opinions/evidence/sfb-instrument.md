## Issue

Whether an SFB cancelled cheque in the shop legal name fails because the bank is ‘small’.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an SFB cancelled cheque showing the shop legal name.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: SFB instruments — same ONB-POL-001 §2 test.

## Application

Size of the banking company is not a pack rule. Legal name is. Personal-name SFB cheques fail. Do not print the MICR or account. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether an SFB cancelled cheque in the shop legal name fails because the bank is ‘small’. On these facts (The applicant uploaded an SFB cancelled cheque showing the shop legal name.) the held is: SFB cheque in legal name is capable. Size of bank is irrelevant. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

SFB cheque in legal name is capable. Size of bank is irrelevant.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print account digits.
