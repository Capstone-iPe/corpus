## Issue

Whether a state Shops and Establishment registration can replace the shopfront photograph.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a Shops Act certificate and asked to skip the photograph.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-ANX-003: Shops and Establishment Acts are background. They are not a fifth pack item and do not waive shopfront.

## Application

Labour-department establishment registration is not a photograph of the front and does not start or stop the ninety-day clock. The Copilot shall not accept it as shopfront, shall not add it as a required extra, and shall still request the photograph.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront. Not a fifth item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request the photograph.
