## Issue

Whether PAN of a spouse can be used as owner ID.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a spouse’s PAN as owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: PAN of a spouse is not the owner’s government ID unless that person is the owner.

## Application

Identity is personal. Unless operations determine the spouse is the owner, the file is the wrong person. The Copilot shall not print the PAN and shall request the owner’s ID. The issue is: Whether PAN of a spouse can be used as owner ID. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a spouse’s PAN as owner ID.) the held is: Does not satisfy owner ID on these facts. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID on these facts.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print PAN.
