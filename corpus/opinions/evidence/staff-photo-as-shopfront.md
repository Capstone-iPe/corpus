## Issue

Whether a staff group photograph can satisfy the shopfront-photograph slot.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a staff group photograph labelled shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: staff group is not capable of satisfying shopfront. The slot is a photograph of the front of the premises.

## Application

People standing together are not the facade. Labelled filenames do not convert the subject. The Copilot shall not describe or identify those persons (ONB-POL-004 spirit), shall not accept the file as shopfront, and shall request a front photograph not older than ninety days on submit.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not describe the persons.
