## Issue

Whether a Google Street View or similar historical street image satisfies shopfront.

## Facts

The applicant pasted a Street View capture dated years earlier.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: historical street images are not the merchant’s photograph and, if old, are stale under the ninety-day rule.

## Application

The slot requires the merchant’s photograph of the front, in date. A platform’s imagery is third-party, often stale, and not an upload of their photo in PDF/JPG/PNG as their file. The Copilot shall not accept it and shall not scrape maps. The issue is: Whether a Google Street View or similar historical street image satisfies shopfront. On these facts (The applicant pasted a Street View capture dated years earlier.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not fetch maps.
