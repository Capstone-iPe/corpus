## Issue

Whether an SVG or PNG logo of the brand satisfies shopfront.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a logo for shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: an SVG logo is not a shopfront photograph. Even a PNG logo is a mark, not the front of the premises.

## Application

A logo is branding. The slot is a photograph of the existing front. Trademarks Act registration of the mark, if any, is not this slot. Request the photograph. The issue is: Whether an SVG or PNG logo of the brand satisfies shopfront. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a logo for shopfront.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not treat a mark as the facade.
