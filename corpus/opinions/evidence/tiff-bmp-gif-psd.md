## Issue

Whether common image working files outside JPG/PNG/PDF can satisfy shopfront or ID.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded TIFF/BMP/GIF/PSD.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: TIFF, BMP, GIF, PSD fail format. Accepted: PDF, JPG, PNG.

## Application

Working-file and legacy image types are outside the closed list. The Copilot shall not convert them and shall request JPG, PNG or PDF. The issue is: Whether common image working files outside JPG/PNG/PDF can satisfy shopfront or ID. On these facts (The applicant uploaded TIFF/BMP/GIF/PSD.) the held is: Fails format. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Fails format.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Request an accepted format.
