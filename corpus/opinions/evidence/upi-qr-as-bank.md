---

## Issue

Whether a UPI QR or VPA satisfies bank proof because payments will settle to some account.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a UPI QR for bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: UPI QR or VPA is not capable of satisfying bank proof. NPCI UPI rules are not a verdict input.

## Application

A QR is a payment address. It does not show a banking instrument in the shop’s legal name. VPAs can be personal. The Copilot shall not parse the VPA in chat, shall not print it back, and shall request passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. The issue is: Whether a UPI QR or VPA satisfies bank proof because payments will settle to some account. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a UPI QR for bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not reprint the VPA.
