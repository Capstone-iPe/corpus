## Issue

Whether a visiting card, because it prints the shop name, is business registration.

## Facts

The applicant photographed a visiting card for the registration slot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a visiting card is a representation, not a public registration extract.

## Application

Anyone can print a card. Registration is an extract of a public or statutory record of the business. The Copilot shall not accept the card, shall not treat it as owner ID, and shall not read telephone numbers off it into chat (ONB-POL-004). The issue is: Whether a visiting card, because it prints the shop name, is business registration. On these facts (The applicant photographed a visiting card for the registration slot.) the held is: Does not satisfy business registration. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy business registration.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not reprint numbers from the card.
