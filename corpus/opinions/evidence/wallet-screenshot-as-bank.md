## Issue

Whether a Paytm or other PPI wallet screen satisfies bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a wallet screenshot for bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: PPI/wallet screenshots are not bank proof. RBI PPI directions are background.

## Application

A prepaid-instrument screen is not a bank letter in the shop’s legal name. PPI regulation does not convert the screenshot into §2’s third item. Request proper bank proof. Do not print wallet balances or numbers. The issue is: Whether a Paytm or other PPI wallet screen satisfies bank proof. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a wallet screenshot for bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print balances.
