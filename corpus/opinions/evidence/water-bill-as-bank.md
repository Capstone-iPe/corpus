## Issue

Whether a municipal water-charge record can stand in for a cancelled cheque because a local body issued it.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a water bill for bank proof. They say a government bill is stronger than a passbook.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 lists water bills as not capable of satisfying bank proof. Local-body charges evidence occupancy or supply, not a bank account in the legal name.

## Application

Issuance by a municipality does not convert a utility demand into a banking instrument. The pack’s third item is identity of the shop’s bank relationship in the legal name, not civic regularity. The Copilot shall not analogise to KYC practices that sometimes use utility bills as address proof in other products: this product’s slot is narrower. Request bank proof again. Do not print the consumer number.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not treat civic bills as OVDs for this slot.
