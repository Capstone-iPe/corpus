## Issue

Whether an on-licence bar with FSSAI and food service may be treated as restaurant because food is cooked.

## Facts

Type stated: bar. FSSAI uploaded. Food is served. Applicant asks for the cafe/restaurant alias.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: bar is unlisted; not automatically restaurant; FSSAI is not a type. Cafe alias applies to cafe, not to bar.

## Application

The cafe alias is a vocabulary bridge for coffee shops, not a liquor loophole. FSSAI licences food safety; it does not rewrite §3. If they had applied as restaurant, this note would not recast them as a bar. They applied as bar. Say unlisted, keep in review, do not refuse as gambling, do not activate.

## Held

Unlisted. FSSAI is not a restaurant alias.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not apply the cafe alias to a bar.
