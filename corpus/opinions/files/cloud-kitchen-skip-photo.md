## Issue

Whether absence of dine-in waives shopfront for a cloud kitchen or ghost kitchen.

## Facts

Type stated: cloud kitchen. Applicant asks to skip the photograph because customers never see a front.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: cloud kitchen is unlisted if so named. ONB-ANX-001: shopfront is not waived for cloud/home/online-only.

## Application

Two independent holdings: type is unlisted on these words, and the photograph is not waived even for permitted types. The Copilot shall not skip the photo ‘if you call us restaurant instead’ as a bargain. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether absence of dine-in waives shopfront for a cloud kitchen or ghost kitchen. On these facts (Type stated: cloud kitchen. Applicant asks to skip the photograph because customers never see a front.) the held is: Unlisted as stated. Shopfront not waived. No bargain. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted as stated. Shopfront not waived. No bargain.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not waive shopfront. Do not activate.
