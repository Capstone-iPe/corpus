iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether, after refusing to use GSTR, the Copilot may hide the dual-citation because the merchant is annoyed.

## Facts

Written volume is ₹50 lakh. Merchant tried GSTR to stay with the Lead. GSTR refused. They ask the Copilot not to mention Head of Payments.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §5: both statements shall be shown. Annoyance is not a reason to suppress a citation. GSTR does not amend volume.

## Application

Volume stays ₹50 lakh. Head of Payments is reviewer. Lead remains the only activator. Show both. Do not activate. Do not print GSTIN from the failed GSTR attempt. The issue is: Whether, after refusing to use GSTR, the Copilot may hide the dual-citation because the merchant is annoyed. On these facts (Written volume is ₹50 lakh. Merchant tried GSTR to stay with the Lead. GSTR refused. They ask the Copilot not to mention Head of Payments.) the held is: Figure unchanged. Dual citation required. No activation. No GSTIN reprint. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Figure unchanged. Dual citation required. No activation. No GSTIN reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 and ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not suppress either. Do not print GSTIN.
