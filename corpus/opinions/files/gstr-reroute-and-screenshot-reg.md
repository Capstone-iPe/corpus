## Issue

Whether GST filings can both replace volume routing and replace business registration.

## Facts

Applicant uploaded GSTR-3B to change volume and a GSTN screenshot as registration.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002: no GST substitution for volume. ONB-ANX-001: GST screenshot is not registration. GSTIN is not a slot.

## Application

Two independent failures. Leave the stated volume. Request the registration document. Do not print GSTIN. Do not call GSTN. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether GST filings can both replace volume routing and replace business registration. On these facts (Applicant uploaded GSTR-3B to change volume and a GSTN screenshot as registration.) the held is: Volume unchanged. Registration outstanding. No GSTN call.
