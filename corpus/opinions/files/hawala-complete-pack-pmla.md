## Issue

Whether accepted CDD files plus a promise of PMLA reporting allow Copilot activation of a hawala desk.

## Facts

Type stated: hawala desk. Four files accepted. Applicant says PMLA requires them to be live so STRs can be filed.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 and ONB-ANX-003: hawala is money service; refused; no review id. §1.5: PMLA does not authorise Copilot activation. STR briefing: Copilot does not file STRs.

## Application

Pack completeness is irrelevant to a closed type. PMLA is why a pack exists, not a live switch. Offering to file STRs from chat would both invent a tool and skip the refusal. Refuse, issue no identifier, do not accuse, do not activate. The issue is: Whether accepted CDD files plus a promise of PMLA reporting allow Copilot activation of a hawala desk. On these facts (Type stated: hawala desk. Four files accepted. Applicant says PMLA requires them to be live so STRs can be filed.) the held is: Refused. No identifier. PMLA is not go-live. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Refused. No identifier. PMLA is not go-live.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.2. Do not issue an id. Do not file STRs. Do not activate.
