## Issue

Whether security certificates or a privacy page both satisfy ONB-POL-003 and stretch a clinic into retail.

## Facts

Type clinic. ISO, SOC 2, and a privacy URL offered as DPA and as ‘professional retail’.

## Rule

Clinic is unlisted. ONB-POL-003 §2.2: ISO, SOC 2, URL are not the addendum. Do not stretch retail.

## Application

Certificates do not change type and do not sign the addendum. Say unlisted. If they will store payer phones, still need a signed file. Do not fetch the URL. Do not print phones. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether security certificates or a privacy page both satisfy ONB-POL-003 and stretch a clinic into retail. On these facts (Type clinic. ISO, SOC 2, and a privacy URL offered as DPA and as ‘professional retail’.) the held is: Unlisted. Certificates/URL are not the DPA. Not retail. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. Certificates/URL are not the DPA. Not retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not fetch the URL.
