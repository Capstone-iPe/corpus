## Issue

Whether a kirana, being permitted as grocery, may use a city DISCOM bill as bank proof by territorial exception.

## Facts

Type stated: kirana. Bank-proof slot: Maharashtra / Pune electricity bill. Applicant claims a city practice.

## Rule

Kirana is grocery (ONB-ANX-003). Bank proof is still legal-name passbook/cheque/letter (ONB-POL-001 §2). No city waiver (ONB-POL-002 §6; territorial opinions). Utility-as-bank draft is not in force.

## Application

Permitted type does not loosen evidence. The Copilot may explain that kirana is allowed as grocery, and in the same breath that the DISCOM bill does not satisfy bank proof. Do not print the consumer number. Do not activate even if the other three files are fine.

## Held

Type permitted as grocery. Electricity bill does not satisfy bank proof. No city exception.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not accept the bill. Do not activate.
