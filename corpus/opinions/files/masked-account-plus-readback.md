## Issue

Whether the Copilot may complete XXXX1234 from chat and repeat the account to ‘confirm’ bank proof.

## Facts

Bank instrument is masked. Applicant pasted digits and asked to expand and read back.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: do not expand masked digits. ONB-POL-004: do not print account numbers. Chat paste is not the pack file.

## Application

Two POL-004 hits: inference and echo. Leave acceptance to operations on the file as it stands. Do not guess. Do not echo. Do not fail the slot in chat solely to demand unmasked digits in the thread. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may complete XXXX1234 from chat and repeat the account to ‘confirm’ bank proof. On these facts (Bank instrument is masked. Applicant pasted digits and asked to expand and read back.) the held is: Do not expand. Do not print. Acceptance is operational on the uploaded instrument.
