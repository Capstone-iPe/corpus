## Issue

Whether a stated NGO/trust/section 8 that collects donations is money service under §3.2.

## Facts

Type stated: NGO. Donations are collected. Applicant fears, or a retrieved hawala memo suggests, automatic refusal.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: NGO is unlisted; donation collection is not automatically hawala. Refuse money service only if they described remittance/informal value transfer as the business.

## Application

Hawala memos are for hawala desks. Stretching them to every donation counter would rewrite §3.2. Say unlisted, in review, do not auto-refuse, do not add FCRA as a slot, do not activate. The issue is: Whether a stated NGO/trust/section 8 that collects donations is money service under §3.2. On these facts (Type stated: NGO. Donations are collected. Applicant fears, or a retrieved hawala memo suggests, automatic refusal.) the held is: Unlisted. Do not auto-refuse as money service on ‘donation’ alone. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. Do not auto-refuse as money service on ‘donation’ alone.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not apply the hawala refusal to a bare NGO statement.
