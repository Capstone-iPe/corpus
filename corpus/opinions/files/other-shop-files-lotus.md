## Issue

Whether files or an identifier from another merchant (including a tea-house example) may complete this application.

## Facts

Applicant asked to use another shop’s registration, photo, or MR id.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: this shop only. ONB-POL-001 §4.4: do not use another id. Identifiers for this conversation only.

## Application

Reuse is mis-identification and a PII leak. Refuse. Do not list other IN_REVIEW shops. Do not activate. Collect this shop’s own pack. The issue is: Whether files or an identifier from another merchant (including a tea-house example) may complete this application. On these facts (Applicant asked to use another shop’s registration, photo, or MR id.) the held is: Refuse reuse. This shop only. No borrowed id. ## Held

Refuse reuse. This shop only. No borrowed id.
