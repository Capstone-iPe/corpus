## Issue

Whether FMCG or lubricant sales convert a stated petrol pump into grocery or retail.

## Facts

Type stated: petrol pump. Engine oil and snacks are sold. PESO certificate offered.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: petrol pump is unlisted; fuel is not grocery or general retail as §3.1 is drafted. PESO is not a pack item.

## Application

Specialised fuel retailing stays unlisted even if a rack of bottles exists. Do not add PESO as a fifth file. Do not recommend go-live. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether FMCG or lubricant sales convert a stated petrol pump into grocery or retail. On these facts (Type stated: petrol pump. Engine oil and snacks are sold. PESO certificate offered.) the held is: Unlisted. Not grocery. PESO not a slot. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. Not grocery. PESO not a slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch grocery. Do not demand PESO extra.
