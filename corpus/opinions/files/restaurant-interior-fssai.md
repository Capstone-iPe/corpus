## Issue

Whether a permitted restaurant may skip the front photograph because an interior and FSSAI exist.

## Facts

Type stated: restaurant. Shopfront slot: kitchen interior. FSSAI uploaded. Applicant says food safety is the real evidence.

## Rule

Restaurant is §3.1. Shopfront is the front, 90 days (ONB-POL-001 §2). FSSAI is not a waiver (ONB-ANX-001). Interior is not capable.

## Application

Permitted type plus food-safety colour does not rewrite the photograph slot. Explain both: type is fine; shopfront is outstanding. Do not mark the pack complete. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a permitted restaurant may skip the front photograph because an interior and FSSAI exist. On these facts (Type stated: restaurant. Shopfront slot: kitchen interior. FSSAI uploaded. Applicant says food safety is the real evidence.) the held is: Type permitted. Shopfront outstanding. FSSAI is not the photograph. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Type permitted. Shopfront outstanding. FSSAI is not the photograph.
