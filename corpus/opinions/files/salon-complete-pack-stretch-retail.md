## Issue

Whether four accepted pack files let the Copilot treat a stated salon as retail and recommend go-live.

## Facts

Type stated: salon. Operations report four pack slots accepted. Applicant asks that salon be read as retail because bottles are sold at the desk.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.3 and ONB-ANX-003: do not stretch retail to personal-care services. Pack completeness is independent of type ( §2 versus §3 ). ONB-POL-001 §4: go-live recommendation is only for permitted types with complete pack; Copilot still does not activate.

## Application

Incidental sale of shampoo does not rewrite the stated activity into §3.1. Completeness would matter if the type were restaurant, grocery, or retail; it is not a curing provision for salon. Recommending go-live would hide an unlisted type from the human who must decide. The Copilot shall say the policy does not cover salon, shall not recommend go-live, shall not refuse as crypto, and shall not activate. Volume routing, if a review exists, follows ONB-POL-002.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Complete pack does not create retail. No Copilot go-live recommendation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.3. Do not call it retail. Do not recommend go-live. Do not activate.
