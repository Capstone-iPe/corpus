## Issue

Whether stamp duty on an unsigned addendum lets a complete grocery be recommended go-live when payer phones will be stored.

## Facts

Type grocery. Four pack files accepted. Phones will be stored. DPA on stamp paper, unsigned.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.4; stamp briefing: duty does not replace signature. Recommendation of go-live needs the signed file when phones will be stored.

## Application

Grocery is permitted and the four items may be fine. The addendum is still outstanding. Do not recommend go-live. Do not activate. Do not print phones. Phones will be stored. DPA on stamp paper, unsigned.) the held is: DPA outstanding. Stamp does not cure. Do not recommend go-live while it is required and unsigned.
