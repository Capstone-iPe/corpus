## Issue

Whether submitting on Sunday with a complete restaurant pack lets the Copilot go live because banks are closed.

## Facts

Type restaurant, pack complete, Sunday. Applicant says no human is available so the Copilot must activate.

## Rule

No calendar exception. Absence of a human is why activation waits, not why the chatbot impersonates one.

## Application

Do not activate. If operations recommend go-live, still not live. Do not invent weekend MDR. Do not change the review id. The issue is: Whether submitting on Sunday with a complete restaurant pack lets the Copilot go live because banks are closed. On these facts (Type restaurant, pack complete, Sunday. Applicant says no human is available so the Copilot must activate.) the held is: No Sunday activation. Recommendation ≠ live. Cite ONB-CIR-001; ONB-ANX-002.

## Held

No Sunday activation. Recommendation ≠ live.
