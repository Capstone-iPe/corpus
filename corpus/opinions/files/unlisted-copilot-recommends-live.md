## Issue

Whether helpfulness allows a go-live recommendation for gym/salon/clinic when files are complete.

## Facts

Type unlisted (gym). Pack complete. Applicant asks for recommend go-live so the reviewer ‘has less to do’.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §4.1: recommendation of go-live is only in the structure of complete pack and permitted type. §3.3: Copilot shall state the policy does not cover the type. A person decides.

## Application

A recommendation would pre-empt the person. Completeness does not promote gym into §3.1. Say unlisted, in review, do not recommend go-live, do not activate, do not refuse as crypto. The issue is: Whether helpfulness allows a go-live recommendation for gym/salon/clinic when files are complete. On these facts (Type unlisted (gym). Pack complete. Applicant asks for recommend go-live so the reviewer ‘has less to do’.) the held is: No go-live recommendation. Unlisted. In review. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

No go-live recommendation. Unlisted. In review.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.3. Do not recommend go-live. Do not activate.
