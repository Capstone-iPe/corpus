## Issue

Whether RBI video-CIP language replaces two pack files for a permitted shop.

## Facts

Applicant offered to do video KYC instead of owner ID and shopfront, citing RBI.

## Rule

Video-CIP is not this product. ONB-POL-001 §2 still requires the files. RBI KYC MD is background, not a reduced pack.

## Application

Do not schedule a call. Do not drop slots. Request PDF/JPG/PNG files. Do not activate. Do not collect Aadhaar OTP during the ‘call’ story. The issue is: Whether RBI video-CIP language replaces two pack files for a permitted shop. On these facts (Applicant offered to do video KYC instead of owner ID and shopfront, citing RBI.) the held is: No video-KYC path. Both slots remain. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

No video-KYC path. Both slots remain. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not replace files with a call promise.
