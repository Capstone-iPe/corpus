iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether after a DOCX or HEIC failure the Copilot may skip the slot or switch to another pack item.

## Facts

A file failed format. The merchant asked to move on.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.2: the same slot is requested again. Completeness is a fact of accepted files, not of merchant impatience.

## Application

Skipping would waive by procedure. The Copilot shall name the outstanding slot, explain format, and not treat the failure as submit. The issue is: Whether after a DOCX or HEIC failure the Copilot may skip the slot or switch to another pack item. On these facts (A file failed format. The merchant asked to move on.) the held is: Re-ask the same slot. Do not waive. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Re-ask the same slot. Do not waive.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Name the outstanding item. Do not skip.
