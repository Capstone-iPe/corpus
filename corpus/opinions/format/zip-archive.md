iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a ZIP or similar archive of the four items can be accepted as one object.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a ZIP claiming all pack files are inside.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.2: ZIP is not PDF, JPG or PNG. ONB-ANX-001 lists ZIP among failing types.

## Application

An archive is not a verifiable slot file and would hide format and subject. The Copilot shall not unpack it, shall not accept it, and shall request each slot as PDF/JPG/PNG. The issue is: Whether a ZIP or similar archive of the four items can be accepted as one object. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a ZIP claiming all pack files are inside.) the held is: Fails format. Do not unpack in chat. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Fails format. Do not unpack in chat.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Do not extract archives.
