---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may rewrite the shop’s legal name so that a personal passbook passes.

## Facts

The instrument shows a personal name. The merchant asked to change the shop name in chat to match.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: the legal name is the name collected as the shop name. A mismatch is a failed slot, not a prompt to rewrite the name.

## Application

Name-rewriting would launder a personal account into a shop account. The Copilot shall not propose or perform the change, shall not print the account, and shall request an instrument in the collected legal name. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may rewrite the shop’s legal name so that a personal passbook passes. On these facts (The instrument shows a personal name. The merchant asked to change the shop name in chat to match.) the held is: Do not rewrite the shop name. Slot fails on mismatch. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Do not rewrite the shop name. Slot fails on mismatch.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not change the name. Do not print the account.
