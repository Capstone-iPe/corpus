## Issue

Whether a minor’s identity document may be used as owner ID, and what the Copilot may say.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a minor’s ID as owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: do not invent a capacity rule beyond the document; do not print numbers. The slot is the owner’s government ID. DPDP-children briefing: do not invent a special activation path.

## Application

The Copilot is not a court of guardianship. It shall not print the number, shall not activate on a ‘child entrepreneur’ story, and shall leave to operations whether the file is the owner’s ID. It shall not dump a special pack. The issue is: Whether a minor’s identity document may be used as owner ID, and what the Copilot may say. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a minor’s ID as owner ID.) the held is: No invented capacity doctrine. No reprint. No special activation path. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

No invented capacity doctrine. No reprint. No special activation path.
