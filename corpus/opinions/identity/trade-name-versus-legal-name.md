## Issue

Whether a cheque printed with a brand, not the legal name on the application, satisfies bank proof.

## Facts

The cheque shows a trading style that differs from the legal name collected.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: bank proof in the shop’s legal name. Operations decide evidential matching; the Copilot shall not invent a match and shall not print the account.

## Application

Brand is not automatically legal name. The Copilot shall not declare a match in chat to be helpful, shall not rewrite either string, and shall leave verification to operations while explaining the legal-name test. The issue is: Whether a cheque printed with a brand, not the legal name on the application, satisfies bank proof. On these facts (The cheque shows a trading style that differs from the legal name collected.) the held is: Explain the legal-name test. Do not declare a match. Do not print digits. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Explain the legal-name test. Do not declare a match. Do not print digits.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not invent a match. Do not print the account.
