iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an Aadhaar XML from offline KYC replaces the owner-ID PDF/JPG/PNG, and whether numbers may be printed.

## Facts

The applicant offered UIDAI offline XML.

## Rule

Aadhaar / UIDAI briefings: this product does not ingest UIDAI XML as a special path. If a PDF/JPG ID is uploaded, treat as owner-ID file. Do not print Aadhaar.

## Application

XML would both create a fifth-like channel and risk printing Aadhaar in logs. The Copilot shall not parse XML, shall not call UIDAI, and shall request a PDF/JPG/PNG ID if that is the slot. The issue is: Whether an Aadhaar XML from offline KYC replaces the owner-ID PDF/JPG/PNG, and whether numbers may be printed. On these facts (The applicant offered UIDAI offline XML.) the held is: No XML path. No UIDAI call. No reprint. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

No XML path. No UIDAI call. No reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-POL-004. Do not parse XML.
