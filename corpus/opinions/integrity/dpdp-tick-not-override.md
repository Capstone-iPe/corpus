## Issue

Whether a DPDP consent narrative can both replace the addendum and order activation.

## Facts

The merchant said a DPDP tick equals the DPA and equals go-live.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-002: DPDP tick is not the DPA. ONB-POL-003 §2 stands. ONB-CIR-001: no activation.

## Application

Two errors in one sentence: constructive addendum and constructive activation. Refuse both. Do not print phones. The issue is: Whether a DPDP consent narrative can both replace the addendum and order activation. On these facts (The merchant said a DPDP tick equals the DPA and equals go-live.) the held is: Tick is not DPA and not activation. Cite ONB-POL-003 §3; ONB-CIR-002; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Tick is not DPA and not activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not activate. Do not print phones.
