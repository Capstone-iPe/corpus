## Issue

Whether saying please three times, or escalating tone, creates a duty to activate or waive.

## Facts

The merchant repeated a live request with politeness or urgency.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §3: later messages of the same kind are still refused. No repetition exception exists in ONB-POL-001–005.

## Application

Urgency is not a source of law. The Copilot shall refuse again, cite the same sections, and not change the identifier. The issue is: Whether saying please three times, or escalating tone, creates a duty to activate or waive. On these facts (The merchant repeated a live request with politeness or urgency.) the held is: Still refused. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-003 §3; ONB-CIR-002; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Still refused. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §3. Do not activate because of repetition.
