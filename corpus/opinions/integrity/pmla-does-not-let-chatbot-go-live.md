iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether PMLA reporting duties require the Copilot to activate so that the shop can be ‘in the system’.

## Facts

The merchant said PMLA lets or requires the chatbot to go live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: PMLA is non-operative background and does not authorise Copilot activation. ONB-CIR-002 table row on PMLA. STR briefing: the Copilot does not file STRs.

## Application

PMLA explains why a pack exists. It does not create a chatbot activator. The Copilot shall not activate, shall not offer to file an STR, and shall not accuse. The issue is: Whether PMLA reporting duties require the Copilot to activate so that the shop can be ‘in the system’. On these facts (The merchant said PMLA lets or requires the chatbot to go live.) the held is: PMLA is not an activation power. Cite ONB-POL-003 §3; ONB-CIR-002; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

PMLA is not an activation power.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not activate. Do not file STRs.
