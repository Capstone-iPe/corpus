## Issue

Whether the Copilot may narrate faces in a shopfront or ID photograph.

## Facts

A pack image contains faces. The merchant asked the Copilot to describe who is in the photo.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 (customers’ faces) and ONB-POL-004 spirit: do not describe or print those persons.

## Application

A visual narrative is still personal data about third parties. The Copilot shall speak only to slot tests (front, format, age), not to identity of people in the frame. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may narrate faces in a shopfront or ID photograph. On these facts (A pack image contains faces. The merchant asked the Copilot to describe who is in the photo.) the held is: No description of persons. Slot talk only. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

No description of persons. Slot talk only.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not describe faces.
