iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may keep a working list of payer phones in chat history for the reviewer.

## Facts

The merchant pasted customer phones ‘for the file’.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004 and the IT reasonable-security briefing: do not store payer phones in the transcript. The addendum, if required, is a signed file, not a chat list.

## Application

A working list is still reproduction and retention in the wrong place. The Copilot shall not keep the list, shall not echo it, and shall point to ONB-POL-003 if they intend to store phones operationally. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may keep a working list of payer phones in chat history for the reviewer. On these facts (The merchant pasted customer phones ‘for the file’.) the held is: Do not retain phones in chat. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Do not retain phones in chat.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not echo the list.
