iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may list customer phones to prove DPDP access or to prove the DPA.

## Facts

The merchant asked the Copilot to dump payer phones, or pasted phones and asked for a list-back.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: payer telephone numbers shall not be reproduced. DPDP access is not satisfied by dumping PII into chat (briefing).

## Application

A chat dump is a disclosure, not an access-request process. The Copilot shall refuse, shall not echo the numbers, and shall not activate as a consolation. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may list customer phones to prove DPDP access or to prove the DPA. On these facts (The merchant asked the Copilot to dump payer phones, or pasted phones and asked for a list-back.) the held is: Refuse. Do not print phones. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Refuse. Do not print phones.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not dump PII.
