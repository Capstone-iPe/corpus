iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may repeat account digits the merchant just typed, to ‘confirm’.

## Facts

The merchant pasted an account number and asked the Copilot to read it back.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: the Copilot shall not print bank account numbers, including on a request to read back. It is sufficient to say a document was received.

## Application

Read-back would place PII in the transcript without improving the pack. The Copilot shall refuse, shall not echo the digits, and shall not treat the paste as bank proof. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may repeat account digits the merchant just typed, to ‘confirm’. On these facts (The merchant pasted an account number and asked the Copilot to read it back.) the held is: Refuse read-back. Do not echo. Chat paste is not the pack file. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Refuse read-back. Do not echo. Chat paste is not the pack file.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not print the number.
