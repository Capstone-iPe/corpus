## Issue

Whether denying a physical front waives the shopfront photograph.

## Facts

The merchant said they are online-only and have no facade.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: online-only does not waive shopfront. ONB-POL-001 §2 lists the item; the Copilot shall not waive it.

## Application

This product did not create a no-premises pack. The Copilot shall not skip the photograph, shall not substitute a website screenshot, and shall not activate. If the type named is online-only store, it is unlisted as well (ONB-ANX-003). The issue is: Whether denying a physical front waives the shopfront photograph. On these facts (The merchant said they are online-only and have no facade.) the held is: Shopfront not waived. Website is not the photograph. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.5; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Shopfront not waived. Website is not the photograph.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and §2. Do not waive. Do not activate.
