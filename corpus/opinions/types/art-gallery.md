## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated art gallery is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether forcing gallery into retail, or refusing as NFT/crypto without a token description.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as art gallery. Nature of activity: exhibition / sale of art if applied as gallery not retail. They advanced the trap: forcing gallery into retail, or refusing as NFT/crypto without a token description.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **art gallery**: exhibition / sale of art if applied as gallery not retail. The live retrieval risk is forcing gallery into retail, or refusing as NFT/crypto without a token description. If they applied as retail, §3.1 — they applied as gallery. Do not refuse as crypto unless they described tokens. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Do not auto-refuse as NFT.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
