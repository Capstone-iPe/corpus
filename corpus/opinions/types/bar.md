## Issue

Whether a bar is restaurant because food may be served, or unlisted on-licence liquor service.

## Facts

The merchant stated bar: on-licence liquor. They have FSSAI and say food is served.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: bar is unlisted; state excise is background; not automatically restaurant. Restaurant applies if they applied as restaurant.

## Application

They applied as bar. Excise licensing and FSSAI do not rewrite the stated type into §3.1. Stretching restaurant to every on-licence room would hide liquor service from reviewers. If they had applied as restaurant, this opinion would not recast them as a bar. Here, say the policy does not cover bar, keep them in review, do not refuse as gambling unless they stated wagering, and do not activate.

## Held

Unlisted unless they applied as restaurant. This applicant: in review.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch restaurant. Do not activate.
