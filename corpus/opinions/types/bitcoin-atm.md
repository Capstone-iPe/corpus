## Issue

Whether an unattended VDA cash-in/out machine is crypto under §3.2 or an unlisted kiosk because no human teller is present.

## Facts

The merchant stated bitcoin ATM: unattended cash-to-token or token-to-cash. They argue it is only hardware rental.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps bitcoin ATM and VDA cash-in to crypto under §3.2. Hardware rental and POS rental are not onboarding verdict inputs (ONB-POL-004).

## Application

The product being onboarded is VDA cash conversion, not a restaurant POS. Unattended delivery does not make it unlisted; §3.2 is about the activity, not staffing. Calling it a kiosk or ‘ATM as a service’ does not reach §3.1 retail. Refuse without an identifier. Do not quote scheme MDR or hardware rent.

## Held

Refused as crypto. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not treat hardware rental as the type.
