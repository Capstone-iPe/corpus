## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated chartered accountant firm is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a CA firm as permitted because CAs certify turnover, or letting a CA activate.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as chartered accountant firm. Nature of activity: professional accountancy services. They advanced the trap: treating a CA firm as permitted because CAs certify turnover, or letting a CA activate.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **chartered accountant firm**: professional accountancy services. The live retrieval risk is treating a CA firm as permitted because CAs certify turnover, or letting a CA activate. Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 is background. A CA cannot activate (activation opinions). ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. CA status is not go-live and not a type alias to grocery.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
