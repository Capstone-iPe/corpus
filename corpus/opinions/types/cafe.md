## Issue

Whether a cafe is permitted as restaurant, or must wait in review as an unlisted beverage format, or is a bar because coffee is served with a licence question.

## Facts

The merchant stated cafe: beverages and light food. They did not state a bar, hotel, or on-licence liquor business.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 treats cafe and coffee shop as restaurant under §3.1. A licensed bar is unlisted and is not automatically restaurant. State excise is background and does not decide this product’s type list.

## Application

Cafe is an alias of restaurant in this catalogue so that the Copilot does not send every coffee shop to §3.3. That alias does not licence liquor. If the only stated type is cafe, explain that it is treated as restaurant, that the four pack items still apply, and that the Copilot still cannot activate. If they stated bar, this alias does not apply. FSSAI, if uploaded, is not a fifth item and does not waive shopfront.

## Held

Permitted as restaurant via the cafe alias. Pack required. Not a bar by default.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §3.1. Do not open an unlisted lecture unless they named an unlisted type.
