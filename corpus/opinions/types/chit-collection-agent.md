## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated chit collection is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether auto-refusing as money service or gambling without that description.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as chit collection. Nature of activity: collection agent for chits. They advanced the trap: auto-refusing as money service or gambling without that description.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **chit collection**: collection agent for chits. The live retrieval risk is auto-refusing as money service or gambling without that description. ONB-ANX-003: not §3.1. Prize Chits Act is background. Do not auto-refuse unless they described wagering or remittance. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. Do not auto-refuse on the word chit alone.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
