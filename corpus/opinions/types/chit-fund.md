## Issue

Whether a chit fund is gambling, money service, or unlisted, given the Prize Chits Act as background.

## Facts

The merchant stated chit fund. They did not describe a lottery or a remittance desk.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 and the briefing on the Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978: chit as a stated type is unlisted, not a Copilot refusal unless described as gambling or money service.

## Application

The Copilot shall not run a chit-versus-prize-chit prosecution in chat. It shall not refuse under §3.2 unless they described wagering or funds transfer as the business. It shall not permit as retail of ‘subscriptions’. Unlisted; person decides; no activation.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. In review. Do not auto-refuse.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not accuse. Do not activate.
