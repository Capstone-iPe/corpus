## Issue

Whether an outpatient clinic is unlisted healthcare or retail of consultations.

## Facts

The merchant stated clinic. Clinical Establishments law is mentioned by the applicant as if it onboarded them.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: clinic is unlisted. Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010 is background. §3.3 requires the Copilot to say the policy does not cover the type.

## Application

A clinic is not restaurant, grocery, or retail of goods. Statutory registration of a clinical establishment does not add a pack item and does not authorise Copilot activation. The Copilot shall not demand a clinical-establishment certificate as a fifth file, shall not waive owner ID because a doctor is the owner, and shall not print registration numbers. In review; a person decides.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Sector licence is background only.

## Copilot

Cite §3.3. Do not add a fifth slot. Do not print numbers.
