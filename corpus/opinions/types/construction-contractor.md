## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated construction contractor is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a contractor as retail of cement or as a shop with a site office photo waiver.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as construction contractor. Nature of activity: works contracts. They advanced the trap: treating a contractor as retail of cement or as a shop with a site office photo waiver.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **construction contractor**: works contracts. The live retrieval risk is treating a contractor as retail of cement or as a shop with a site office photo waiver. Works-contract GST colour is background. Shopfront is not waived. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Shopfront not waived. Cement does not make retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
