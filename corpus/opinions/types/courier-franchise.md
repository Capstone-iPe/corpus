## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated courier franchise is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating courier as retail of packaging or as money service because COD cash is handled.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as courier franchise. Nature of activity: parcel delivery. They advanced the trap: treating courier as retail of packaging or as money service because COD cash is handled.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **courier franchise**: parcel delivery. The live retrieval risk is treating courier as retail of packaging or as money service because COD cash is handled. COD handling is not hawala without a remittance-desk description. Courier is unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Do not auto-refuse as money service on COD alone.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
