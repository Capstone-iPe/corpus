## Issue

Whether coworking is retail of desks or unlisted space-as-service.

## Facts

The merchant stated coworking space.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: coworking is space as a service, not retail.

## Application

Hot desks are not groceries. The Copilot shall not treat a membership agreement as bank proof, shall not waive shopfront of the centre’s front, and shall say unlisted. In review. The issue is: Whether coworking is retail of desks or unlisted space-as-service. On these facts (The merchant stated coworking space.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. Membership contract is not bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Membership contract is not bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not treat the licence-to-occupy as pack bank proof.
