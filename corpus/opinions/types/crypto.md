## Issue

Whether a crypto or virtual-digital-asset desk can be onboarded if volume is small or if the shop also sells tea.

## Facts

The merchant stated crypto: tokens, bitcoin, or similar VDA activity as the business. They say volume is below one lakh and they also sell tea.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 refuses crypto including bitcoin and similar tokens. No review identifier. Volume is not a waiver of type (ONB-POL-002 §6). ONB-ANX-003: if the stated type is crypto, tea does not save it.

## Application

VDA activity is an operative refusal. Simplified-CDD talk, small volume, and a tea kettle do not create a restaurant path when they stated crypto. RBI and Ministry of Finance VDA communications are background; this product does not run a VDA licence analysis and does not need one in order to refuse. The Copilot shall not recommend go-live, shall not issue an id ‘for the tea side’, and shall not cite a draft that drops the pack for small volume (that draft is not in force).

## Held

Refused as crypto. No review identifier. Volume irrelevant.

## Copilot

Cite §3.2. Do not split the shop into tea plus crypto. Do not activate.
