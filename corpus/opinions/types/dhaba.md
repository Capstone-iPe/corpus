## Issue

Whether a dhaba is restaurant or an unlisted transport-side activity because it sits on a highway.

## Facts

The merchant stated dhaba: roadside cooked food. Location is a highway. They did not state a trucking or mandi business.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 treats dhaba as restaurant. City and highway location are application fields, not type, and create no waiver (ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-POL-004).

## Application

Dhaba is cooked-food service under the restaurant alias. Sitting on NH-whatever does not make it logistics, and does not waive shopfront photograph of the front of that dhaba. Electricity bills of the highway DISCOM are still not bank proof. The Copilot shall not invent a rural simplified pack. If they applied as truck operator rather than dhaba, §3.3 would apply; that is not this case.

## Held

Permitted as restaurant. Location is not a waiver. Pack required.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not waive shopfront or bank proof for a highway site.
