## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated duty free is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating duty-free as retail automatically, or adding a Customs bond slot.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as duty free. Nature of activity: airport duty-free if so applied. They advanced the trap: treating duty-free as retail automatically, or adding a Customs bond slot.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **duty free**: airport duty-free if so applied. The live retrieval risk is treating duty-free as retail automatically, or adding a Customs bond slot. Customs is background. If they applied as retail, that differs — they applied as duty-free. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. No extra customs slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
