## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated e-waste is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether adding E-Waste Rules authorisation as a fifth slot, or treating as scrap-retail.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as e-waste. Nature of activity: handling of e-waste. They advanced the trap: adding E-Waste Rules authorisation as a fifth slot, or treating as scrap-retail.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **e-waste**: handling of e-waste. The live retrieval risk is adding E-Waste Rules authorisation as a fifth slot, or treating as scrap-retail. E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 are background, not pack slots. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. No extra e-waste slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
