## Issue

Whether esports with prizes is gambling, or unlisted, when the merchant did not describe cash wagering.

## Facts

The merchant stated esports with prizes. They did not describe a betting book.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: if they did not describe cash wagering, unlisted; if they described betting, refuse as gambling. Use the type they wrote.

## Application

The Copilot shall not stretch this into §3.2 to be conservative, and shall not stretch it into a cafe. Unlisted; say the policy does not cover it; a person decides. If a later message says they run a betting book, that is a different classification under §3.2.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Do not auto-refuse. Do not treat as cafe.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Use the type they wrote.
