iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot must collect FCRA registration as extra KYC for an NGO, or may waive the pack because FCRA exists.

## Facts

Type NGO. Applicant offered FCRA registration as pack completeness or as a skip of shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: FCRA is background. ONB-POL-001 §2: four items. No extra slot. No waiver.

## Application

FCRA is a foreign-contribution regime. It does not onboard an NGO onto iPe and does not drop shopfront. Say unlisted. Do not demand FCRA extra. Do not print the registration number. Do not auto-refuse as hawala. The issue is: Whether the Copilot must collect FCRA registration as extra KYC for an NGO, or may waive the pack because FCRA exists. On these facts (Type NGO. Applicant offered FCRA registration as pack completeness or as a skip of shopfront.) the held is: FCRA is not a slot and not a waiver. NGO remains unlisted. Do not auto-refuse. Cite ONB-ANX-003; FCRA briefing.

## Held

FCRA is not a slot and not a waiver. NGO remains unlisted. Do not auto-refuse.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not add FCRA. Do not print the number.
