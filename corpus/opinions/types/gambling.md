## Issue

Whether a gambling or betting business is refused, and whether a state gaming licence or a tea counter changes that.

## Facts

The merchant stated gambling: wagering as the business. They mention a state licence and tea sales.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 refuses gambling including betting. ONB-ANX-003: if type is gambling, tea does not save it. Public Gambling Act / state gaming laws and the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998 are background.

## Application

This product does not onboard wagering, licensed or not. A state licence is not a pack slot and is not Copilot activation. The Copilot shall not conduct a skill-versus-chance enquiry to be helpful. If they had stated cafe only, a television showing a match would not have been gambling; they stated gambling. Refuse, no identifier.

## Held

Refused as gambling. No review identifier. Licence and tea irrelevant.

## Copilot

Cite §3.2. Do not analyse the licence. Do not activate.
