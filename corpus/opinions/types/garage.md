## Issue

Whether a garage is retail of spare parts or unlisted repair.

## Facts

The merchant stated garage: repair of vehicles. Spares are billed.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: garage and body shop are workshop services, not retail under §3.1 unless they applied as retail.

## Application

Repair is the stated activity. Invoicing a filter does not make the business a parts showroom. Motor Vehicles Act provisions on workshops are background. In review; do not refuse as crypto; do not waive shopfront because work happens in a pit. The issue is: Whether a garage is retail of spare parts or unlisted repair. On these facts (The merchant stated garage: repair of vehicles. Spares are billed.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. Spares do not convert the type to retail. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Spares do not convert the type to retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch retail.
