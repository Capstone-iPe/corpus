## Issue

Whether grocery, including a kirana described as grocery, is permitted, and whether a UPI QR at the counter converts it into money service.

## Facts

The merchant stated grocery: retail of food staples and household provisions. A UPI QR is displayed for collecting sale proceeds.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.1 permits grocery. Kirana described as grocery is grocery (ONB-ANX-003). Money service in §3.2 means transmission or conversion of funds as a business, not acceptance of UPI for one’s own grocery sales. PSS Act / NPCI UPI rules are background and are not a verdict input (ONB-POL-004).

## Application

Collecting consideration for groceries through UPI is ordinary merchant acquiring, not a remittance desk. The Copilot shall not refuse a kirana as money service merely because a QR is printed on the counter. Conversely it shall not treat a hawala or forex business as grocery because biscuits are also sold. Pharmacy, liquor off-licence, and petrol are not grocery even if FMCG is on the shelf. GSTIN is not a pack slot. The pack of four items still applies.

## Held

Permitted as grocery where so described. UPI for own sales is not §3.2. Pack still required.

## Copilot

Cite §3.1. Do not refuse for the QR. Do not stretch grocery over pharmacy or liquor.
