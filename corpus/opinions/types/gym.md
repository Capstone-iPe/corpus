## Issue

Whether a gym is retail of memberships or equipment, or unlisted fitness service.

## Facts

The merchant stated gym: fitness services and access to equipment. They argue a membership is a good.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: gym is not grocery, not restaurant, and membership is not retail of goods under §3.1 as drafted. §3.3 applies.

## Application

Access to a floor and a trainer is a service. Calling a membership a ‘good’ to reach retail would also sweep hotels and SaaS into §3.1. The Copilot shall not recommend go-live, shall not refuse as gambling unless they stated wagering, and shall not waive shopfront because the floor is indoors. In review; a person decides.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Membership is not retail for this product.
