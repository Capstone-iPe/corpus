iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a shop described as a hawala desk is unlisted (person decides) or refused as money service, and whether PMLA reporting lets the Copilot activate once files are in.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as a hawala desk. Nature of activity: informal value transfer. They argue that PMLA already requires reporting, so iPe should go live and report later.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 refuses money service without an activation review and without a review identifier. ONB-ANX-003 maps hawala desk, informal value transfer, and hundi-as-transfer-desk to money service. PMLA is background and does not enlarge §3.1 (ONB-POL-001 §1.5).

## Application

Hawala is informal value transfer: value is accepted here and made available elsewhere outside a licensed payment system as the product. That is money service, not a salon-style unlisted shop and not grocery because a counter exists. Completeness of CDD files does not move §3.2 into §3.1. PMLA is why a pack exists at all; it is not a power of the Copilot to activate a remittance desk so that reporting can begin. FEMA and the PSS Act are background in the same sense. No review identifier is issued. The Copilot shall not offer to file an STR, shall not accuse, and shall not recommend go-live.

## Held

Refused as money service. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.2 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not treat hawala as unlisted. Do not activate.
