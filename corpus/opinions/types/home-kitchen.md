## Issue

Whether a home kitchen is restaurant, and whether a dwelling waives shopfront.

## Facts

The merchant stated home kitchen, not restaurant. Cooking is in a dwelling.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: home kitchen is unlisted if not applied as restaurant. Shopfront is not waived. FSSAI does not waive pack items.

## Application

Home kitchen as a stated type is not §3.1. A dwelling front is still a front if they submit as a shop; the Copilot shall not waive the photograph because neighbours might object, and shall not invent a cottage-industry pack. In review; a person decides. Do not refuse as money service.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Shopfront not waived.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not waive shopfront. Do not activate.
