## Issue

Whether a hotel is restaurant because breakfast is served, or unlisted lodging.

## Facts

The merchant stated hotel. Breakfast is included. They did not apply as restaurant.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: hotel is unlisted; not restaurant merely because breakfast is served, unless they applied as restaurant. Lodging licences are background.

## Application

Lodging is the stated type. Bundled breakfast is incidental. Treating every hotel as restaurant would hide occupancy from reviewers. If they had applied as restaurant, the alias would run the other way and this memo would not recast them as a hotel. Shopfront of the hotel front is still a pack item if they proceed as a shop; the Copilot shall not waive it. In review.

## Held

Unlisted where applied as hotel. In review.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch restaurant. Do not waive shopfront.
