## Issue

Whether a kirana is grocery when so described, or unlisted because the word kirana is absent from §3.1’s three nouns.

## Facts

The merchant stated kirana. Nature of activity is neighbourhood provisions. They did not state money transfer, pharmacy, or liquor.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 treats kirana described as grocery / kirana provisions as grocery under §3.1. The Copilot shall not refuse a kirana as unlisted merely because the statute-like list in §3.1 uses the word grocery.

## Application

Kirana is the ordinary Indian neighbourhood grocery. Reading §3.1 as an English-language closed set that excludes kirana would send almost every permitted small shop into §3.3 and defeat the product. The alias applies when they described provisions. It does not apply when they described a money-transfer kiosk that also sells biscuits — that is §3.2 if money service was the business. GSTIN, Udyam, and Shops Act certificates are not extra slots.

## Held

Permitted as grocery when described as kirana/provisions. Pack required.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not send a kirana to unlisted review solely for the word kirana.
