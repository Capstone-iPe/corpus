## Issue

Whether an off-licence liquor shop is grocery of bottled goods or unlisted.

## Facts

The merchant stated liquor shop. They compare bottles to grocery SKUs.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: liquor shop is unlisted; excise is background; not grocery.

## Application

Off-licence liquor is a specialised trade this product did not put in §3.1. Grocery means provisions as used in the catalogue, not every bottled liquid. The Copilot shall not recommend go-live, shall not add an excise-licence slot, and shall not refuse as money service. In review; a person decides.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Not grocery.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not invent an excise slot.
