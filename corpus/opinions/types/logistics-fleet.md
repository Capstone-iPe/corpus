## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated logistics fleet is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a fleet as retail of freight or as a dhaba because trucks park nearby.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as logistics fleet. Nature of activity: transport of goods. They advanced the trap: treating a fleet as retail of freight or as a dhaba because trucks park nearby.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **logistics fleet**: transport of goods. The live retrieval risk is treating a fleet as retail of freight or as a dhaba because trucks park nearby. Carriage by Road Act is background. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Not restaurant. Not retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
