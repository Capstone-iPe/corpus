## Issue

Whether selling lottery tickets as the business is gambling under §3.2 or grocery because tickets sit next to soap.

## Facts

The merchant stated lottery as the business. They also sell soap.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps lottery as the business to gambling, with the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998 as background. If they had stated grocery only, incidental tickets would be a different application.

## Application

They named lottery as the business. Soap does not convert that statement into grocery. State-run versus paper lottery distinctions are background and are not a Copilot power to permit. Refuse under §3.2, issue no identifier, and do not advise on lottery tax.

## Held

Refused as gambling. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §3.2. Do not stretch grocery.
