## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated mandi agent is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a mandi agent as grocery, or adding an APMC licence slot.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as mandi agent. Nature of activity: agricultural market intermediary. They advanced the trap: treating a mandi agent as grocery, or adding an APMC licence slot.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **mandi agent**: agricultural market intermediary. The live retrieval risk is treating a mandi agent as grocery, or adding an APMC licence slot. APMC / state mandi law is background, not a pack item. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. No extra mandi slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
