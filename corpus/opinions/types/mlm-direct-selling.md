## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated MLM / direct selling is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether auto-refusing as prize-chit/gambling, or treating as grocery.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as MLM / direct selling. Nature of activity: network selling. They advanced the trap: auto-refusing as prize-chit/gambling, or treating as grocery.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **MLM / direct selling**: network selling. The live retrieval risk is auto-refusing as prize-chit/gambling, or treating as grocery. Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules are background. Do not auto-refuse unless they described wagering or money service. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Do not auto-refuse. Do not stretch grocery.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
