## Issue

Whether trade in tokenised assets is crypto under §3.2 or retail of digital art under §3.1.

## Facts

The merchant stated NFT shop: sale of tokenised assets. They say it is retail of art.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps NFT shop to crypto. §3.1 retail means a shop selling goods in the ordinary sense of this product, not token rails.

## Application

An NFT shop’s inventory is a token. Reading it as retail of art would move VDA activity into §3.1 by metaphor. Copyright in an image, if any, is background (Copyright Act, 1957) and does not convert a token desk into a permitted shop. Refuse as crypto, issue no id, and do not open a how-to-mint discussion.

## Held

Refused as crypto. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch retail to tokens.
