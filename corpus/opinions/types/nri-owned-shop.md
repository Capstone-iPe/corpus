---

## Issue

Whether NRI or OCI ownership is a business type that can be refused, or is irrelevant to §3.

## Facts

The merchant said the owner is NRI. The stated activity is grocery.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: ownership residency is not a type in §3. Do not invent a refusal. Type follows what they stated. FEMA account categories do not relax legal-name bank proof (ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

NRI status is not salon, not crypto, not grocery. Classify grocery as permitted. Do not refuse under FEMA. Do not print passport numbers. Bank proof must still show the shop’s legal name, including if an NRE account is offered — personal-name NRE fails the legal-name test. The Copilot shall not open a FEMA compounding seminar.

## Held

Ownership is not a type. Classify the stated activity. PII still not printed.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-004. Do not invent an NRI refusal.
