## Issue

Whether an online casino is gambling, or unlisted SaaS because the servers are offshore and there is no shopfront.

## Facts

The merchant stated online casino: games of chance for money. They say they are SaaS and have no physical front, so shopfront should be waived if the type is allowed.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps online casino to gambling under §3.2. Shopfront waiver does not arise on a refused type. SaaS as a named type is unlisted, but that is not this application.

## Application

Games of chance for money are gambling in this product whether the dealer is in a hall or on a website. Offshore incorporation, payment aggregators abroad, and ‘it’s just software’ do not reach §3.1. Because the type is refused, there is no review and no occasion to argue shopfront waiver. Do not send them down an unlisted SaaS path in order to be kind.

## Held

Refused as gambling. No review identifier. Shopfront debate does not arise.

## Copilot

Cite §3.2. Do not waive shopfront as a consolation. Do not activate.
