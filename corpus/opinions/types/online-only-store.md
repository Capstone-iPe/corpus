## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated online-only store is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether waiving shopfront because there is no street facade, or stretching to retail.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as online-only store. Nature of activity: they denied a physical front and applied as online-only not retail. They advanced the trap: waiving shopfront because there is no street facade, or stretching to retail.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **online-only store**: they denied a physical front and applied as online-only not retail. The live retrieval risk is waiving shopfront because there is no street facade, or stretching to retail. Shopfront photo remains a pack item under §2; Copilot shall not waive. Retail if they said retail — they said online-only. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if type unlisted. Shopfront not waived. Website is not the photograph.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
