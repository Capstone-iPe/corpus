## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated optical clinic is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether confusing optical clinic with optical retail.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as optical clinic. Nature of activity: optometry as clinic. They advanced the trap: confusing optical clinic with optical retail.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **optical clinic**: optometry as clinic. The live retrieval risk is confusing optical clinic with optical retail. Optical retail if they applied as retail — they applied as clinic. Unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Do not stretch retail of frames.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
