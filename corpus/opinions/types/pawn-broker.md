## Issue

Whether pawn is retail of unredeemed goods, money service, or unlisted lending against pledge.

## Facts

The merchant stated pawn broker. State pawnbroker statutes are cited by them as a licence to go live.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: pawn is unlisted; state pawnbroker statutes are background; not retail. It is not money service unless they described remittance.

## Application

Lending against pledged goods is not kirana retail and not, without more, hawala. The Copilot shall not refuse as §3.2 on these facts, shall not stretch retail, shall not treat the pawn licence as a pack slot or as activation, and shall say unlisted. A person decides.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Not retail; not automatically money service.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not refuse as hawala without a remittance description.
