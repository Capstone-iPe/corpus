## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated pesticide shop as pesticide is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether automatically treating pesticide trade as grocery.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as pesticide shop as pesticide. Nature of activity: Insecticides Act trade if that is the type named. They advanced the trap: automatically treating pesticide trade as grocery.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **pesticide shop as pesticide**: Insecticides Act trade if that is the type named. The live retrieval risk is automatically treating pesticide trade as grocery. Insecticides Act, 1968 is background. Not grocery automatically. In review if that is the type named. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if that is the type named. Not grocery. No extra licence slot demanded by Copilot.
