---

## Issue

Whether issuing stored value is money service / outside §3.1, or retail of a ‘digital product’.

## Facts

The merchant stated prepaid wallet issuer: they issue stored value to the public.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps prepaid wallet issuer to money service. RBI PPI directions are background. §3.1 does not list issuance of stored value.

## Application

Issuing a wallet is a payments-business activity, not a kirana. PPI authorisation, if claimed, is not a pack slot and is not Copilot activation. Refuse as money service. Do not analogise wallets to gift hampers in order to reach retail. The issue is: Whether issuing stored value is money service / outside §3.1, or retail of a ‘digital product’. On these facts (The merchant stated prepaid wallet issuer: they issue stored value to the public.) the held is: Refused as money service. No review identifier. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Refused as money service. No review identifier.
