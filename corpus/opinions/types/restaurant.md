## Issue

Whether a shop that states its business as a restaurant is a permitted type under ONB-POL-001 §3.1, and whether a complete pack lets the Copilot activate it.

## Facts

The merchant described the activity as a restaurant: preparation and sale of food for consumption on or off the premises. No money-transmission, VDA, or wagering activity was stated.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.1 permits restaurant. Cafe and dhaba are treated as restaurant (ONB-ANX-003). Activation remains ONB-POL-001 §4: only the Merchant Risk Lead, outside chat, and only if the pack is complete. FSSAI licensing under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 is background and is not a fifth pack item.

## Application

A restaurant is the paradigm permitted shop. The Copilot may explain that this type is within §3.1 and that go-live still requires four accepted pack files plus a human activator. It shall not treat FSSAI as a substitute for business registration, shall not waive shopfront because food is prepared inside, and shall not mark the shop live in chat. If the merchant later restates the type as a bar, hotel, or home kitchen, that later statement is a different classification; this opinion applies only where restaurant was the type written.

## Held

Permitted as restaurant. Pack still required. Copilot does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.1 and §4. Do not invent an FSSAI slot. Do not say the shop is live.
