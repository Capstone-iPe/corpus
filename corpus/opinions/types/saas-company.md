---

## Issue

Whether a SaaS company is unlisted, and whether absence of a shopfront of a ‘shop’ waives the photograph.

## Facts

The merchant stated SaaS company. They say there is no physical shop.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: SaaS is unlisted. ONB-POL-001 §2 still lists shopfront photograph; the Copilot shall not waive it (ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

SaaS is not restaurant, grocery, or retail of goods. Unlisted, in review. Independently, this product did not create an online-only pack. The Copilot shall not skip shopfront, shall not recommend go-live, and shall not analogise to ISO 27001 as a substitute for pack files.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Shopfront not waived.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §2. Do not waive shopfront.
