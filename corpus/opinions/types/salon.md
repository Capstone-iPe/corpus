## Issue

Whether a salon is retail because products are sold at the desk, or unlisted personal service under §3.3.

## Facts

The merchant stated salon: grooming services on the person. They sell shampoo at the desk and have a complete pack.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.3 sends types in neither §3.1 nor §3.2 to human review. ONB-ANX-003: do not stretch retail to services on the person. Pack completeness does not alter type.

## Application

A salon’s primary activity is service on the person. Incidental retail of bottles does not rewrite the stated type into §3.1. Completeness of files would matter for activation if the type were permitted; it is not a curing provision for §3.3. The Copilot shall say the policy does not cover salon, shall not recommend go-live, shall not refuse as crypto, and shall not activate. A person decides. Volume routing, if a review exists, follows ONB-POL-002.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Do not stretch to retail. Do not refuse as crypto.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not activate.
