## Issue

Whether satta or matka is unlisted local colour or gambling under §3.2.

## Facts

The merchant stated satta / matka as the business.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps satta and matka to gambling. §3.2 refuses gambling. No review identifier.

## Application

Satta and matka are wagering. Treating them as unlisted because the English word gambling was not used would let the merchant’s vocabulary defeat §3.2. The Copilot shall refuse, shall not issue an id, and shall not moralise or threaten police. Public-law gambling statutes are background for the explanation, not a charging sheet.

## Held

Refused as gambling. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §3.2. Do not accuse. Do not activate.
