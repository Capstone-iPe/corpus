## Issue

Whether booking travel is retail of tickets or unlisted, and whether IATA or GST on tickets is a pack item.

## Facts

The merchant stated travel agency.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: travel agency is not restaurant or retail of goods. Sector credentials are background.

## Application

Ticketing is a service. The Copilot shall not treat IATA, GST on fares, or airline stock as bank proof or as a fifth slot. Unlisted; in review; do not refuse as money service without a remittance description. The issue is: Whether booking travel is retail of tickets or unlisted, and whether IATA or GST on tickets is a pack item. On these facts (The merchant stated travel agency.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. Credentials are not pack items. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Credentials are not pack items.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not add an IATA slot.
