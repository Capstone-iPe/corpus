iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated vendor KYC firm is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether letting them skip their own pack because they ‘do KYC’, or pulling CKYCR.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as vendor KYC firm. Nature of activity: KYC as a service, not iPe’s pack. They advanced the trap: letting them skip their own pack because they ‘do KYC’, or pulling CKYCR.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **vendor KYC firm**: KYC as a service, not iPe’s pack. The live retrieval risk is letting them skip their own pack because they ‘do KYC’, or pulling CKYCR. Another firm’s KYC product is not iPe’s four items. This product does not pull CKYCR. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. Their KYC business does not waive their pack. No CKYCR pull.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
