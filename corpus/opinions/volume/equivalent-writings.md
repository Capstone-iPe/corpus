## Issue

Whether ‘12 lakh’, ‘₹12,00,000’, and ‘INR 1200000’ may be routed differently because the strings differ.

## Facts

The merchant wrote one of the equivalent forms in ONB-POL-002 §4.1.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §4.1: amounts that denote the same rupee total must route the same way. ONB-ANX-002 interprets lakh and crore.

## Application

String identity is not the test; rupee identity is. Operations parse. The Copilot explains that twelve lakh is twelve lakh however written, and does not invent a different reviewer because of punctuation. It does not activate. The issue is: Whether ‘12 lakh’, ‘₹12,00,000’, and ‘INR 1200000’ may be routed differently because the strings differ. On these facts (The merchant wrote one of the equivalent forms in ONB-POL-002 §4.1.) the held is: Same rupees, same routing. Copilot does not parse as a decision engine; it explains. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Same rupees, same routing. Copilot does not parse as a decision engine; it explains.
