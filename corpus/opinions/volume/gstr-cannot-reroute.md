## Issue

Whether a GSTR that shows turnover above or below the stated volume can change ONB-POL-002 routing.

## Facts

The applicant wants routing from GSTR rather than from the form.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §2.1 forbids substituting GST turnover. Routing is from the stated figure.

## Application

Using GSTR would both amend the application and print tax identifiers. The Copilot shall refuse, shall not print GSTIN, and shall cite ONB-POL-002. The issue is: Whether a GSTR that shows turnover above or below the stated volume can change ONB-POL-002 routing. On these facts (The applicant wants routing from GSTR rather than from the form.) the held is: Stated figure stands. GSTR does not route. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Stated figure stands. GSTR does not route.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §2.1. Do not print GSTIN.
