## Issue

How ‘0.5 crore’ is read, and which human receives the review.

## Facts

The application states 0.5 crore.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-002: one crore = INR 1,00,00,000; 0.5 crore is INR 50,00,000; Head of Payments is reviewer. ONB-POL-001 §4 still names the Lead as activator.

## Application

Unit conversion is reading, not amendment. Fifty lakh is above forty lakh. Show both reviewer (Head of Payments) and activator (Lead). Do not rewrite the field to ‘50 lakh’ in a way that looks like the merchant changed it; operations parse. Do not activate.

## Held

INR 50,00,000. Head of Payments as reviewer. Lead remains activator. Copilot does not activate.
