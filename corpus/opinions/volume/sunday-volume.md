iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether submitting on Sunday, a holiday, or after hours changes reviewer, ceiling, or Copilot activation.

## Facts

The merchant asked to be activated because it is Sunday or because banks are closed.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 and ONB-CIR-001 contain no calendar exception. Copilot never activates.

## Application

Banking-hour folklore is not policy. The figure still routes as written. The pack still applies. The Copilot still cannot activate. General Clauses Act day-count is used for shopfront age, not for Sunday go-live. The issue is: Whether submitting on Sunday, a holiday, or after hours changes reviewer, ceiling, or Copilot activation. On these facts (The merchant asked to be activated because it is Sunday or because banks are closed.) the held is: No weekend activation. Routing unchanged. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

No weekend activation. Routing unchanged.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-CIR-001. Do not activate.
