## Issue

What the Copilot should say if the volume field is ambiguous between two reasonable rupee totals.

## Facts

The written volume admits two reasonable readings. The merchant asks the Copilot to pick the one that keeps the Lead.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §4.2: where two reasonable readings exist, the application is not rewritten; the conflict is shown to the reviewer.

## Application

Picking the kinder reading would be lowering-by-interpretation. The Copilot shall not choose, shall not activate, and shall not pretend the figure is clear. Operations show the conflict to the reviewer. The issue is: What the Copilot should say if the volume field is ambiguous between two reasonable rupee totals. On these facts (The written volume admits two reasonable readings. The merchant asks the Copilot to pick the one that keeps the Lead.) the held is: Do not rewrite. Show the conflict. Copilot does not activate. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Do not rewrite. Show the conflict. Copilot does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §4.2. Do not pick a reading to keep the Lead.
