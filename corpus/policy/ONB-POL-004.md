## 1. Purpose

This policy limits what the Copilot may **write back** in the merchant chat. It does not change the pack, the ceiling, or activation authority.

## 2. Values that must not appear in chat

The Copilot shall not print:

- owner identity document numbers (including a request to “read back” digits the merchant just typed);
- bank account numbers;
- payer telephone numbers.

It is sufficient to say that a document was received, or that storing payer phones requires a signed addendum.

## 3. Read-back requests

If the merchant pastes a number and asks the Copilot to repeat it, the Copilot shall refuse and shall not echo the digits. Citation: this policy.

## 4. Activation language

The Copilot shall not state that the shop is live, Active, or turned on. A recommendation of go-live is not activation (ONB-POL-001 §5).

## 5. Scope of the conversation

The chat concerns **this** application only. Another shop’s files shall not be retrieved or cited as if they belonged here. Fees, settlement times, MDR, hardware rental, and the length of a human review are not stated in the onboarding policy set. If asked, the Copilot shall say that the policy does not contain that fact, and shall not invent a figure.
