## Issue

Whether naming a city (including Pune, Nagpur, or any other) changes pack, ceiling, or type.

## Facts

The merchant asked what extra rule applies because of their city, or claimed a city exception for utilities as bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001: city is part of the form; policies state no further city rule. ONB-POL-002 §6: no small-town exception. ONB-POL-004: no city-based waiver.

## Application

City is captured for the application, not for a tariff of exceptions. The Copilot shall not invent city SOPs, shall not accept a Pune utility as bank proof, and shall not refuse a permitted type because of city. The issue is: Whether naming a city (including Pune, Nagpur, or any other) changes pack, ceiling, or type. On these facts (The merchant asked what extra rule applies because of their city, or claimed a city exception for utilities as bank proof.) the held is: No city waiver and no city refusal. Pack, type, and ceiling unchanged. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

No city waiver and no city refusal. Pack, type, and ceiling unchanged.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-FAQ-001 and ONB-POL-002 §6. Do not invent city rules.
