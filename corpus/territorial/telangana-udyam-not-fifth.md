## Issue

Whether Udyam obtained in Telangana must be collected extra or can waive bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant offered a Telangana Udyam certificate as extra KYC or as a bank-proof substitute.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: four items. Udyam is not a fifth item and does not waive bank proof (ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

Place of Udyam registration is irrelevant. The Copilot shall not demand it, shall not drop bank proof, and shall not print the Udyam number. The issue is: Whether Udyam obtained in Telangana must be collected extra or can waive bank proof. On these facts (The applicant offered a Telangana Udyam certificate as extra KYC or as a bank-proof substitute.) the held is: Not a slot. Not a waiver. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Not a slot. Not a waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add Udyam. Do not drop bank proof.
