## Issue

Whether a West Bengal / Kolkata municipal trade licence satisfies bank proof or registration automatically.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a municipal trade licence for bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: local-body documents of this kind are not passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. They are not automatically business registration either.

## Application

A trade licence is civic permission, not a banking instrument in the legal name. The Copilot shall not accept it as bank proof, shall not print licence numbers, and shall not create a Kolkata schedule. The issue is: Whether a West Bengal / Kolkata municipal trade licence satisfies bank proof or registration automatically. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a municipal trade licence for bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. No municipal exception. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof. No municipal exception.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print the licence number.
