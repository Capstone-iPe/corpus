# Reading Expected Monthly Volume

This annex is for consistent **reading** of the figure the shop wrote. It does not authorise changing that figure. GST turnover, ITR, a CA certificate, bank OD limits, and seasonal estimates do not replace the stated figure.

---

## 1. Units

- One **lakh** = INR 1,00,000. “12 lakh”, “12 lakhs”, and “₹12,00,000” are INR 12,00,000.
- One **crore** = INR 1,00,00,000. “0.5 crore” is INR 50,00,000.
- “Forty lakh one” and “₹40,00,001” are above the ceiling.

## 2. Ceiling examples (illustrative)

| Written | INR | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| ₹1,00,000 / 1 lakh | 1,00,000 | Merchant Risk Lead |
| ₹39,00,000 / 39 lakh | 39,00,000 | Merchant Risk Lead |
| ₹40,00,000 / forty lakh | 40,00,000 | Merchant Risk Lead (**inclusive**) |
| ₹40,00,001 | 40,00,001 | Head of Payments |
| 40.5 lakh | 40,50,000 | Head of Payments |
| ₹45,00,000 | 45,00,000 | Head of Payments |
| 0.5 crore | 50,00,000 | Head of Payments |

Small volume is not auto-live. There is no “below 1 lakh activate” rule. Pack and type still apply. The Copilot still does not activate.

## 3. What this annex is not

This annex is not a table the Copilot searches in order to **decide** routing. Routing is computed from the parsed figure by onboarding operations. The annex exists so that a “why is this going to Head of Payments?” answer can cite ONB-POL-002 with a worked reading.

Above the ceiling the Copilot shall show **both** ONB-POL-002 (Head of Payments as reviewer) and ONB-POL-001 §4 (only the Merchant Risk Lead may activate). It shall not choose a winner in silence.

## 4. Refusal to amend

“Take us as 39 lakh” after a higher figure was written is refused under ONB-POL-002. Cite that policy, not this table as if it were a waiver schedule. Tea houses, shops named Test, demo mode, and Sunday do not alter the figure or the ceiling.
