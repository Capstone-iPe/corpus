This catalogue helps the Copilot **explain** a type question after onboarding operations have classified the type the merchant wrote. It does not add permitted types, does not create a fifth pack item, and does not authorise the Copilot to activate.

The governing rule remains ONB-POL-001 §3. Where this catalogue and §3 conflict, §3 prevails.

---

## 1. Permitted (ONB-POL-001 §3.1)

These types may be recommended for go-live only if the pack is complete. The Copilot still does not activate.

| Merchant says | Treat as | Do not read as |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant | restaurant | bar, hotel, cloud kitchen, or home kitchen merely because food is involved |
| Cafe, coffee shop | restaurant | a licensed bar |
| Dhaba | restaurant | a transport or mandi business |
| Grocery | grocery | pharmacy, liquor off-licence, or petrol |
| Kirana, general provisions described as grocery | grocery | money service merely because a UPI QR is displayed |
| Retail, general store described as retail | retail | salon, gym, garage, clinic, or workshop services |

If the merchant clearly applied as restaurant, grocery, or retail, use that type. Incidental side activity does not rewrite the stated type into a refused type, and does not rewrite an unlisted type into §3.1.

---

## 2. Not permitted (ONB-POL-001 §3.2) — refused, no review identifier

Money service, crypto, and gambling are refused. No activation review is opened. No review identifier is issued. Completeness of the pack does not cure a §3.2 type. PMLA, FEMA, the PSS Act, VDA guidance, and gaming statutes are **background**; they do not create a permitted type and do not let the Copilot file an STR or turn the shop on.

| Merchant says | Treat as | Background only |
|---|---|---|
| Money service, remittance agent, money transfer kiosk | money service | FEMA; PSS Act |
| Hawala desk, informal value transfer, hundi as a transfer desk | money service | PMLA |
| Forex counter, currency exchange as the business | money service | FEMA |
| Prepaid wallet issuer, unlicensed funds aggregation described as the business | money service | RBI PA / PPI |
| Crypto, bitcoin, token desk, NFT shop, bitcoin ATM, VDA cash-in | crypto | RBI / MoF VDA communications |
| Gambling, betting, satta, matka, online casino, lottery as the business, fantasy sports for cash | gambling | Public Gambling Act / state gaming; Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998 |

If the merchant stated cafe only, that is restaurant even if a television in the cafe shows a cricket match. If they stated betting shop, that is gambling even if tea is also sold.

---

## 3. Not listed (ONB-POL-001 §3.3) — in review, a person decides

A type in neither §3.1 nor §3.2 remains in review. The Copilot shall state that the policy does not cover that type. Stretching “retail” or “grocery” or “restaurant” to mop up an unlisted type would rewrite §3.1. Refusing an unlisted type as crypto, unless the merchant’s type is actually crypto, would rewrite §3.2.

Examples (not exhaustive): salon, spa, gym, clinic, dental clinic, veterinary clinic, pharmacy, diagnostic lab, hospital, garage, body shop, hotel, hostel, paying guest, bar, liquor shop, pawn broker, tuition centre, coaching class, driving school, school, warehouse, cold storage, logistics, courier, travel agency, real estate broker, construction, manufacturing, SaaS, coworking, cinema, farm, dairy, petrol pump, EV charging, scrap, security agency, laundry, tailor, photo studio, jewellery workshop, mobile repair, CA / law / architect firm, NGO, trust counter, cloud kitchen or food truck *if that is the type named*, dark store *if so named*, NBFC, chit fund, insurance agency, share or MF distribution, SEZ unit *if so named*.

**Do not stretch**

- Salon, gym, spa, tailor, laundry → not retail of goods under §3.1.
- Pharmacy, clinic, hospital → not grocery. Drugs and Cosmetics Act / Clinical Establishments Act are background.
- Bar, liquor shop → not restaurant or grocery. State excise is background.
- Petrol pump → not grocery or general retail as §3.1 is drafted.
- Garage, workshop, service-only two-wheeler → not retail of vehicles unless they applied as retail.
- Hotel → not restaurant merely because breakfast is served, unless they applied as restaurant.
- Cloud kitchen, food truck, home kitchen, tiffin, mess → restaurant **only** if they applied as restaurant. Shopfront is not waived.
- Pawn, NBFC gold loan, chit, insurance, MF distribution → not retail and not automatically money service unless they described remittance, VDA, or wagering.
- FSSAI, Shops Act, Udyam, GSTIN, PESO, RERA, IRDAI, SEBI registrations → not a type, not a fifth pack item, not a waiver.

---

## 4. Mixed descriptions

1. If they clearly stated a permitted type, use that type.
2. If they clearly stated a not-permitted type, refuse. No review identifier.
3. If they only named a not-listed type, §3.3 applies.
4. If they named two types in one sentence and one is §3.2, operations will refuse where the stated business is the refused activity. The Copilot shall not pick the kinder type to be helpful.
5. Ownership (NRI, HUF, section 8 company) is not a type. Do not invent a refusal from nationality or constitution. Classify the activity they stated.

Volume routing for a review, when a review exists, follows ONB-POL-002. The Copilot does not choose the reviewer.
