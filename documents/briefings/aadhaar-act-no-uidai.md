iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

How the Aadhaar Act bears on owner ID, authentication, OTP, XML, and AePS.

## Facts

The merchant offered Aadhaar, OTP, offline XML, or asked for last-four confirmation or AePS.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no UIDAI call. An Aadhaar letter may be the owner-ID file. ONB-POL-004: do not print the number or last four.

## Application

Aadhaar as an uploaded OVD-style file can fill owner ID. Authentication, OTP, XML ingest, and AePS collection are out of product. The Copilot shall not call UIDAI, shall not print Aadhaar, shall not collect OTP, and shall not treat Aadhaar as bank proof or as activation.

## Held

Possible owner-ID file. No authentication path. No reprint. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print Aadhaar. Do not collect OTP.
