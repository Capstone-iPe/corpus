---

## Issue

Whether AA consent can replace passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter.

## Facts

The merchant offered AA or OCEN-style rails instead of bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: AA is not a substitute in this product. OCEN/TReDS/NeSL briefings: out of scope of pack and verdict.

## Application

Lending and consent-artefact rails are different products. The Copilot shall not start AA, shall not print balances, and shall request the §2 bank instrument. The issue is: Whether AA consent can replace passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. On these facts (The merchant offered AA or OCEN-style rails instead of bank proof.) the held is: No AA path. Bank-proof slot unchanged. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No AA path. Bank-proof slot unchanged.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not start AA. Do not print balances.
