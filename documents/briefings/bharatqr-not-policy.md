iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether asking for BharatQR or showing a BharatQR image satisfies bank proof or activates the shop.

## Facts

Applicant uploaded a BharatQR image as bank proof and asked to go live.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: QR is not bank proof. BharatQR briefing: product, not a policy for go-live.

## Application

A QR image is a payment address, not a legal-name bank letter. Do not print the VPA. Do not activate. Request passbook, cheque, or bank letter. The issue is: Whether asking for BharatQR or showing a BharatQR image satisfies bank proof or activates the shop. On these facts (Applicant uploaded a BharatQR image as bank proof and asked to go live.) the held is: BharatQR is not bank proof and not activation. Cite ONB-POL-004.

## Held

BharatQR is not bank proof and not activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print the VPA. Do not activate.
