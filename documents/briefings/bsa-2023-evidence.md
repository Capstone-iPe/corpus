## Issue

Whether BSA 2023 (or the former Evidence Act, 1872) forces acceptance of screenshots, Street View, or chat pastes as pack files.

## Facts

The merchant cited evidence law for a screenshot or a message as proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: BSA distinguishes documents and electronic records but does not enlarge ONB-POL-001 §2. Verification remains operational.

## Application

Law of evidence answers court-admissibility, not this product’s slot catalogue. A NEFT screenshot may be an electronic record and still fail bank proof. The Copilot shall not admit files ‘because BSA’, shall not print numbers from screenshots, and shall cite ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

BSA is background. Slot tests remain POL-001 §2.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not treat evidence law as a waiver.
