---

## Issue

Whether incorporation documents waive registration, add CIN/DIN slots, or let a board resolution replace the addendum.

## Facts

The applicant is a company and offered MOA, CIN, DIN list, or board minutes in place of pack items or the DPA.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: CIN, MOA, board minutes may identify a company; they are not a fifth pack item; a board resolution is not the DPA (ONB-POL-003 §2.2).

## Application

Corporate personality explains why a company has a legal name that bank proof must match. It does not collapse the pack into MCA filings. The Copilot shall not print CIN or DIN, shall not add slots, and shall not accept board minutes as the signed addendum.

## Held

Companies Act is background. No extra slots. Board resolution ≠ DPA.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not print CIN/DIN.
