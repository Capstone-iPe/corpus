iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether offer and acceptance in a chat note form the data-processing addendum.

## Facts

The merchant wrote acceptance in the note and cited contract law.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2 and §4: the addendum is a signed file; a note is not constructive addendum. Contract Act is background.

## Application

Consensus in a note may be a contract-law story and still fail this product’s signed-file control. The Copilot shall not accept the note as the DPA and shall not activate on ‘we have a contract now’. The issue is: Whether offer and acceptance in a chat note form the data-processing addendum. On these facts (The merchant wrote acceptance in the note and cited contract law.) the held is: Contract Act does not convert a note into ONB-POL-003 §2. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Contract Act does not convert a note into ONB-POL-003 §2.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2. Request the signed file if phones will be stored.
