---

## Issue

Whether IP registrations (designs, patents, GI) are pack items or type aliases to retail.

## Facts

Applicant offered a patent, design, or GI certificate as KYC or as proof they are retail of goods.

## Rule

Designs/Patents/GI briefings: out of scope of pack and verdict.

## Application

IP is not shopfront, not bank proof, and not a type. Do not add slots. Classify the stated activity. Do not print application numbers. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether IP registrations (designs, patents, GI) are pack items or type aliases to retail. On these facts (Applicant offered a patent, design, or GI certificate as KYC or as proof they are retail of goods.) the held is: IP registrations are not pack items and not type. Out of verdict. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-001 §2.

## Held

IP registrations are not pack items and not type. Out of verdict.

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add IP slots. Do not print numbers.
