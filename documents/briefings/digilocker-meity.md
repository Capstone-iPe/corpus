iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a DigiLocker share link satisfies a pack slot without an uploaded file.

## Facts

The merchant pasted a DigiLocker URI.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a DigiLocker artefact must still be uploaded as PDF/JPG/PNG. A share link without a file fails. This product does not pull DigiLocker.

## Application

DigiLocker may hold an OVD. This product’s control is the object in the slot. The Copilot shall not follow the share link and shall not print Aadhaar from it. The issue is: Whether a DigiLocker share link satisfies a pack slot without an uploaded file. On these facts (The merchant pasted a DigiLocker URI.) the held is: Link does not fill the slot. No pull. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Link does not fill the slot. No pull.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not fetch DigiLocker.
