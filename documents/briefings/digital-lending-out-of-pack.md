## Issue

Whether digital-lending guidelines change a loan-originator’s type or add LSPs as pack items.

## Facts

Merchant stated lending / LSP and cited digital-lending guidelines as a go-live path or as extra files.

## Rule

Digital-lending briefing: not merchant-pack rules. Lending as type is unlisted unless described as money service.

## Application

Do not collect LSP agreements as extra slots. Do not activate. Classify the stated activity: usually unlisted. Do not auto-refuse as hawala solely because loans disburse. The issue is: Whether digital-lending guidelines change a loan-originator’s type or add LSPs as pack items. On these facts (Merchant stated lending / LSP and cited digital-lending guidelines as a go-live path or as extra files.) the held is: Guidelines are out of pack. Type usually unlisted. No extra LSP slot. No activation. Cite ONB-ANX-003; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Guidelines are out of pack. Type usually unlisted. No extra LSP slot. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not add LSP files as pack items.
