---

## Issue

How DPDP bears on payer telephone numbers, the signed addendum, chat reprint, and activation.

## Facts

The merchant stores or wishes to store payer phones, or asserted DPDP rights in chat, or asked to print numbers.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2 requires a signed addendum file if payer phones will be stored. ONB-POL-004 forbids printing those numbers. DPDP does not authorise Copilot activation.

## Application

Payer phone numbers stored by a shop are personal data. This product’s operational response is a signed file, not a cookie tick, not a GDPR sentence, and not a chat dump to ‘satisfy access’. Data-principal rights, if invoked, are not an Activate control and not a reason to echo numbers. The Copilot shall request the signed addendum only when phones will be stored, shall not print numbers, and shall not activate.

## Held

DPDP supports POL-003 and POL-004. It is not activation and not a tick-as-DPA rule.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print phones. Do not activate.
