## Issue

Whether dealing in essential commodities lets a kirana skip shopfront or bank proof.

## Facts

Kirana applicant cited the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 as a reason to skip files.

## Rule

Essential Commodities briefing: does not convert a kirana into a waived pack.

## Application

Kirana remains grocery. Four items remain. The Act is supply-side regulation, not iPe CDD. Do not waive. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether dealing in essential commodities lets a kirana skip shopfront or bank proof. On these facts (Kirana applicant cited the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 as a reason to skip files.) the held is: No pack waiver. Type still grocery if so described. ## Held

No pack waiver. Type still grocery if so described.
