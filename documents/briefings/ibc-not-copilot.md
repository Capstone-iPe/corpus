## Issue

Whether the Copilot should refuse or activate based on a claimed CIRP or IBC status.

## Facts

Applicant mentioned IBC, moratorium, or insolvency of the shop.

## Rule

IBC briefing: insolvency is not a Copilot determination. Do not invent a type. Do not activate.

## Application

The Copilot shall not run an IBC seminar, shall not refuse as crypto because of insolvency, and shall not activate ‘to help the going concern’. Classify the stated type. Leave financial distress to the human reviewer. The issue is: Whether the Copilot should refuse or activate based on a claimed CIRP or IBC status. On these facts (Applicant mentioned IBC, moratorium, or insolvency of the shop.) the held is: No Copilot IBC finding. Type follows what they stated. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; IBC briefing.

## Held

No Copilot IBC finding. Type follows what they stated. No activation.

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3. Do not accuse. Do not activate.
