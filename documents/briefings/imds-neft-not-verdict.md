iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a successful NEFT test payment can replace bank proof or activate the shop.

## Facts

Applicant sent a rupee via NEFT and said that proves the account and should go live.

## Rule

RBI retail-payment-systems briefing: settlement rails are not onboarding verdict inputs. A transfer screenshot is not bank proof.

## Application

A test debit is not a passbook in the legal name. Do not print the counterparty account. Do not activate. Request proper bank proof. The issue is: Whether a successful NEFT test payment can replace bank proof or activate the shop. On these facts (Applicant sent a rupee via NEFT and said that proves the account and should go live.) the held is: Rails ≠ bank proof. Rails ≠ activation. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Rails ≠ bank proof. Rails ≠ activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print account digits. Do not activate.
