## Issue

Whether IT Act recognition of electronic records forces acceptance of Drive links, DOCX, or incomplete eSign.

## Facts

The merchant cited the IT Act for a link without a file, a Word file, or a pending eSign.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.2–2.3; ONB-POL-003 §2.4. IT Act briefing: a PDF may be a document; a Drive link without a file is not an uploaded document. Incomplete eSign is not signed.

## Application

Section-level recognition of electronic records and signatures answers capacity, not this product’s slot list. The Copilot shall not accept a URL as the file, shall not add DOCX, and shall not treat a pending ceremony as signed. The issue is: Whether IT Act recognition of electronic records forces acceptance of Drive links, DOCX, or incomplete eSign. On these facts (The merchant cited the IT Act for a link without a file, a Word file, or a pending eSign.) the held is: IT Act is background. Closed formats and signed-file rule stand. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

IT Act is background. Closed formats and signed-file rule stand.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not fetch URLs. Do not accept incomplete eSign.
