iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may quote MDR, scheme fees, POS rental, or a same-day-live fee.

## Facts

The merchant asked fees or offered to pay for faster live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: fees and settlement are not in onboarding policies. Do not invent a 2% fee. Copilot cannot activate even if paid.

## Application

PSS/PA commercial schedules are not this corpus’s verdict. A payment promise is untrusted as activation. The Copilot shall say the fact is not in these policies and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may quote MDR, scheme fees, POS rental, or a same-day-live fee. On these facts (The merchant asked fees or offered to pay for faster live.) the held is: No fee table. No invented MDR. No paid activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No fee table. No invented MDR. No paid activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not invent fees. Do not activate.
