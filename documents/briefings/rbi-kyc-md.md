## Issue

How the RBI KYC Master Direction bears on owner ID and the pack, including OVDs, CKYCR, and video-CIP.

## Facts

The merchant cited RBI KYC to demand CKYCR pull, video KYC, beneficial-owner forms, or a reduced pack.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and §2: operational pack is four items. No CKYCR, CERSAI, or UIDAI call. OVDs may appear as the owner-ID file. Simplified CDD does not drop slots here.

## Application

KYC Directions supply vocabulary (CDD, OVD, beneficial owner) that makes owner ID and registration intelligible. They do not rewrite the four-item list. Officially valid documents may satisfy owner ID if that is the uploaded file; they are not bank proof. The Copilot shall not pull CKYCR, shall not start video-CIP, shall not demand a beneficial-owner declaration as a fifth slot, shall not print OVD numbers, and shall not drop shopfront because volume is small.

## Held

KYC MD is background. Pack remains four items. No registry calls.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §1.5. Do not call CKYCR/UIDAI. Do not print numbers.
