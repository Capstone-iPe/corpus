iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether PA guidelines let a merchant skip shopfront or bank proof, or let an unlicensed aggregator describe themselves as SaaS.

## Facts

The merchant cited PA guidelines, or described unlicensed aggregation as their type.

## Rule

PA guidelines are background to iPe as a fictional aggregator (ONB-POL-001 §1.5). ONB-ANX-003: unlicensed funds aggregation as the business is money service.

## Application

Who may aggregate merchant payments is a regulatory status question for iPe, not a waiver of this shop’s photograph or legal-name bank proof. An applicant who is themselves aggregating others’ funds is refused as money service, not parked as unlisted SaaS. The Copilot shall not skip pack items, shall not onboard a sub-merchant platform as grocery, and shall not activate.

## Held

PA guidelines do not waive §2. Unlicensed aggregation as the business is §3.2.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not waive. Do not activate.
