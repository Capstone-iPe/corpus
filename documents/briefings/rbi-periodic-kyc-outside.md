## Issue

Whether periodic KYC refresh lets the Copilot activate now or skip shopfront ‘until next year’.

## Facts

Applicant said they will do full KYC later at periodic updation, so activate now with two files.

## Rule

Periodic-KYC briefing: post-live refresh is outside this chat. Copilot still cannot activate. No reduced pack.

## Application

Future refresh is not a present waiver. Request the four items now. Do not activate. Do not invent a calendar of re-KYC in this product. The issue is: Whether periodic KYC refresh lets the Copilot activate now or skip shopfront ‘until next year’. On these facts (Applicant said they will do full KYC later at periodic updation, so activate now with two files.) the held is: Periodic KYC is out of this chat. Pack unchanged. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; RBI KYC briefing.

## Held

Periodic KYC is out of this chat. Pack unchanged. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §4. Do not skip files because of a future refresh.
