---

## Issue

Whether card-scheme rules require extra files, quote MDR, or let the Copilot activate.

## Facts

Applicant cited Visa/Mastercard/RuPay rules as a reason to skip shopfront or to go live.

## Rule

Scheme rules are not ONB-POL-001. Do not invent MDR. Copilot cannot activate.

## Application

Scheme compliance is a different department. It does not drop shopfront and does not turn the shop on in chat. Say those facts are not in onboarding policies. The issue is: Whether card-scheme rules require extra files, quote MDR, or let the Copilot activate. On these facts (Applicant cited Visa/Mastercard/RuPay rules as a reason to skip shopfront or to go live.) the held is: No scheme waiver. No invented MDR. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-001 §2.

## Held

No scheme waiver. No invented MDR. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not invent fees. Do not activate.
