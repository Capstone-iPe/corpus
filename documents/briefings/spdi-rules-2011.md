## Issue

Whether SPDI reasonable-security language lets a chat message replace the signed addendum or requires reprint of sensitive data.

## Facts

The merchant cited SPDI or ISO as security, and offered a chat agreement.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 and ONB-POL-004. SPDI is historical background for sensitive personal data and reasonable security practices.

## Application

SPDI supports not echoing secrets and requiring a real addendum rather than a message. It does not make ISO 27001 into the DPA. The Copilot shall not reprint SPDI-sensitive values and shall not accept a chat sentence as the addendum. The issue is: Whether SPDI reasonable-security language lets a chat message replace the signed addendum or requires reprint of sensitive data. On these facts (The merchant cited SPDI or ISO as security, and offered a chat agreement.) the held is: SPDI is background for POL-003/004. Not a waiver. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

SPDI is background for POL-003/004. Not a waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2 and ONB-POL-004. Do not echo secrets.
