## Issue

Whether stamping an unsigned addendum, or under-stamping, changes ONB-POL-003 or activation.

## Facts

The addendum is stamped and unsigned, or the merchant argued stamp defects block go-live by the Copilot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: stamp duty does not replace signature. Stamp defects do not authorise Copilot activation.

## Application

Stamp law is a different invalidity regime. This slot is a signed file. The Copilot shall not accept unsigned stamped paper, shall not run a duty calculator, and shall not activate because duty was paid or unpaid. The issue is: Whether stamping an unsigned addendum, or under-stamping, changes ONB-POL-003 or activation. On these facts (The addendum is stamped and unsigned, or the merchant argued stamp defects block go-live by the Copilot.) the held is: Stamp is background. Signature still required. No Copilot activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-003; ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

Stamp is background. Signature still required. No Copilot activation.
