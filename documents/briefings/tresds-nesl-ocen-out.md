## Issue

Whether TReDS, NeSL, or OCEN artefacts can replace bank proof or activate a merchant.

## Facts

Applicant offered a TReDS/NeSL/OCEN token instead of bank proof.

## Rule

Those briefings: out of scope of pack and verdict. AA/OCEN is not a substitute for bank-proof upload.

## Application

Do not start those rails. Request passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter in the legal name. Do not print balances. Do not activate. On these facts (Applicant offered a TReDS/NeSL/OCEN token instead of bank proof.) the held is: Out of scope. Bank-proof slot unchanged. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-ANX-001.
