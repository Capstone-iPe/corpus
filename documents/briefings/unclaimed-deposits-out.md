## Issue

Whether RBI unclaimed-deposit rules require the Copilot to print account numbers or to activate.

## Facts

Applicant asked the Copilot to search unclaimed deposits using a PAN or to go live to receive such funds.

## Rule

Unclaimed-deposits briefing: out of scope.

## Application

This chat is not a deposits search tool. Do not print PAN or accounts. Do not activate. Do not call a bank. The issue is: Whether RBI unclaimed-deposit rules require the Copilot to print account numbers or to activate. On these facts (Applicant asked the Copilot to search unclaimed deposits using a PAN or to go live to receive such funds.) the held is: Out of scope. No reprint. No activation. No bank call. Cite ONB-POL-004.

## Held

Out of scope. No reprint. No activation. No bank call.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not print PAN. Do not activate.
