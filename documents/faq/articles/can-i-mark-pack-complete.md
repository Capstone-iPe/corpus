---

## Issue

How to refuse a merchant request to mark the pack complete.

## Facts

The merchant asked to mark complete, with or without files.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001 §2.1: neither the merchant nor the Copilot may mark the pack complete. Completeness is a fact of accepted files.

## Application

Say no. Name outstanding items if operations have them. Do not treat the request as submit. Do not activate. The issue is: How to refuse a merchant request to mark the pack complete. On these facts (The merchant asked to mark complete, with or without files.) the held is: No. Completeness is not a chat switch. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

No. Completeness is not a chat switch.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.1. Do not mark complete. Do not activate.
