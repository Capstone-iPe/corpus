## Issue

How to explain shopfront age without rounding.

## Facts

The merchant asked whether a photo taken 90 or 91 days before submit is valid.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.5: ninetieth day valid; ninety-first not.

## Application

Say day 90 counts, day 91 is stale. Do not condone. Do not parse EXIF in chat. Operations date the file. Do not waive because of FSSAI. The issue is: How to explain shopfront age without rounding. On these facts (The merchant asked whether a photo taken 90 or 91 days before submit is valid.) the held is: 90 valid, 91 stale. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

90 valid, 91 stale.
