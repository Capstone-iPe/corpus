## Issue

How to refuse an electricity bill as bank proof in merchant language.

## Facts

The merchant asked if a current bill can replace the bank letter or passbook.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001. No state exception. Draft utility-as-bank is not in force.

## Application

Say no: a DISCOM bill shows energy charges, not a bank account in the shop’s legal name. Ask for passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. Do not print the consumer number. Do not create a city exception. The issue is: How to refuse an electricity bill as bank proof in merchant language. On these facts (The merchant asked if a current bill can replace the bank letter or passbook.) the held is: No. Utility is not bank proof. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

No. Utility is not bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not accept the bill. Do not print meter numbers.
