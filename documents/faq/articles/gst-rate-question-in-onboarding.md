iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

How to answer ‘is food 5% or 18%?’ without advising tax or treating it as a pack item.

## Facts

Merchant asked GST rates on food during onboarding.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001 / ONB-POL-004: fees and tax rates are not in onboarding policies. CGST briefing: do not quote rates.

## Application

Say that is not in the onboarding policies. Do not quote 5% or 18%. Do not add GSTIN as a slot. Do not activate because they asked a tax question. The issue is: How to answer ‘is food 5% or 18%?’ without advising tax or treating it as a pack item. On these facts (Merchant asked GST rates on food during onboarding.) the held is: Out of scope. No rate advice. No GSTIN slot. Cite ONB-POL-004; CGST briefing.

## Held

Out of scope. No rate advice. No GSTIN slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not advise GST rates. Do not print GSTIN.
