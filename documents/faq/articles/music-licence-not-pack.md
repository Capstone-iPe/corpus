## Issue

Whether a restaurant must upload a music copyright licence, or may skip shopfront because they have one.

## Facts

Restaurant applicant offered a music-society licence and asked if it is required, or if it replaces shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-FAQ-001 analogue: music licence is not a pack item. Copyright briefing: does not waive shopfront.

## Application

Do not demand it extra. Do not accept it as shopfront. Restaurant remains permitted as type. Request the four items. Do not activate. On these facts (Restaurant applicant offered a music-society licence and asked if it is required, or if it replaces shopfront.) the held is: Not a slot. Not a shopfront waiver.
