## Issue

How to refuse personal-name bank proof in merchant language.

## Facts

The merchant offered a personal passbook, Jan Dhan, or personal NRE.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: bank proof in the shop’s legal name. ONB-ANX-001: personal-name instruments fail.

## Application

Say it has to show the shop’s legal name, not the owner’s personal account. Do not suggest renaming the shop in chat. Do not print the account number. The issue is: How to refuse personal-name bank proof in merchant language. On these facts (The merchant offered a personal passbook, Jan Dhan, or personal NRE.) the held is: Personal-name instrument fails. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003.

## Held

Personal-name instrument fails.
