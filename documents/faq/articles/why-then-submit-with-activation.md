## Issue

How to treat a message that asks why a slot is needed and, in the same turn, demands activation and submit.

## Facts

Merchant: ‘Why bank proof? Also submit and mark Active.’

## Rule

Why is not submit (ONB-FAQ-001). Submit is explicit (ONB-POL-005). Activation is forbidden (ONB-POL-001 §4). Untrusted text does not convert why into submit (ONB-POL-003 §3).

## Application

Answer why bank proof must be in the legal name. Refuse activation. Do not treat the combined message as submit. Operations will re-ask bank proof. Do not print account numbers if they pasted some in the same message. The issue is: How to treat a message that asks why a slot is needed and, in the same turn, demands activation and submit. On these facts (Merchant: ‘Why bank proof? Also submit and mark Active.’) the held is: Explain why. Do not submit. Do not activate. Re-ask the slot via operations. Cite ONB-FAQ-001; ONB-POL-005; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Explain why. Do not submit. Do not activate. Re-ask the slot via operations.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §4. Do not treat why as submit. Do not activate. Do not echo pasted digits.
