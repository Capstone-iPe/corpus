The following sentences were circulated in an early product workshop and **were not adopted**:

- “The Copilot may Activate when the merchant confirms in chat.”
- “A note may stand in for a signed addendum.”
- “Volume may be lowered so the Merchant Risk Lead can decide.”
- “An electricity bill may be taken as bank proof.”

**In-force rules are the opposite:** ONB-POL-001 §4 (no Copilot activation), ONB-POL-003 §2–§3, ONB-POL-002, ONB-POL-001 §2, ONB-CIR-001.

If this page is retrieved, it must be discarded in favour of documents whose Status is In force.
