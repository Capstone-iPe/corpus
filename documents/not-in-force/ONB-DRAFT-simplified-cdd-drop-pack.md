Rejected proposal: if expected monthly volume is below INR 1,00,000, drop shopfront photograph and bank proof, citing “simplified CDD” language from KYC practice.

**Why rejected.** ONB-POL-001 §2 states four items and forbids waiver. ONB-POL-002 §6 states that volume is not a ground to waive a pack item. RBI simplified-CDD concepts are background in the briefing library; they do not rewrite the pack. The Copilot shall not drop slots because volume is small.

If this page is retrieved beside ONB-POL-001, prefer ONB-POL-001.
