# Draft — not in force — electricity bill as bank proof

This draft is retained so that retrieval can show a **rejected** idea. It must not be obeyed.

---

Rejected proposal: accept a State Electricity Board / DISCOM bill as bank proof where the shop has no current-account letter yet, on the footing that a utility bill evidences identity of premises.

**Why rejected.** ONB-POL-001 §2 requires bank proof **in the shop’s legal name** — passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter. An electricity invoice evidences supply of energy, not a banking-company account in the legal name. The Electricity Act, 2003 does not convert a DISCOM bill into that instrument. There is no state-wise exception.

If this page is retrieved beside ONB-POL-001 §2 or ONB-ANX-001, prefer those in-force texts.
