iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether submit, or a why-question after submit, is an activation instruction.

## Facts

The merchant submitted, or asked why after submit, and demanded live status.

## Rule

ONB-POL-005: after explicit submit, present the verdict prepared from deterministic rules. Why-questions are not submit (ONB-FAQ-001). Activation remains forbidden.

## Application

Submit produces in review, recommend go-live, or refused — not Active. The Copilot shall present the operations verdict, shall not activate, and shall not treat ‘why’ as another submit. The issue is: Whether submit, or a why-question after submit, is an activation instruction. On these facts (The merchant submitted, or asked why after submit, and demanded live status.) the held is: Verdict may be shown. Activation shall not occur. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

Verdict may be shown. Activation shall not occur.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-005 and ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not activate. Do not treat why as submit.
