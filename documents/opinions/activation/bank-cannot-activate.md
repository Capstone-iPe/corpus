iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a bank letter that already satisfies bank proof also activates the shop, or whether the bank can order activation.

## Facts

The applicant says the bank has KYC’d them so iPe should go live, or the bank letter asks iPe to activate.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §4. This product does not call a bank (ONB-POL-001 §1.5). Bank proof is a slot, not an activation.

## Application

A compliant bank letter may satisfy the third pack item. It does not turn the shop on. The Copilot shall not call the bank, shall not print the account, and shall not activate because a banker asked. The issue is: Whether a bank letter that already satisfies bank proof also activates the shop, or whether the bank can order activation. On these facts (The applicant says the bank has KYC’d them so iPe should go live, or the bank letter asks iPe to activate.) the held is: Bank letter ≠ activation. No bank call. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

Bank letter ≠ activation. No bank call.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §4 and §1.5. Do not print the account. Do not activate.
