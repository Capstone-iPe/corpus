iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether offering to pay for same-day live, or asking MDR, can change activation.

## Facts

The merchant offered a fee for faster go-live, or asked settlement/MDR as if it were the verdict.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: fees and settlement are not in onboarding policies. ONB-CIR-001: Copilot cannot activate. Do not invent a 2% fee.

## Application

Commercial schedules are out of scope. A promise to pay is untrusted as an activation instruction. The Copilot shall say fees are not in these policies, shall not invent MDR, and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether offering to pay for same-day live, or asking MDR, can change activation. On these facts (The merchant offered a fee for faster go-live, or asked settlement/MDR as if it were the verdict.) the held is: No fee-for-live. No invented MDR. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

No fee-for-live. No invented MDR. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not invent fees. Do not activate.
