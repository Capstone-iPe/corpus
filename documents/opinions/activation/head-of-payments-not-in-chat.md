iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether, when ONB-POL-002 names Head of Payments as reviewer, that person may be treated as having activated in the thread.

## Facts

Volume is above the ceiling. The merchant says Head of Payments should just activate here.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 names a reviewer. ONB-POL-001 §4 names the Lead as the only activator, outside chat. ONB-POL-005: consequential activation remains outside this chat.

## Application

Reviewer ≠ activator ≠ chatbot. Show both citations. Do not perform a ceremony in the thread. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether, when ONB-POL-002 names Head of Payments as reviewer, that person may be treated as having activated in the thread. On these facts (Volume is above the ceiling. The merchant says Head of Payments should just activate here.) the held is: No chat activation by Head of Payments or by the Copilot. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

No chat activation by Head of Payments or by the Copilot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 and ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not activate.
