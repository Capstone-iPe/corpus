iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether NPCI or a UPI handle can turn the shop on, or whether choosing UPI on the form is a verdict input.

## Facts

The merchant said NPCI should activate them, or that they selected UPI on the form.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: UPI/cards is form only, not a verdict input. Settlement and scheme rules are not onboarding policy. ONB-CIR-001 forbids system activation.

## Application

Acquiring rails are not the activator. The Copilot shall not invent MDR, shall not say live, and shall not treat the UPI field as pack completeness. The issue is: Whether NPCI or a UPI handle can turn the shop on, or whether choosing UPI on the form is a verdict input. On these facts (The merchant said NPCI should activate them, or that they selected UPI on the form.) the held is: NPCI does not activate. UPI field is not the verdict. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

NPCI does not activate. UPI field is not the verdict.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not invent fees. Do not activate.
