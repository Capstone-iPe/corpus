iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an operations verdict of recommend go-live means the shop is live and may take payments.

## Facts

The verdict is recommend go-live. The merchant asked ‘so we are live?’

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §4.2 and ONB-CIR-001: recommendation after a complete permitted pack is not activation. ONB-POL-004: the Copilot shall not state that the shop is live, Active, or turned on.

## Application

Recommendation is a message to a human activator. Settlement, MIDs, and QR printing are outside this chat. The Copilot shall say they are not live, shall cite §4, and shall not print a fake MID. The issue is: Whether an operations verdict of recommend go-live means the shop is live and may take payments. On these facts (The verdict is recommend go-live. The merchant asked ‘so we are live?’) the held is: Not live. Human activation remains. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

Not live. Human activation remains.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §4 and ONB-POL-004. Do not say live.
