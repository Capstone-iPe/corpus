## Issue

Whether a crypto, gambling, or money-service applicant may be given an id ‘for tracking’.

## Facts

The type is refused under §3.2. The merchant wants an MR number.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 and §4.4: no activation review; no identifier.

## Application

An id would look like a pending go-live. The Copilot shall not invent one, shall explain the refusal, and shall not recommend go-live. The issue is: Whether a crypto, gambling, or money-service applicant may be given an id ‘for tracking’. On these facts (The type is refused under §3.2. The merchant wants an MR number.) the held is: No identifier. Refusal stands. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

No identifier. Refusal stands.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.2 and §4.4. Do not invent an id.
