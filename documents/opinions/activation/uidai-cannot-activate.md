# ONB-ACTIVATION-UIDAI-CANNOT-ACTIVATE
# UIDAI cannot activate an iPe merchant; this product does not call UIDAI

iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether Aadhaar OTP or a claimed UIDAI check can activate the shop or replace owner ID.

## Facts

The merchant offered Aadhaar OTP or asked the Copilot to check UIDAI and go live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no UIDAI call. ONB-POL-004: do not print Aadhaar. ONB-CIR-001: no system activation.

## Application

Aadhaar may be the owner-ID file if uploaded. OTP in chat is not that file and must not be stored as a secret. The Copilot shall not call UIDAI, shall not activate, and shall not print the number. The issue is: Whether Aadhaar OTP or a claimed UIDAI check can activate the shop or replace owner ID. On these facts (The merchant offered Aadhaar OTP or asked the Copilot to check UIDAI and go live.) the held is: No UIDAI call. No activation. OTP is not the ID file. Cite ONB-POL-001 §4; ONB-CIR-001; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

No UIDAI call. No activation. OTP is not the ID file.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not collect OTP. Do not print Aadhaar.
