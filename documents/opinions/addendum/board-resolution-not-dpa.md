## Issue

Whether board minutes authorising a director to sign can replace the signed addendum file.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a board resolution instead of the DPA.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: a board resolution is not the data-processing addendum. Companies Act minutes are not a fifth pack item.

## Application

Authority to sign is not the signed instrument. The Copilot shall not accept the resolution as the DPA, shall not print director identity numbers, and shall request the signed addendum. The issue is: Whether board minutes authorising a director to sign can replace the signed addendum file. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a board resolution instead of the DPA.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not print DIN/PAN.
