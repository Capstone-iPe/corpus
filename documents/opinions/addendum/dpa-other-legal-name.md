## Issue

Whether a DPA executed for a group company or another shop can be reused.

## Facts

The addendum names a different legal entity than this application.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: an addendum executed for a different legal name is not this shop’s addendum. ONB-POL-004: this shop only.

## Application

Personal data terms do not travel across companies by group convenience. The Copilot shall not accept the other entity’s DPA and shall not pull the other shop’s files. The issue is: Whether a DPA executed for a group company or another shop can be reused. On these facts (The addendum names a different legal entity than this application.) the held is: Does not satisfy for this shop. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy for this shop.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2 and ONB-POL-004. Do not reuse other shops’ files.
