iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an employee confidentiality agreement satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded staff NDAs.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: an employee NDA does not authorise processing of payer phones for iPe.

## Application

Staff secrecy is the wrong contract and the wrong parties. The Copilot shall not accept NDAs as the addendum and shall not print staff names or phones. The issue is: Whether an employee confidentiality agreement satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2. On these facts (The applicant uploaded staff NDAs.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not print staff PII.
