iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an ISO 27001 certificate can replace the signed addendum.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an ISO certificate for the addendum slot.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: ISO is not the addendum. Certification is a management-system claim, not this shop’s signed processing terms with iPe.

## Application

Controls certification does not authorise processing of payer phones for this product. The Copilot shall not accept ISO as the DPA and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether an ISO 27001 certificate can replace the signed addendum. On these facts (The applicant uploaded an ISO certificate for the addendum slot.) the held is: Does not satisfy the addendum. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy the addendum.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not treat ISO as the DPA.
