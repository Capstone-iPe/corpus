iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether an OTP on a POS device can replace the signed addendum.

## Facts

The applicant said customers already OTP on POS.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: POS consent is not the onboarding addendum.

## Application

Device-level consent, if any, is not this file. The Copilot shall not collect OTPs and shall request the signed addendum if the shop will store payer phones. The issue is: Whether an OTP on a POS device can replace the signed addendum. On these facts (The applicant said customers already OTP on POS.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Do not collect OTP. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy. Do not collect OTP.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.2. Do not collect OTP.
