## Issue

Whether an unsigned addendum on stamp paper satisfies ONB-POL-003 because it is stamped under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899.

## Facts

The addendum is on stamp paper and unsigned.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.4; ONB-ANX-001: stamp duty does not replace signature. State stamp statutes likewise do not authorise Copilot activation.

## Application

Insufficient or sufficient stamping is a stamp-law question; this slot is signature. The Copilot shall not accept unsigned stamped paper and shall not activate because duty was paid. The issue is: Whether an unsigned addendum on stamp paper satisfies ONB-POL-003 because it is stamped under the Indian Stamp Act, 1899. On these facts (The addendum is on stamp paper and unsigned.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not activate.
