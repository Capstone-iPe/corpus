## Issue

Whether an uploaded but unsigned data-processing addendum satisfies the signed-file requirement when payer phones will be stored.

## Facts

The shop will store payer telephone numbers. An addendum PDF is in the slot with a blank signature block.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.4: uploaded but unsigned does not clear the slot. Indian Contract Act, 1872: agreement; this product additionally requires a signed file. The shop remains in review if the addendum was required.

## Application

DPDP does not convert an unsigned template into a completed agreement. The Copilot shall not treat the upload as complete, shall not activate, and shall not print payer numbers to ‘prove’ the DPA exists. The issue is: Whether an uploaded but unsigned data-processing addendum satisfies the signed-file requirement when payer phones will be stored. On these facts (The shop will store payer telephone numbers. An addendum PDF is in the slot with a blank signature block.) the held is: Does not satisfy the addendum. In review if phones will be stored. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy the addendum. In review if phones will be stored.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2.4. Do not activate. Do not print phones.
