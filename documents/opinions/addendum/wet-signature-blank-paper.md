## Issue

Whether a photographed signature without the addendum terms satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a signature on otherwise blank paper.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a signature without the addendum terms is not the addendum.

## Application

A mark without terms is not the processing agreement. The Copilot shall not accept it and shall not store the image as if it were PII-authorising. The issue is: Whether a photographed signature without the addendum terms satisfies ONB-POL-003 §2. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a signature on otherwise blank paper.) the held is: Does not satisfy. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-001; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request the signed addendum file.
