## Issue

Whether a drawing or computer render of a future facade satisfies shopfront.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an architect’s elevation and a 3D render, saying the shop will look like that.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: architect’s elevation and 3D render are not capable. The slot is a photograph of the existing front, not more than ninety days before submit.

## Application

A drawing is not photographic evidence of a present facade. Future fit-out does not start the ninety-day clock. The Copilot shall not accept the render, shall not waive because construction is incomplete, and shall not activate a shop that ‘will be ready on go-live day’.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not accept future facades.
