## Issue

Whether a photograph of a BIS mark or certificate satisfies shopfront or registration.

## Facts

Applicant uploaded a BIS certificate for shopfront.

## Rule

BIS briefing: certification marks are not shopfront photos. ONB-ANX-001: not the front of the premises.

## Application

A standards mark is not a dated photograph of the facade. Request the shopfront photograph. Do not add BIS as a fifth item. Do not print certificate numbers. The issue is: Whether a photograph of a BIS mark or certificate satisfies shopfront or registration. On these facts (Applicant uploaded a BIS certificate for shopfront.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Not a fifth item. Cite ONB-ANX-001; BIS briefing.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront. Not a fifth item.
