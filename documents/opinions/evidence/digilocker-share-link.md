---

## Issue

Whether a DigiLocker share URI satisfies a pack slot without an uploaded PDF/JPG/PNG.

## Facts

The applicant pasted a DigiLocker share link for owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: DigiLocker artefact still must be uploaded as PDF/JPG/PNG into the slot. A share link without a file fails. This product does not pull DigiLocker.

## Application

MeitY’s DigiLocker may hold an OVD. This product still needs the object in the slot. The Copilot shall not follow the share link and shall not print Aadhaar from it. The issue is: Whether a DigiLocker share URI satisfies a pack slot without an uploaded PDF/JPG/PNG. On these facts (The applicant pasted a DigiLocker share link for owner ID.) the held is: Link does not fill the slot. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Link does not fill the slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not fetch DigiLocker.
