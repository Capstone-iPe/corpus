## Issue

Whether a print of director identification numbers satisfies owner ID.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a DIN list for owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a DIN list is not the owner’s government ID document. DIN is not a slot.

## Application

DIN identifies a director in the MCA system. It is not an OVD. The Copilot shall not print DINs, shall not add a slot, and shall request the owner’s government ID. The issue is: Whether a print of director identification numbers satisfies owner ID. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a DIN list for owner ID.) the held is: Does not satisfy owner ID. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print DINs.
