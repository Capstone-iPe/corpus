## Issue

Whether a .docx business registration can be accepted because it is electronic.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a Word document as registration.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.2: PDF, JPG, or PNG. A Word document does not satisfy the slot; the same slot is requested again. The IT Act’s recognition of electronic records does not add DOCX to that list.

## Application

Electronic does not mean ‘any extension’. The pack’s format list is closed. Request PDF, JPG or PNG of the registration. Do not convert the file in chat. The issue is: Whether a .docx business registration can be accepted because it is electronic. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a Word document as registration.) the held is: Fails format. Slot outstanding. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Fails format. Slot outstanding.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Do not convert files.
