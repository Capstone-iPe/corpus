## Issue

Whether a Google Drive, Dropbox, or similar link satisfies a pack slot without a PDF/JPG/PNG in the slot.

## Facts

The applicant pasted a Drive link and said the registration is attached.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.3: a statement that a file is attached, without a file, does not satisfy the slot. ONB-ANX-001: a Drive, DigiLocker or WhatsApp link with no file in the slot is not capable.

## Application

This product verifies an uploaded object in an accepted format. A URL is a pointer, often permissioned, and is not the file. The Copilot shall not follow the link, shall not scrape it, and shall say the slot is empty. IT Act recognition of electronic records still requires a record in the slot, not a path to one.

## Held

Slot not filled.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.3. Do not fetch the URL.
