## Issue

Whether an FSSAI licence under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 replaces business registration or shopfront.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded FSSAI and asked to skip registration and shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: FSSAI offered instead of registration is not automatically that slot. ONB-ANX-003: FSSAI is not a type and not a waiver. It is not a fifth item.

## Application

FSSAI licences a food business operator. It is not, without more, the legal registration extract, not bank proof, and not a photograph of the front. The Copilot shall not add FSSAI as a required fifth file and shall not waive §2 items because it exists. If operations determine the same PDF is in fact the registration extract, that is an operational finding — not these facts as presented (‘instead of’).

## Held

Does not by itself satisfy registration or shopfront. Not a fifth slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not waive shopfront. Do not demand FSSAI extra.
