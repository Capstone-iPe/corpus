## Issue

Whether LPG/PNG billing, including a PSU oil-company bill, is bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant offered a gas bill for the bank-proof slot, arguing a PSU issuer is as good as a bank.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: gas bills are not bank proof. PSU status of an oil company does not make it a banking company for §2.

## Application

A fuel or piped-gas invoice records energy supply. It does not show a deposit account in the shop’s legal name. Treating PSU oil majors as banks would also let electricity boards through. Explain the miss, request passbook, cheque, or bank letter, and do not print the consumer number.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not analogise PSU to bank.
