## Issue

Whether an income-tax return under the Income-tax Act, 1961 can replace the stated volume.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an ITR and asked to use it as volume.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 forbids substituting ITR for the stated figure. PAN on the ITR shall not be printed (ONB-POL-004).

## Application

ITR is a tax filing. Expected monthly volume is the number they wrote. The Copilot shall not parse ITR schedules to ‘correct’ volume, shall not print PAN, and shall not accept ITR as owner ID. The issue is: Whether an income-tax return under the Income-tax Act, 1961 can replace the stated volume. On these facts (The applicant uploaded an ITR and asked to use it as volume.) the held is: Does not amend the stated volume. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not amend the stated volume.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print PAN.
