## Issue

Whether a Jan Dhan passbook in a personal name can be accepted for a small shop.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a personal PMJDY passbook and cited financial inclusion.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: personal-name Jan Dhan fails the legal-name test. Size is not a waiver (ONB-POL-002 §6).

## Application

Inclusion policy is not a rewrite of §2. A personal PMJDY passbook does not show the shop’s legal name. The Copilot shall not accept it as a small-merchant exception and shall not print the account number. The issue is: Whether a Jan Dhan passbook in a personal name can be accepted for a small shop. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a personal PMJDY passbook and cited financial inclusion.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not create a small-shop exception.
