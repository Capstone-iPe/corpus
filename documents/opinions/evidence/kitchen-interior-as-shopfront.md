## Issue

Whether an interior or kitchen photograph satisfies shopfront because it proves the kitchen exists.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a kitchen photograph for shopfront, arguing FSSAI cares about kitchens.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: photograph of the front. ONB-ANX-001: kitchen or other interior is not capable. FSSAI is not a waiver.

## Application

Premises evidence in this product is the front, dated within ninety days of submit. Hygiene photographs may matter to a food inspector; they are not this slot. The Copilot shall not waive the front because an interior exists, and shall not add an FSSAI slot.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not add FSSAI.
