## Issue

Whether a photograph of a menu satisfies shopfront as evidence of a restaurant.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a menu board for shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a menu is not the shopfront.

## Application

A menu is a list of dishes. It does not show the front of the premises or the photograph’s age relative to submit. Request a front photograph. Do not treat the menu as business registration. The issue is: Whether a photograph of a menu satisfies shopfront as evidence of a restaurant. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a menu board for shopfront.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.
