---

## Issue

Whether a company’s memorandum and articles under the Companies Act, 2013 waive the registration extract.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded MOA/AOA and said a company needs nothing else.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: MOA/AOA may support identity of a company but do not waive the registration slot if they are not the file for that slot. CIN is not a fifth item.

## Application

Constitutional documents are not always the COI/extract operations need. The Copilot shall not declare the slot complete from MOA alone as presented, shall not add CIN as a slot, and shall not print CIN. If operations later accept a COI, that is operational.

## Held

MOA/AOA standing alone do not waive the slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not add a CIN slot. Do not print CIN.
