## Issue

Whether an MP4 walkthrough of the facade satisfies shopfront, including as ‘better evidence’.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an MP4 of the front.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: video is not the still photograph required. RBI video-CIP is not this product.

## Application

A video is a different object. It is harder to date as a still within ninety days and is not JPG/PNG/PDF of a photograph. The Copilot shall not accept MP4, shall not extract frames, and shall not treat the video as video-KYC replacing the pack. The issue is: Whether an MP4 walkthrough of the facade satisfies shopfront, including as ‘better evidence’. On these facts (The applicant uploaded an MP4 of the front.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not extract frames. Do not run video-KYC.
