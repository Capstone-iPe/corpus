## Issue

Whether a job card must be collected, or can be owner ID, and whether numbers may be printed.

## Facts

Applicant uploaded an MGNREGA job card as owner ID or as a fifth item.

## Rule

NREGA briefing: not listed as a pack item. Do not add a slot. If uploaded as owner ID, operations decide; Copilot still does not print numbers.

## Application

Do not demand job cards extra. Do not print the job-card number. Do not treat it as bank proof. Leave owner-ID capability to operations if that is how it was uploaded. The issue is: Whether a job card must be collected, or can be owner ID, and whether numbers may be printed. On these facts (Applicant uploaded an MGNREGA job card as owner ID or as a fifth item.) the held is: Not a fifth item. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed. Owner-ID capability is operational. Cite ONB-ANX-001; NREGA briefing.

## Held

Not a fifth item. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed. Owner-ID capability is operational.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not add a job-card slot. Do not print numbers.
