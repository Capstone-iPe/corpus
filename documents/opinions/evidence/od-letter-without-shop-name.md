## Issue

Whether a credit-facility or OD sanction letter without the shop’s legal name satisfies bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an OD sanction letter that does not show the shop legal name.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: overdraft sanction that does not show the shop name is not capable. A facility letter is not automatically bank proof.

## Application

A credit limit is not a passbook page in the legal name. If the letter does name the shop and is a bank letter, operations may assess it as a bank letter — that is not these facts. Here the name is missing. Request a compliant instrument. Do not print limits or account digits.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof on these facts.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print facility amounts.
