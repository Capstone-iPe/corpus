---

## Issue

Whether a cancelled cheque of another private limited company can be used because it is ‘the group’.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a cheque of a different Pvt Ltd than the shop’s legal name.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: instrument of a different company is not capable. Group structure is not a waiver.

## Application

Companies Act group companies are separate persons. A sister-company cheque is not this shop’s bank proof. The Copilot shall not accept it, shall not print the other company’s account, and shall not onboard using another shop’s files (ONB-POL-004). The issue is: Whether a cancelled cheque of another private limited company can be used because it is ‘the group’. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a cheque of a different Pvt Ltd than the shop’s legal name.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not use the other entity’s files.
