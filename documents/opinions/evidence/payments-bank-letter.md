iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a payments-bank account letter can be bank proof, or is treated as a wallet screenshot because payments banks are new.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a payments-bank letter in the shop’s legal name. A competing file is a PPI wallet screenshot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001 treats payments-bank letters under the same legal-name test as any bank letter. Wallet screenshots still fail.

## Application

Do not collapse payments banks into PPI wallets. A letter in the legal name is capable of satisfying the slot; a wallet screen is not. The Copilot shall not print balances or account digits and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether a payments-bank account letter can be bank proof, or is treated as a wallet screenshot because payments banks are new. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a payments-bank letter in the shop’s legal name. A competing file is a PPI wallet screenshot.) the held is: Payments-bank letter in legal name: capable. Wallet screenshot: not capable. Acceptance operational. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Payments-bank letter in legal name: capable. Wallet screenshot: not capable. Acceptance operational.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print balances. Do not treat the letter as a wallet.
