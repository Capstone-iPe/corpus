## Issue

Whether a passbook that shows the owner’s personal name, not the shop’s legal name, can be accepted because the owner is the proprietor.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a personal-name passbook. The shop legal name differs.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: bank proof in the shop’s legal name. ONB-ANX-001: personal-name passbook or cheque fails. A mismatch is a failed slot, not a prompt to rewrite the shop name.

## Application

Proprietorship folklore that ‘owner equals shop’ does not rewrite the slot. If the instrument does not show the legal name collected on the application, it fails. The Copilot shall not suggest changing the shop name to match the passbook, shall not expand masked digits, and shall request an instrument in the legal name.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof. Do not rewrite the shop name.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not propose a name change. Do not print account digits.
