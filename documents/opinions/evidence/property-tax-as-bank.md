## Issue

Whether a local-body property-tax receipt can satisfy bank proof as official evidence of the shop.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a property-tax receipt to the bank-proof slot.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: property-tax receipts are not passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter.

## Application

Tax on land or building is not evidence of a bank account. It may interest a reviewer as occupancy colour; it does not clear the slot. The Copilot shall not waive the slot because the tax receipt is ‘government’, and shall not print the property identification number.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print property IDs.
