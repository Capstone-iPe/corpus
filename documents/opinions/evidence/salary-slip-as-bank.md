## Issue

Whether a salary slip can satisfy bank proof as evidence of an account into which salary is credited.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a payslip for bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a payslip is not bank proof in the shop’s legal name.

## Application

A payslip is employment evidence, often in a personal name. It is not a passbook, cancelled cheque, or bank letter of the shop. Do not print salary or account digits. Request proper proof. The issue is: Whether a salary slip can satisfy bank proof as evidence of an account into which salary is credited. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a payslip for bank proof.) the held is: Does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print salary or account digits.
