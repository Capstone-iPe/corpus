## Issue

Whether a photograph of a rubber stamp is the owner’s government identity document.

## Facts

The applicant photographed the shop stamp for owner ID.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a shop stamp is not the owner’s government ID.

## Application

A stamp identifies, at most, a name impression. It is not an OVD of a natural person. The Copilot shall not accept it for owner ID or for registration, and shall request the owner’s government ID. The issue is: Whether a photograph of a rubber stamp is the owner’s government identity document. On these facts (The applicant photographed the shop stamp for owner ID.) the held is: Does not satisfy owner ID. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy owner ID.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Request the government ID file.
