## Issue

Whether a registration under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 satisfies the business-registration slot.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a trade-mark certificate for registration.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: a trademark certificate is not capable of satisfying business registration standing alone.

## Application

A trade mark is a badge of origin. It is not incorporation or shop registration as a business. The Copilot shall not accept it as the slot, shall not print application numbers, and shall request the business registration file. The issue is: Whether a registration under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 satisfies the business-registration slot. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a trade-mark certificate for registration.) the held is: Does not satisfy business registration. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy business registration.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001. Do not print TM numbers.
