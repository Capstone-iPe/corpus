## Issue

Whether an Udyam certificate under the MSMED framework adds a slot or waives bank proof or registration.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded Udyam and asked to skip bank proof as a small enterprise.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: Udyam offered as a fifth item does not replace registration unless it is in fact the registration file for that slot. ONB-POL-002 §6: volume/size is not a ground to waive a pack item.

## Application

Udyam may identify an MSME. It is not a cancelled cheque in the legal name. It is not automatically the registration extract. Simplified-CDD talk does not drop slots (the simplified-CDD draft is not in force). Explain that bank proof remains, do not add a fifth slot, and do not print the Udyam number unless it is unavoidable in a citation — prefer not to print it (ONB-POL-004 spirit).

## Held

Not a fifth slot. Does not waive bank proof. Registration only if it is actually that file.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-002 §6. Do not drop bank proof.
