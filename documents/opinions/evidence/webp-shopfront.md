## Issue

Whether a WebP image from a messaging app satisfies shopfront.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a WebP of the front.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: WebP fails format.

## Application

Messaging-app default formats are not the pack list. Request JPG, PNG or PDF. Do not fetch the image from the chat thumbnail as a workaround. The issue is: Whether a WebP image from a messaging app satisfies shopfront. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a WebP of the front.) the held is: Fails format. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Fails format.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Request an accepted format.
