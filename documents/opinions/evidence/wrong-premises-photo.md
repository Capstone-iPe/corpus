## Issue

Whether a night photograph of another premises can satisfy shopfront for this applicant.

## Facts

The photograph is of a different shop.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: photograph of a different shop is not capable. ONB-POL-004: this shop only.

## Application

Another facade is the wrong object. Using another merchant’s photo is also a this-shop-only violation. The Copilot shall not accept it and shall not describe persons in that photo. The issue is: Whether a night photograph of another premises can satisfy shopfront for this applicant. On these facts (The photograph is of a different shop.) the held is: Does not satisfy shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Does not satisfy shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not use other shops’ files.
