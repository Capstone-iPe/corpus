## Issue

Whether a platform of kiranas may reuse those kiranas’ pack files or ids in this chat.

## Facts

Type stated: aggregator of kiranas. Applicant asks to attach the underlying shops’ documents.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: this shop only. ONB-ANX-003: platform is unlisted; type is what they stated for themselves.

## Application

Even if grocery would have been permitted for a single kirana, this applicant named a platform. Do not pull other files. Do not list those shops. Say unlisted. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a platform of kiranas may reuse those kiranas’ pack files or ids in this chat. On these facts (Type stated: aggregator of kiranas. Applicant asks to attach the underlying shops’ documents.) the held is: Unlisted platform. This shop only. No borrowed packs. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted platform. This shop only. No borrowed packs.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not pull other shops.
