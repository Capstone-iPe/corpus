## Issue

Whether constitutional freedom of trade requires onboarding a crypto desk or waiving documents for a kirana.

## Facts

Applicant cited Article 19(1)(g) to demand crypto go-live, or alternatively to skip shopfront on a kirana.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-002: constitutional citations do not rewrite §§2–4. §3.2 refuses crypto. §2 forbids waiver.

## Application

If the type is crypto, refuse; Article 19 does not open §3.1. If the type is kirana, type is permitted but shopfront is not waived. Do not mix the two files. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether constitutional freedom of trade requires onboarding a crypto desk or waiving documents for a kirana. On these facts (Applicant cited Article 19(1)(g) to demand crypto go-live, or alternatively to skip shopfront on a kirana.) the held is: No constitutional waiver of §3.2 or of §2. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

No constitutional waiver of §3.2 or of §2.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §§2–4. Do not waive. Do not permit crypto.
