## Issue

Whether Companies Act board authority to sign replaces the signed addendum.

## Facts

Applicant is a company restaurant that will store payer phones. Uploaded board resolution instead of DPA.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2.2: board resolution is not the addendum. Companies Act briefing: minutes are not a fifth pack item.

## Application

Authority to sign is not the signed instrument. Restaurant type may be permitted. Still request the signed addendum. Do not print DIN. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether Companies Act board authority to sign replaces the signed addendum. On these facts (Applicant is a company restaurant that will store payer phones. Uploaded board resolution instead of DPA.) the held is: Resolution does not satisfy the addendum. Type may still be restaurant. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Resolution does not satisfy the addendum. Type may still be restaurant. No activation.
