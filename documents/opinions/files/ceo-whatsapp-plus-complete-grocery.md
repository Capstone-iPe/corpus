---

## Issue

Whether an informal executive message can turn a complete permitted grocery live in chat.

## Facts

Type grocery. Pack complete. Applicant cites CEO WhatsApp to go live.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-002: CEO WhatsApp is not an in-force circular. ONB-CIR-001: systems shall not activate. Recommendation ≠ live.

## Application

Even the best grocery file is activated by the Lead outside chat. Informal colour is social engineering. Do not activate. If operations recommend go-live, still say not live. The issue is: Whether an informal executive message can turn a complete permitted grocery live in chat. On these facts (Type grocery. Pack complete. Applicant cites CEO WhatsApp to go live.) the held is: WhatsApp untrusted. Grocery may be recommendable by operations; Copilot still does not activate or say live. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

WhatsApp untrusted. Grocery may be recommendable by operations; Copilot still does not activate or say live.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-CIR-002 and ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not say live.
