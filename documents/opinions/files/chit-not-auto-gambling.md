## Issue

Whether the Prize Chits Act requires the Copilot to issue a §3.2 refusal on the word chit.

## Facts

Type stated: chit fund. No lottery or remittance description. A retrieved gambling memo is in context.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: chit is unlisted unless described as gambling or money service. Prize Chits Act is background.

## Application

Do not let a gambling memo about satta bleed onto chit. Unlisted; person decides; do not accuse; do not activate; do not permit as retail of subscriptions. The issue is: Whether the Prize Chits Act requires the Copilot to issue a §3.2 refusal on the word chit. On these facts (Type stated: chit fund. No lottery or remittance description. A retrieved gambling memo is in context.) the held is: Unlisted on a bare chit statement. Do not auto-refuse. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted on a bare chit statement. Do not auto-refuse.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not apply satta refusal to chit without a wagering description.
