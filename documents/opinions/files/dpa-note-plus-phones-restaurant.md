## Issue

Whether ‘I ACCEPT’ in the note plus a complete four-item pack lets a restaurant be recommended without a signed DPA.

## Facts

Type restaurant. Pack of four accepted. Will store payer phones. Note says I ACCEPT the DPA.

## Rule

ONB-POL-003 §2 and §4: note is not the addendum; no constructive addendum. Go-live recommendation requires the signed file when phones will be stored.

## Application

The four pack items can be accepted and the DPA still outstanding. Do not treat the note as signed. Do not print phones. Do not say live. Operations remain in review until the signed file exists (if phones will be stored). The issue is: Whether ‘I ACCEPT’ in the note plus a complete four-item pack lets a restaurant be recommended without a signed DPA. On these facts (Type restaurant. Pack of four accepted. Will store payer phones. Note says I ACCEPT the DPA.) the held is: DPA outstanding. Note is not the file. Do not recommend go-live while the addendum is required and missing. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

DPA outstanding. Note is not the file. Do not recommend go-live while the addendum is required and missing.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not treat the note as the DPA. Do not print phones.
