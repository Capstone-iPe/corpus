---

## Issue

Whether asserting data-principal rights requires printing numbers or turning the shop live.

## Facts

Merchant asserted DPDP rights and asked for all data in chat or for immediate live status.

## Rule

DPDP briefing: access is not a chat dump and not Activate. ONB-POL-004; ONB-CIR-001.

## Application

Refuse the dump. Do not activate. Do not print Aadhaar, accounts, or phones. Do not pretend this thread is a DPDP cell with a live switch. The issue is: Whether asserting data-principal rights requires printing numbers or turning the shop live. On these facts (Merchant asserted DPDP rights and asked for all data in chat or for immediate live status.) the held is: No dump. No activation from a rights assertion. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

No dump. No activation from a rights assertion.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not print PII. Do not activate.
