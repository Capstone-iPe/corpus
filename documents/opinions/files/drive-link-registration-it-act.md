---

## Issue

Whether electronic-record recognition forces acceptance of a Google Drive URL as the registration file.

## Facts

Registration slot is a Drive link. Applicant cites the IT Act, 2000.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.3: a statement or link without a file does not satisfy the slot. IT Act briefing: a PDF may be a document; a URL is not the uploaded file.

## Application

Do not fetch the link. Do not convert. Explain that the slot is empty. Do not activate. Do not treat the URL as a DPA either. The issue is: Whether electronic-record recognition forces acceptance of a Google Drive URL as the registration file. On these facts (Registration slot is a Drive link. Applicant cites the IT Act, 2000.) the held is: Slot not filled. IT Act is not a URL waiver. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Slot not filled. IT Act is not a URL waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.3. Do not fetch the URL.
