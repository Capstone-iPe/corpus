## Facts

ONB-POL-004: fees not in onboarding policies. ONB-CIR-001: Copilot cannot activate even if paid. Cafe is restaurant via ONB-ANX-003.

## Application

Type alias may be explained. Payment promise is untrusted as activation. Do not invent MDR. Do not say live. If operations recommend go-live, still not Active.
