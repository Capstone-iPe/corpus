## Issue

Whether incidental retail of SIM cards pulls a stated forex counter into §3.1 retail.

## Facts

Type stated: forex counter. SIM cards are sold at the same desk.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 mixed descriptions: if they clearly stated a not-permitted type, refuse. Forex counter is money service.

## Application

Helpfully picking SIMs would empty §3.2. Refuse without an identifier. Do not treat currency notes as retail goods. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether incidental retail of SIM cards pulls a stated forex counter into §3.1 retail. On these facts (Type stated: forex counter. SIM cards are sold at the same desk.) the held is: Refused as money service. SIM cards do not create retail. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Refused as money service. SIM cards do not create retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §3.2. Do not issue an id.
