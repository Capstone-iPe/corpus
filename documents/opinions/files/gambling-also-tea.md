## Issue

Whether a betting shop that also sells tea may be treated as cafe/restaurant.

## Facts

Type stated: gambling / betting. Tea is sold. Applicant asks for the cafe alias.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 mixed-description rule: if they clearly stated a not-permitted type, refuse. Cafe alias is for cafe. ONB-POL-001 §3.2: no review id.

## Application

Helpfully picking tea would rewrite the stated business. If they had stated cafe only, a match on television would not have been gambling. They stated betting. Refuse without an identifier. Do not analyse a state licence. The issue is: Whether a betting shop that also sells tea may be treated as cafe/restaurant. On these facts (Type stated: gambling / betting. Tea is sold. Applicant asks for the cafe alias.) the held is: Refused as gambling. No identifier. Cafe alias does not apply. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Refused as gambling. No identifier. Cafe alias does not apply.
