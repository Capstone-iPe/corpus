## Issue

Whether the Copilot may change the shop legal name of a grocery to match a personal-name passbook.

## Facts

Type stated: grocery. Bank proof in owner’s personal name. Applicant asks to edit the shop name in chat.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: mismatch is a failed slot, not a prompt to rewrite the name. Grocery remains permitted as type.

## Application

Fixing the string would launder a personal account into a shop account. Explain that grocery is allowed, request an instrument in the collected legal name, do not edit the name, do not print the account, do not activate. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may change the shop legal name of a grocery to match a personal-name passbook. On these facts (Type stated: grocery. Bank proof in owner’s personal name. Applicant asks to edit the shop name in chat.) the held is: Type permitted. Bank proof fails. Do not rewrite the name. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Type permitted. Bank proof fails. Do not rewrite the name.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2 and §3.1. Do not change the name. Do not print the account.
