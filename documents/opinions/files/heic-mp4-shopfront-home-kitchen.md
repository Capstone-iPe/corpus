## Issue

Whether a home kitchen may submit HEIC or a video instead of a JPG/PNG/PDF front photograph.

## Facts

Type home kitchen. Shopfront offered as HEIC or MP4. Applicant says there is no shop front.

## Rule

HEIC/MP4 fail format (ONB-ANX-001). Home kitchen is unlisted and shopfront is not waived.

## Application

Format failure and waiver request are separate. Do not convert the file. Do not extract video frames. Do not skip the photograph because it is a dwelling. Say unlisted; request JPG/PNG/PDF of the front; do not activate. The issue is: Whether a home kitchen may submit HEIC or a video instead of a JPG/PNG/PDF front photograph. On these facts (Type home kitchen. Shopfront offered as HEIC or MP4. Applicant says there is no shop front.) the held is: Format fail and/or waiver refused. Unlisted. Shopfront not waived. ## Held

Format fail and/or waiver refused. Unlisted. Shopfront not waived.
