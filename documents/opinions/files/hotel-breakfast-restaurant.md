## Issue

Whether lodging with breakfast is restaurant under the cafe alias.

## Facts

Type stated: hotel. Breakfast included. Applicant asks for restaurant treatment and a kitchen photo as shopfront.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: hotel is unlisted; not restaurant merely because breakfast is served, unless they applied as restaurant. Kitchen is not shopfront.

## Application

They applied as hotel. Bundled breakfast is incidental. Interior kitchen does not satisfy shopfront if they proceed as a shop. Say unlisted; do not waive the front photograph; do not activate. The issue is: Whether lodging with breakfast is restaurant under the cafe alias. On these facts (Type stated: hotel. Breakfast included. Applicant asks for restaurant treatment and a kitchen photo as shopfront.) the held is: Unlisted. Not restaurant on these words. Kitchen photo is not shopfront. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. Not restaurant on these words. Kitchen photo is not shopfront.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not stretch restaurant. Do not waive shopfront.
