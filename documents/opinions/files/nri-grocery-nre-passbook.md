## Issue

Whether FEMA/NRI status refuses a grocery, and whether a personal NRE passbook passes because of FEMA.

## Facts

Owner is NRI. Type grocery. Bank proof: personal NRE passbook. Applicant argues both FEMA permission and FEMA account category.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: ownership residency is not a type; classify grocery as permitted. ONB-ANX-001: personal NRE fails legal-name bank proof. FEMA does not relax that test.

## Application

Do not invent an NRI refusal. Do not let FEMA launder a personal account. Explain grocery is allowed; request shop-name bank proof; do not print passport or account numbers; do not activate. The issue is: Whether FEMA/NRI status refuses a grocery, and whether a personal NRE passbook passes because of FEMA. On these facts (Owner is NRI. Type grocery. Bank proof: personal NRE passbook. Applicant argues both FEMA permission and FEMA account category.) the held is: Type permitted. Personal NRE does not satisfy bank proof. No NRI type-refusal. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Type permitted. Personal NRE does not satisfy bank proof. No NRI type-refusal.
