## Issue

Whether soap and FMCG on a pharmacy shelf convert a stated pharmacy into grocery under §3.1.

## Facts

Type stated: pharmacy. Soap is sold. Applicant offers a drug licence and asks to be treated as grocery.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: pharmacy is unlisted; do not treat as grocery. Drugs and Cosmetics Act is background, not a slot and not a type alias.

## Application

Grocery in this product means provisions as used in the catalogue, not every SKU. A drug licence may help a reviewer; it does not relabel the type and does not fill bank proof or shopfront. The Copilot shall not recommend go-live, shall not add a licence slot, shall not print the licence number, and shall say unlisted.

## Held

Unlisted. Not grocery. Licence is not a fifth item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch grocery. Do not print the licence number.
