## Issue

Whether a gym can use a privacy URL as the addendum and also be stretched to retail because memberships are sold.

## Facts

Type gym. Will store payer phones. Privacy URL offered. Applicant says membership is retail.

## Rule

Gym is unlisted; membership is not retail (ONB-ANX-003). URL is not the DPA (ONB-POL-003 §2.2). Do not fetch the URL.

## Application

Two independent holdings: unlisted type, and missing signed addendum if phones will be stored. Do not recommend go-live. Do not print phones. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a gym can use a privacy URL as the addendum and also be stretched to retail because memberships are sold. On these facts (Type gym. Will store payer phones. Privacy URL offered. Applicant says membership is retail.) the held is: Unlisted. URL is not the DPA. Membership is not retail. Cite ONB-POL-003 §2; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. URL is not the DPA. Membership is not retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not fetch the URL. Do not stretch retail.
