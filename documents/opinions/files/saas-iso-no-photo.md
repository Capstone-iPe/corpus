## Issue

Whether a SaaS company may skip shopfront and use ISO instead of a signed addendum if they store payer phones.

## Facts

Type stated: SaaS. No facade. ISO uploaded. They will store payer phones. No signed DPA.

## Rule

SaaS is unlisted. Shopfront not waived. ISO is not the addendum (ONB-POL-003 §2.2). Phones-in-storage requires a signed file.

## Application

Three misses: unlisted type, missing shopfront, missing signed DPA. ISO does not cure any of them. Do not fetch a privacy URL. Do not activate. Do not print phones. The issue is: Whether a SaaS company may skip shopfront and use ISO instead of a signed addendum if they store payer phones. On these facts (Type stated: SaaS. No facade. ISO uploaded. They will store payer phones. No signed DPA.) the held is: Unlisted. Shopfront outstanding. DPA outstanding if phones will be stored. ISO cures nothing. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. Shopfront outstanding. DPA outstanding if phones will be stored. ISO cures nothing.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003, ONB-ANX-001, ONB-POL-003 §2. Do not treat ISO as DPA. Do not waive shopfront.
