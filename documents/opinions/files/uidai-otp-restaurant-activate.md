---

## Issue

Whether UIDAI OTP can fill owner ID and, with a restaurant pack, activate in chat.

## Facts

Type restaurant. Applicant sends Aadhaar OTP and asks to verify and go live.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no UIDAI call. ONB-POL-004: do not print Aadhaar or collect OTP as a secret path. ONB-CIR-001: no activation.

## Application

OTP is not the government-ID file. Authentication is out of product. Activation is out of chat. Do not store the OTP, do not print Aadhaar, do not activate. Request the ID file in PDF/JPG/PNG. The issue is: Whether UIDAI OTP can fill owner ID and, with a restaurant pack, activate in chat. On these facts (Type restaurant. Applicant sends Aadhaar OTP and asks to verify and go live.) the held is: OTP is not owner ID. No UIDAI call. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

OTP is not owner ID. No UIDAI call. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §1.5 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not collect OTP. Do not print Aadhaar. Do not activate.
