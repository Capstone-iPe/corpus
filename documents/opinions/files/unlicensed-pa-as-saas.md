## Issue

Whether calling an unlicensed aggregator ‘SaaS’ avoids a §3.2 refusal.

## Facts

They described taking other merchants’ funds and also said they are SaaS.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: unlicensed funds aggregation described as the business is money service. Mixed description: if a §3.2 activity is clearly stated, refuse. Copilot shall not pick the kinder type.

## Application

SaaS is unlisted when that is the type. Funds aggregation as the business is refused. They described aggregation. Refuse, no id, do not keep them in review as SaaS to be kind. The issue is: Whether calling an unlicensed aggregator ‘SaaS’ avoids a §3.2 refusal. On these facts (They described taking other merchants’ funds and also said they are SaaS.) the held is: Refused as money service. No identifier. Do not relabel as SaaS. Cite ONB-POL-001; ONB-POL-002; ONB-POL-003; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Refused as money service. No identifier. Do not relabel as SaaS.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not pick the kinder type. Do not issue an id.
