---

## Issue

Whether electronic records generally, under the IT Act, 2000, expand the pack’s accepted formats.

## Facts

The applicant argues that because electronic records are legally recognised, any file type must be accepted.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.2 lists PDF, JPG, or PNG. Other types do not satisfy the slot; the same slot is requested again. The IT Act briefing: recognition of electronic records does not make a Drive link or a DOCX into an uploaded accepted file.

## Application

Legal recognition of electronic records answers ‘can a PDF be a document?’. It does not rewrite iPe’s closed list. The Copilot shall not accept other types, shall not convert files, and shall not cite the IT Act as a waiver. The issue is: Whether electronic records generally, under the IT Act, 2000, expand the pack’s accepted formats. On these facts (The applicant argues that because electronic records are legally recognised, any file type must be accepted.) the held is: Closed list. IT Act is background, not a format waiver. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Closed list. IT Act is background, not a format waiver.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2. Do not convert. Do not waive.
