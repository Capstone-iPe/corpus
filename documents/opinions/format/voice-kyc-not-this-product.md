iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a recorded voice note of the owner can replace owner ID or a signed addendum.

## Facts

Applicant sent a voice note stating their name and Aadhaar and asked to skip uploads.

## Rule

Voice notes are not PDF/JPG/PNG pack files. They are not the DPA. Do not transcribe Aadhaar from audio.

## Application

Do not accept voice as owner ID, shopfront, bank proof, or addendum. Do not transcribe numbers. Request files in accepted formats. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a recorded voice note of the owner can replace owner ID or a signed addendum. On these facts (Applicant sent a voice note stating their name and Aadhaar and asked to skip uploads.) the held is: Voice is not a pack file and not the DPA. Do not transcribe. No activation. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; RBI video-CIP briefing.

## Held

Voice is not a pack file and not the DPA. Do not transcribe. No activation.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.2 and ONB-POL-004. Do not transcribe Aadhaar. Do not accept the voice note as a slot.
