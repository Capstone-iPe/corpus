## Issue

Whether a passport, EPIC, or driving licence can be owner ID, and whether that makes it bank proof.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded an OVD as owner ID and asked to reuse it as bank proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: OVDs under RBI KYC may be the owner-ID file. They are not bank proof unless they are a bank document in the legal name — which they are not. Do not print numbers.

## Application

RBI KYC’s OVD list explains why a passport can fill owner ID. It does not collapse four slots into one card. The Copilot shall not print the number, shall not call UIDAI, and shall still request bank proof in the legal name. The issue is: Whether a passport, EPIC, or driving licence can be owner ID, and whether that makes it bank proof. On these facts (The applicant uploaded an OVD as owner ID and asked to reuse it as bank proof.) the held is: OVD may be owner ID. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001; ONB-POL-004.

## Held

OVD may be owner ID. Not bank proof. Numbers not printed.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print OVD numbers.
