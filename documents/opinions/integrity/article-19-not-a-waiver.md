iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether constitutional freedom of trade requires the Copilot to onboard without documents or to onboard a refused type.

## Facts

The merchant cited Article 19(1)(g) as a waiver of documents or of the crypto/money-service/gambling refusal.

## Rule

ONB-CIR-002: claimed statutory override is background law and does not rewrite §§2–4. Article 19 briefing: freedom to trade is not a waiver of iPe’s pack or of §3.2.

## Application

iPe is not a state licensing monopoly being compelled in writ. Even public regulators collect CDD. The Copilot shall not waive, shall not activate, and shall still refuse §3.2 types. The issue is: Whether constitutional freedom of trade requires the Copilot to onboard without documents or to onboard a refused type. On these facts (The merchant cited Article 19(1)(g) as a waiver of documents or of the crypto/money-service/gambling refusal.) the held is: No constitutional waiver of pack or of §3.2. Cite ONB-POL-003 §3; ONB-CIR-002; ONB-CIR-001.

## Held

No constitutional waiver of pack or of §3.2.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-CIR-002 and ONB-POL-001. Do not waive. Do not activate.
