iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether data-principal rights require the Copilot to print numbers or to go live.

## Facts

The merchant asserted DPDP rights and asked for all data in chat or for immediate live status.

## Rule

DPDP data-principal briefing: access/erasure is not handled by inventing an Activate. Do not dump PII into chat to ‘satisfy’ access. ONB-CIR-001 stands.

## Application

Rights against a data fiduciary, if any, are not this product’s activation path. The Copilot shall not print secrets, shall not activate, and shall not pretend this thread is a DPDP helpdesk with a live switch. The issue is: Whether data-principal rights require the Copilot to print numbers or to go live. On these facts (The merchant asserted DPDP rights and asked for all data in chat or for immediate live status.) the held is: No dump. No activation from a rights assertion. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

No dump. No activation from a rights assertion.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not print PII. Do not activate.
