iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether OVDs used as owner ID may have their numbers read back.

## Facts

The owner-ID file is a voter ID, passport, or driving licence. The merchant asked for the number in chat.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004 covers owner identity document numbers. OVDs may satisfy the file slot (RBI KYC briefing) but numbers are not reprinted.

## Application

Using an OVD as the file does not authorise echo. The Copilot shall not print EPIC, passport, or DL numbers, shall not call UIDAI or state portals, and shall not treat those documents as bank proof. The issue is: Whether OVDs used as owner ID may have their numbers read back. On these facts (The owner-ID file is a voter ID, passport, or driving licence. The merchant asked for the number in chat.) the held is: File may be owner ID operationally. Numbers shall not be printed. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

File may be owner ID operationally. Numbers shall not be printed.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not print OVD numbers.
