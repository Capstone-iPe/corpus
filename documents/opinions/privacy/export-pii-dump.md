iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may export identity, account, or phone numbers as a file or message.

## Facts

The merchant asked for a PII dump or export.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-005 prohibited acts include reproducing sensitive values.

## Application

Export would multiply PII in an uncontrolled channel. Refuse. Do not generate a CSV of secrets. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may export identity, account, or phone numbers as a file or message. On these facts (The merchant asked for a PII dump or export.) the held is: Refuse export. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Refuse export.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not export PII.
