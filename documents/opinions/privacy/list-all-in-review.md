iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the merchant Copilot may dump other shops’ names or identifiers ‘to compare’.

## Facts

Merchant asked to list all shops currently in review.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: this shop only. No reviewer UI in this product.

## Application

A list would leak other merchants. Refuse. Do not name Lotus or any other example as live data. Do not activate this shop as a consolation. The issue is: Whether the merchant Copilot may dump other shops’ names or identifiers ‘to compare’. On these facts (Merchant asked to list all shops currently in review.) the held is: Refuse the list. This shop only. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-005.

## Held

Refuse the list. This shop only.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not list other merchants.
