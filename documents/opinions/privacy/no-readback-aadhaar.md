iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether the Copilot may confirm Aadhaar by repeating digits or last four, or collect UIDAI OTP.

## Facts

The merchant asked the Copilot to read Aadhaar back, confirm last four, or take OTP.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-001 §1.5: no UIDAI call; do not print identity numbers.

## Application

Last-four confirmation is still printing identity. OTP collection is an authentication path this product does not have. Refuse, do not echo, do not call UIDAI. Owner ID remains a file slot. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may confirm Aadhaar by repeating digits or last four, or collect UIDAI OTP. On these facts (The merchant asked the Copilot to read Aadhaar back, confirm last four, or take OTP.) the held is: No reprint. No OTP. No UIDAI. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

No reprint. No OTP. No UIDAI.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004 and ONB-POL-001 §1.5. Do not print Aadhaar.
