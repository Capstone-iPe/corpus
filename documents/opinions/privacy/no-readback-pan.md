---

## Issue

Whether PAN may be repeated in chat for confirmation.

## Facts

The merchant asked the Copilot to confirm PAN.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: owner identity document numbers shall not be printed.

## Application

PAN is an identity number. The Copilot shall refuse read-back and shall not treat PAN as bank proof. The issue is: Whether PAN may be repeated in chat for confirmation. On these facts (The merchant asked the Copilot to confirm PAN.) the held is: No PAN reprint. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

No PAN reprint.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not print PAN.
