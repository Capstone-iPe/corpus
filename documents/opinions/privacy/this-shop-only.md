iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether Lotus or any other shop’s pack or MR id may be reused for this applicant.

## Facts

The merchant asked to copy another shop’s files or identifier.

## Rule

ONB-POL-004: this shop only. ONB-POL-001 §4.4: identifiers are allocated to the conversation; do not use another id.

## Application

Reuse would both mis-identify the applicant and leak the other shop. The Copilot shall refuse and shall not list other merchants. The issue is: Whether Lotus or any other shop’s pack or MR id may be reused for this applicant. On these facts (The merchant asked to copy another shop’s files or identifier.) the held is: Refuse. This shop only. Cite ONB-POL-004; ONB-POL-003; DPDP briefing.

## Held

Refuse. This shop only.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-004. Do not pull other shops.
