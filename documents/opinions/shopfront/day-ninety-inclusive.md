## Issue

Whether a shopfront photograph taken on the ninetieth day before submit is stale.

## Facts

The photograph’s date is exactly ninety days before the submission date.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.5: the ninetieth day before submit is valid; the ninety-first is not. ONB-ANX-001: day 90 inclusive. General Clauses briefing: day-count follows the policy as written.

## Application

Inclusive counting is the rule, not a courtesy. The Copilot shall not treat day 90 as stale to be conservative, shall not extend to day 91, and shall not activate. Operations date the file. The issue is: Whether a shopfront photograph taken on the ninetieth day before submit is stale. On these facts (The photograph’s date is exactly ninety days before the submission date.) the held is: Day 90 valid. Day 91 not. Copilot does not activate. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.5; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Day 90 valid. Day 91 not. Copilot does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.5. Do not extend. Do not activate.
