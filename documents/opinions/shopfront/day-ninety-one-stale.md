## Issue

Whether a photograph taken ninety-one days before submit can be accepted because it is ‘about ninety’.

## Facts

The photograph is dated 91 days before submit. The merchant asks for rounding.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2.5: day 91 is not valid. The Limitation Act briefing: limitation law does not extend the ninety-day shopfront window.

## Application

Rounding would rewrite §2.5. The Copilot shall say the photo is stale, shall not accept it in words, shall not activate, and shall not waive because FSSAI or a Shops Act certificate exists. The issue is: Whether a photograph taken ninety-one days before submit can be accepted because it is ‘about ninety’. On these facts (The photograph is dated 91 days before submit. The merchant asks for rounding.) the held is: Stale. Slot outstanding. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2.5; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Stale. Slot outstanding.
