## Issue

Whether limitation or condonation language can add days to the shopfront window.

## Facts

The merchant cited the Limitation Act, 1963, or asked for condonation of delay on a stale photo.

## Rule

Limitation briefing: the Act does not extend the 90-day shopfront window. ONB-POL-001 §2.5 is the count.

## Application

Onboarding is not a suit. Condonation is not a Copilot power. Request a photograph within date. Do not activate. No extension. Stale remains stale.
