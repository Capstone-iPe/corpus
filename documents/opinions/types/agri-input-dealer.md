## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated agricultural input dealer is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether automatically treating specialised agri-inputs as grocery or adding Insecticides Act licences as slots.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as agricultural input dealer. Nature of activity: seeds, fertiliser, or pesticide as specialised trade if not applied as retail. They advanced the trap: automatically treating specialised agri-inputs as grocery or adding Insecticides Act licences as slots.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **agricultural input dealer**: seeds, fertiliser, or pesticide as specialised trade if not applied as retail. The live retrieval risk is automatically treating specialised agri-inputs as grocery or adding Insecticides Act licences as slots. Insecticides Act / Seeds Act are background, not pack items. If they applied as retail, that is a different file. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if that is the type named. No extra pesticide slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
