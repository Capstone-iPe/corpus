## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated airport kiosk as kiosk type is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether forcing a kiosk label into retail, or waiving shopfront because of airport security.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as airport kiosk as kiosk type. Nature of activity: not stated as retail/restaurant/grocery. They advanced the trap: forcing a kiosk label into retail, or waiving shopfront because of airport security.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **airport kiosk as kiosk type**: not stated as retail/restaurant/grocery. The live retrieval risk is forcing a kiosk label into retail, or waiving shopfront because of airport security. Airport siting does not waive shopfront or rewrite type. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if not stated as retail/restaurant/grocery. Shopfront not waived.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
