## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated blood bank is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a blood bank as a clinic-retail or adding a NACO/D&C licence slot.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as blood bank. Nature of activity: blood and components. They advanced the trap: treating a blood bank as a clinic-retail or adding a NACO/D&C licence slot.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **blood bank**: blood and components. The live retrieval risk is treating a blood bank as a clinic-retail or adding a NACO/D&C licence slot. Drugs and Cosmetics / NACO is background, not a fifth item. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. No extra blood-bank slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
