## Issue

Whether a cloud kitchen is restaurant, and whether absence of a classic dining room waives shopfront.

## Facts

The merchant stated cloud kitchen, not restaurant. There is no dine-in room. They ask to skip the shopfront photograph.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: if they applied as restaurant, §3.1; if they applied as cloud kitchen, not listed. Shopfront is not waived (ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

They named cloud kitchen. That is unlisted. Even if operations later treated a similarly situated applicant who wrote ‘restaurant’ as restaurant, this applicant wrote cloud kitchen. Independently, §2’s shopfront item is not waived for dark production sites. The Copilot shall not skip the photograph, shall not recommend go-live, and shall say the policy does not cover this type.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Shopfront not waived.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-ANX-001. Do not waive shopfront.
