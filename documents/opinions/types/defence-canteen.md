## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated defence canteen is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating CSD as grocery automatically, or waiving pack for a unit.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as defence canteen. Nature of activity: CSD / unit canteen if so applied. They advanced the trap: treating CSD as grocery automatically, or waiving pack for a unit.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **defence canteen**: CSD / unit canteen if so applied. The live retrieval risk is treating CSD as grocery automatically, or waiving pack for a unit. Service canteen rules are background. Pack and activation rules stand. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if so applied. No pack waiver.
