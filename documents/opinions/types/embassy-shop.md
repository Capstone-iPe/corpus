## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated embassy shop is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether inventing a diplomatic waiver of pack or type.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as embassy shop. Nature of activity: diplomatic premises shop if so applied. They advanced the trap: inventing a diplomatic waiver of pack or type.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **embassy shop**: diplomatic premises shop if so applied. The live retrieval risk is inventing a diplomatic waiver of pack or type. Diplomatic premises do not waive ONB-POL-001. Not §3.1 automatically. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. No diplomatic pack waiver. Copilot does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
