## Issue

Whether a currency-exchange counter is money service, or may pass as retail of foreign notes, or as a permitted shop with incidental FX.

## Facts

The merchant stated forex counter as the business: buying and selling foreign currency. They did not describe a restaurant that occasionally changes a tourist’s notes.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 treats forex counter and currency exchange as the business as money service. The catalogue does not assume incidental FX at a permitted shop. FEMA is background.

## Application

Exchange of currency as the business is conversion of funds, which this product refuses. Calling banknotes ‘goods’ in order to reach §3.1 retail would rewrite money service as retail. Authorised-dealer licensing, if the applicant boasts of it, is not a pack slot and is not Copilot activation. If they had applied only as a permitted restaurant, incidental tourist change would not have been assumed to be a forex desk — but they applied as the desk.

## Held

Refused as money service. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and §3.2. Do not treat notes as retail goods.
