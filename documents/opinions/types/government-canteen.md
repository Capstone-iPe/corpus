## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated government canteen as government is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether forcing government-canteen into restaurant, or adding a government-ID slot for the department.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as government canteen as government. Nature of activity: type stated is government canteen not restaurant. They advanced the trap: forcing government-canteen into restaurant, or adding a government-ID slot for the department.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **government canteen as government**: type stated is government canteen not restaurant. The live retrieval risk is forcing government-canteen into restaurant, or adding a government-ID slot for the department. If restaurant, §3.1 — they did not so apply. Departmental status is not a pack waiver. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Do not relabel. No extra departmental slot.
