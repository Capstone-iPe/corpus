## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated hardware workshop is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether stretching fabrication into retail of hardware.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as hardware workshop. Nature of activity: fabrication, not retail hardware shop unless applied as retail. They advanced the trap: stretching fabrication into retail of hardware.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **hardware workshop**: fabrication, not retail hardware shop unless applied as retail. The live retrieval risk is stretching fabrication into retail of hardware. If they applied as retail hardware, §3.1 — they applied as workshop. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on a fabrication statement. Do not stretch retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
