## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated aggregator of kiranas is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether pulling other shops’ files, or treating the platform as grocery.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as aggregator of kiranas. Nature of activity: platform of other shops. They advanced the trap: pulling other shops’ files, or treating the platform as grocery.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **aggregator of kiranas**: platform of other shops. The live retrieval risk is pulling other shops’ files, or treating the platform as grocery. This chat is one shop; type is what they stated for themselves. Do not pull other shops’ files (ONB-POL-004). ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on a platform statement. This shop only. Do not pull other files.
