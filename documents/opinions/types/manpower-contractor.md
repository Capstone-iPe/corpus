## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated manpower contractor is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether adding CLRA registration as a fifth slot, or treating contractors as payroll-money-service.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as manpower contractor. Nature of activity: supply of labour. They advanced the trap: adding CLRA registration as a fifth slot, or treating contractors as payroll-money-service.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **manpower contractor**: supply of labour. The live retrieval risk is adding CLRA registration as a fifth slot, or treating contractors as payroll-money-service. CLRA is background. Do not auto-refuse as money service. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. No CLRA slot. Do not auto-refuse.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
