## Issue

Whether a factory is retail because goods are made and sold, or unlisted manufacturing.

## Facts

The merchant stated manufacturing unit. A factory gate exists. Some goods are sold at the gate.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: manufacturing is not retail at a shop. Factories Act is background. Not a pack waiver.

## Application

Making goods is not the shop-type in §3.1 unless they applied as retail. Gate sales do not automatically convert the stated type. The Copilot shall not demand a factory licence as a fifth file, shall not waive shopfront of the unit’s front, and shall say unlisted.

## Held

Unlisted where applied as manufacturing. In review.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not add a factory-licence slot.
