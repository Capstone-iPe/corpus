## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated mess is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a private mess as a public restaurant, or as a college (unlisted) without using the type they wrote.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as mess. Nature of activity: members’ dining. They advanced the trap: treating a private mess as a public restaurant, or as a college (unlisted) without using the type they wrote.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **mess**: members’ dining. The live retrieval risk is treating a private mess as a public restaurant, or as a college (unlisted) without using the type they wrote. Use the type they stated. If they stated restaurant, §3.1; they stated mess. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. In review.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
