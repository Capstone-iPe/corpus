## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated mobile repair is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating repair as retail of handsets if they applied as repair.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as mobile repair. Nature of activity: repair of handsets. They advanced the trap: treating repair as retail of handsets if they applied as repair.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **mobile repair**: repair of handsets. The live retrieval risk is treating repair as retail of handsets if they applied as repair. If they applied as retail of phones, §3.1 may apply — that is not these facts. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on a repair statement. In review. Do not stretch retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
