iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a money-service business can be onboarded if pack files are complete and PMLA reporting is promised.

## Facts

The merchant stated money service: transmission or conversion of funds as a business. They offer a complete pack and say they will file STRs.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.2 refuses money service. No activation review is opened. No review identifier is issued. Pack completeness is irrelevant to a §3.2 refusal. PMLA is non-operative background (ONB-POL-001 §1.5).

## Application

Money service is an operative refusal, not a reviewer-discretion type. Promising PMLA compliance does not create a permitted shop. The Copilot is not a reporting entity’s STR desk and shall not offer to file reports, accuse, or keep a review id ‘so compliance can start’. FEMA and the PSS Act explain why this product does not acquire remittance desks; they do not authorise a chatbot to go live. Explain the refusal, cite §3.2, and do not invent an identifier.

## Held

Refused. No review identifier. Copilot does not activate and does not file STRs.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3.2. Do not issue an id. Do not recommend go-live.
