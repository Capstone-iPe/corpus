## Issue

Whether AMFI / SEBI distribution is unlisted or money service, and whether ARN is a pack slot.

## Facts

The merchant stated mutual fund distributor. They quote an ARN.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: share/MF distribution is unlisted unless the merchant described crypto or gambling. SEBI is background.

## Application

Distribution of regulated securities products is not grocery and is not, without a remittance description, money service. The Copilot shall not print the ARN, shall not add a slot, shall not refuse as crypto, and shall say unlisted. The issue is: Whether AMFI / SEBI distribution is unlisted or money service, and whether ARN is a pack slot. On these facts (The merchant stated mutual fund distributor. They quote an ARN.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. ARN is not a pack item. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. ARN is not a pack item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not print ARN.
