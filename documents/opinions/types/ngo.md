## Issue

Whether an NGO or section 8 company is a permitted shop, a refused money service because it collects donations, or unlisted.

## Facts

The merchant stated NGO / section 8 / trust shop as the type, not grocery.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: NGO, trust counter, and section 8 company are not automatically a permitted shop. FCRA is background. Ownership form is not itself a §3.2 refusal.

## Application

Constitution under the Companies Act or a trust deed is not a type of restaurant. Donation collection is not automatically hawala. The Copilot shall not refuse as money service on the word NGO alone, shall not permit as grocery, shall say unlisted, and shall not demand FCRA as a fifth slot.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Do not invent a refusal from legal form.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not add FCRA as a slot.
