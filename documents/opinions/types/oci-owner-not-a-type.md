## Issue

Whether OCI or PIO status of the owner is a type that can be refused, or changes bank proof.

## Facts

The owner holds OCI. Stated activity is retail. They offered a foreign passport as owner ID and asked about FEMA.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: owner nationality is not a type. Do not invent refusal. Passport may be owner ID. Do not print the number. Legal-name bank proof still applies.

## Application

Classify retail as permitted. Do not refuse under FEMA because of OCI. A passport may fill owner ID; do not print it; it is not bank proof. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether OCI or PIO status of the owner is a type that can be refused, or changes bank proof. On these facts (The owner holds OCI. Stated activity is retail. They offered a foreign passport as owner ID and asked about FEMA.) the held is: Ownership nationality is not a type. Retail remains permitted. Passport may be owner ID; never bank proof; never printed. Cite ONB-ANX-003; FEMA briefing.

## Held

Ownership nationality is not a type. Retail remains permitted. Passport may be owner ID; never bank proof; never printed.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-004. Do not invent an OCI refusal. Do not print passport numbers.
