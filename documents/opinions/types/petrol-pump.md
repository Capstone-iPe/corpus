## Issue

Whether fuel retail is §3.1 retail or unlisted, and whether PESO or Petroleum Rules add a pack item.

## Facts

The merchant stated petrol pump. They argue fuel is goods sold at retail.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: petrol pump is unlisted; Petroleum Rules / PESO are background; fuel is not grocery or general retail in §3.1 as drafted.

## Application

§3.1’s retail is the ordinary shop, not every sale of a commodity. Fuel retailing sits with specialised safety and licensing regimes; those regimes are not slots and not Copilot activation. Do not stretch grocery because engine oil is on a rack. Say unlisted, in review, no fifth PESO file demanded by the Copilot.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. PESO is not a pack item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not demand PESO as a fifth item. Do not activate.
