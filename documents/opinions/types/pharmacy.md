## Issue

Whether a pharmacy is grocery because medicines are sold like SKUs, or unlisted, and whether the Drugs and Cosmetics Act adds a slot or a refusal.

## Facts

The merchant stated pharmacy: dispensing medicines. They also sell soap. They offer a drug licence as pack proof.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: pharmacy is unlisted; do not treat as grocery. Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 is background. It is not a fifth pack item and not a Copilot refusal list.

## Application

Dispensing medicines is not kirana grocery as this product uses the word. Soap does not convert the stated type. A drug licence may be interesting to a human reviewer; it is not registration of the business unless that is the file in the registration slot, and it is not bank proof. Do not refuse as crypto. Do not recommend go-live. Say the policy does not cover pharmacy.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Not grocery. Licence is not a fifth slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not invent a drug-licence slot. Do not activate.
