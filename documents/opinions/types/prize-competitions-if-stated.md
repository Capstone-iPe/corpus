## Issue

Whether a shop that states prize competitions as its business is unlisted entertainment or gambling.

## Facts

Merchant stated prize competitions as the business, for money.

## Rule

Prize Competitions Act briefing supports gambling/refusal analysis when they described prize competitions as the business. ONB-POL-001 §3.2.

## Application

They named prize competitions as the business, not a cafe with a lucky draw poster. Refuse as gambling for this product. No review identifier. Do not accuse under the 1955 Act. Do not activate. The issue is: Whether a shop that states prize competitions as its business is unlisted entertainment or gambling. On these facts (Merchant stated prize competitions as the business, for money.) the held is: Refused as gambling on these facts. No identifier. Cite ONB-ANX-003; Prize Competitions Act briefing.

## Held

Refused as gambling on these facts. No identifier.
