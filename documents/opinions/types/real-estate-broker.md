## Issue

Whether a RERA-registered broker is unlisted or retail of properties, and whether RERA registration is a pack slot.

## Facts

The merchant stated real estate broker and offers a RERA number.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: brokers are unlisted. RERA, 2016 is background. RERA registration is not a pack slot.

## Application

Intermediation in immovable property is not §3.1 retail of goods. The Copilot shall not print the RERA number, shall not add a slot for it, shall not recommend go-live, and shall say unlisted. In review. The issue is: Whether a RERA-registered broker is unlisted or retail of properties, and whether RERA registration is a pack slot. On these facts (The merchant stated real estate broker and offers a RERA number.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. RERA is not a fifth item. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. RERA is not a fifth item.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003 and ONB-POL-004. Do not print the RERA number.
