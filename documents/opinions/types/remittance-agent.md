## Issue

Whether a domestic or cross-border remittance agent is money service or an unlisted financial intermediary for a person to decide.

## Facts

The merchant stated remittance agent: they take cash and arrange payout to a beneficiary on another corridor or in another city.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 treats remittance agent and money-transfer kiosk as money service under §3.2. FEMA and the PSS Act are background. Unlisted treatment is for types in neither §3.1 nor §3.2.

## Application

A remittance agent’s product is the transfer, not groceries. Putting it in §3.3 would ask a reviewer to invent a permitted money-service path that §3.2 closed. Principal/agent labels, Money Transfer Service Scheme history, and authorised-dealer categories do not appear in this product’s type list and shall not be used by the Copilot to create a live MID. Refuse, issue no identifier, and do not discuss settlement corridors.

## Held

Refused as money service. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite §3.2. Do not open a FEMA licensing seminar. Do not activate.
