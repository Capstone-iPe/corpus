## Issue

Whether retail of goods from a shop is permitted, and whether personal-care or workshop services can be read as retail because goods are used in the service.

## Facts

The merchant stated retail: sale of goods to consumers from a shop. Separately, other applicants try to stretch salon, gym, or garage into this word.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3.1 permits retail. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching retail to services on the person or workshop services. Completeness of pack does not recast an unlisted type as retail.

## Application

Retail here means a shop selling goods, not every establishment that issues a bill. A salon that uses shampoo, a garage that fits a spare part, or a gym that sells a protein sachet is still the service they stated unless they applied as retail. Treating those as retail would empty §3.3. When the merchant did apply as retail of goods, the type is permitted; shopfront, legal-name bank proof, owner ID, and registration remain. The Copilot shall not recommend go-live for an unlisted type by calling it retail to be helpful.

## Held

Permitted only where the stated type is retail of goods. Services named as salon/gym/garage remain unlisted.

## Copilot

Cite §3.1 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not activate.
