## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated seed shop as seed not retail is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether automatically treating a seeds type as grocery or retail.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as seed shop as seed not retail. Nature of activity: they named seeds as type not retail. They advanced the trap: automatically treating a seeds type as grocery or retail.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **seed shop as seed not retail**: they named seeds as type not retail. The live retrieval risk is automatically treating a seeds type as grocery or retail. Seeds Act is background. In review if they named seeds as the type. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted if named as seeds. Not automatic grocery.
