## Issue

Whether spa or massage is salon-as-retail or unlisted wellness service.

## Facts

The merchant stated spa / massage parlour.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: spa and massage parlour are personal service, not salon-as-retail; unlisted.

## Application

Wellness services are not §3.1. The Copilot shall not analogise to salon-as-retail (salon itself is unlisted). In review; do not refuse as gambling; do not waive shopfront. The issue is: Whether spa or massage is salon-as-retail or unlisted wellness service. On these facts (The merchant stated spa / massage parlour.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review.
