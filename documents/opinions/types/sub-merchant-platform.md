## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated sub-merchant platform is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether relabelling aggregation as SaaS to avoid §3.2 when they described taking others’ funds.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as sub-merchant platform. Nature of activity: aggregation of merchants. They advanced the trap: relabelling aggregation as SaaS to avoid §3.2 when they described taking others’ funds.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **sub-merchant platform**: aggregation of merchants. The live retrieval risk is relabelling aggregation as SaaS to avoid §3.2 when they described taking others’ funds. If they described unlicensed funds aggregation as the business, refuse as money service (ONB-ANX-003). If they only named a platform without that description, unlisted — use the type they wrote. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Use the type they wrote. Do not relabel to be kind. Do not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
