## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated temple prasad stall as temple is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether forcing a temple statement into restaurant because prasad is food.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as temple prasad stall as temple. Nature of activity: religious institution as the stated type. They advanced the trap: forcing a temple statement into restaurant because prasad is food.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **temple prasad stall as temple**: religious institution as the stated type. The live retrieval risk is forcing a temple statement into restaurant because prasad is food. If they applied as restaurant, restaurant — they applied as temple. Person decides. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. ## Held

Unlisted if applied as a religious institution. Do not relabel as restaurant.
