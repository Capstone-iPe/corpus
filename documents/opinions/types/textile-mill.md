## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated textile mill is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether treating a mill as retail because there is a factory-gate counter.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as textile mill. Nature of activity: manufacturing of cloth. They advanced the trap: treating a mill as retail because there is a factory-gate counter.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **textile mill**: manufacturing of cloth. The live retrieval risk is treating a mill as retail because there is a factory-gate counter. Factories Act is background, not a fifth slot. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted where applied as mill. In review. No factory-licence slot.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
