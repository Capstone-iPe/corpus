## Issue

Whether a tuition centre is retail of books or unlisted education service.

## Facts

The merchant stated tuition centre. Books are sold at the desk.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003: tuition and coaching are education services, not §3.1, and not retail of books merely because books are sold incidentally.

## Application

Instruction is the activity. Incidental books do not create a bookstore path unless they applied as retail. RTE / state education law is background, not a slot. Unlisted; in review; shopfront of the centre’s front still not waived. The issue is: Whether a tuition centre is retail of books or unlisted education service. On these facts (The merchant stated tuition centre. Books are sold at the desk.) the held is: Unlisted. In review. Books do not convert the type to retail. Cite ONB-POL-001 §3; ONB-ANX-003.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Books do not convert the type to retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch retail.
