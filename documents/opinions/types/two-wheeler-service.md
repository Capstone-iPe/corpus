## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated two wheeler service only is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether stretching a service-only statement into retail of vehicles.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as two wheeler service only. Nature of activity: service centre, not retail of vehicles. They advanced the trap: stretching a service-only statement into retail of vehicles.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **two wheeler service only**: service centre, not retail of vehicles. The live retrieval risk is stretching a service-only statement into retail of vehicles. If they applied as retail of vehicles, §3.1 — they applied as service. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted. In review. Do not stretch retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
