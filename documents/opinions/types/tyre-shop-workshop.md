## Issue

Whether a merchant who stated tyre shop as workshop is permitted under §3.1, refused under §3.2, or unlisted under §3.3, and whether stretching a workshop application into retail of tyres.

## Facts

The merchant described the business as tyre shop as workshop. Nature of activity: fitting workshop not retail. They advanced the trap: stretching a workshop application into retail of tyres.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §3 divides permitted (restaurant, grocery, retail), refused (money service, crypto, gambling), and unlisted. ONB-ANX-003 forbids stretching §3.1 and forbids refusing unlisted types as crypto. Sector statutes named below are background, not extra pack items and not Copilot activation.

## Application

They stated **tyre shop as workshop**: fitting workshop not retail. The live retrieval risk is stretching a workshop application into retail of tyres. If they applied as retail, §3.1. They applied as workshop. ONB-ANX-003 requires the Copilot to use the type as written rather than a kinder alias (retail, grocery, restaurant) or a harsher §3.2 label that they did not earn. Sector licences, FSSAI, Udyam, GSTIN, and pack completeness do not recast the type and are not fifth pack items. Shopfront photograph is not waived because the activity is indoors, mobile, online, or sited in a factory, dwelling, airport, or highway. The Copilot shall not print licence numbers, shall not accuse, and shall not activate. Held follows from ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Held

Unlisted on these facts. Do not stretch retail.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §3 and ONB-ANX-003. Do not stretch. Do not invent a fifth slot. Do not activate. Do not print licence numbers.
