iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether a person who describes unlicensed merchant-fund aggregation as their business is money service or an unlisted SaaS platform.

## Facts

The merchant stated their business as aggregating other shops’ funds without claiming a PA authorisation. They say they are SaaS.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-003 maps unlicensed funds aggregation described as the business to money service; RBI PA guidelines are background. SaaS named as type is unlisted — but only if that is the type they stated, not a relabel of aggregation.

## Application

If the stated business is taking other merchants’ money, that is money service for this product, not a coworking-style unlisted SaaS. PA guidelines explain why iPe itself is fictional-aggregator shaped; they do not let an applicant skip §3.2 by saying platform. Refuse, no id. Do not onboard them as a sub-merchant of themselves.

## Held

Refused as money service. No review identifier.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-003. Do not relabel as SaaS to keep them in review.
