iPe (India Pay) is a fictional payments business. This memorandum is for retrieval after onboarding operations have acted. It does not decide the next field, accept a file, or activate a merchant.

---

## Issue

Whether one rupee above forty lakh changes the reviewer, and whether that person then activates in chat.

## Facts

The application states ₹40,00,001.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §3.2: above INR 40,00,000, including INR 40,00,001, the Head of Payments is the reviewer. ONB-POL-001 §4: only the Merchant Risk Lead may activate. Both sentences shall be shown (ONB-POL-002 §5).

## Application

Routing and activation are different verbs. The Copilot shall cite both policies and shall not suppress the Lead’s activation monopoly to make the story tidy. It shall not activate, shall not lower the figure to keep the file with the Lead, and shall not tell the merchant they are live because Head of Payments ‘has’ the file.

## Held

Head of Payments is reviewer. Lead remains the only activator. Copilot activates no one.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 and ONB-POL-001 §4 together. Do not choose a winner in silence.
