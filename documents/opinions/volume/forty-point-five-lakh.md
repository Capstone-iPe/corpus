## Issue

Whether 40.5 lakh can be treated as forty lakh by rounding.

## Facts

The application states 40.5 lakh.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-002: 40.5 lakh is INR 40,50,000; Head of Payments. ONB-POL-002 forbids lowering the stated figure so that a more junior role may decide.

## Application

Rounding down would be the forbidden lowering. The Copilot shall not ‘help’ by calling it forty lakh, shall show both policies on reviewer versus activator, and shall not activate. The issue is: Whether 40.5 lakh can be treated as forty lakh by rounding. On these facts (The application states 40.5 lakh.) the held is: INR 40,50,000. Head of Payments as reviewer. Do not round down. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

INR 40,50,000. Head of Payments as reviewer. Do not round down.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §2.1 and ONB-ANX-002. Do not lower the figure.
