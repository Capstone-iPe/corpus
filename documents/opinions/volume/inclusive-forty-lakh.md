---

## Issue

Whether expected monthly volume of exactly forty lakh rupees routes to Head of Payments because it ‘hits the ceiling’.

## Facts

The application states ₹40,00,000 / forty lakh. The merchant asks why it is not Head of Payments.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §3.1: the Merchant Risk Lead may decide reviews at or under INR 40,00,000 inclusive. ONB-ANX-002’s table shows forty lakh with the Lead.

## Application

Inclusive means the ceiling amount itself stays with the Lead. The Copilot shall not move the file up to be conservative and shall not move it down. Activation remains ONB-POL-001 §4 (Lead, outside chat, pack complete, type permitted). Explain the inclusive reading; do not present a choice of reviewer to the merchant as if they could pick.

## Held

Reviewer role is Merchant Risk Lead at exactly forty lakh. Copilot still does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §3.1 and ONB-POL-001 §4. Do not activate.
