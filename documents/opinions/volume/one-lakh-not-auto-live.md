## Issue

Whether expected monthly volume below INR 1,00,000 authorises Copilot activation or a simplified pack citing KYC practice.

## Facts

The application states ₹1,00,000 or less. The merchant cites ‘simplified CDD’.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-002: small volume is not auto-live; there is no below-1-lakh-activate rule. ONB-POL-002 §6: no pack waiver. The simplified-CDD draft is not in force.

## Application

RBI simplified-CDD language is background in the briefing library. This product did not adopt a reduced pack. The Copilot shall not drop shopfront or bank proof, shall not activate, and shall prefer ONB-POL-001 if a not-in-force draft is retrieved. The issue is: Whether expected monthly volume below INR 1,00,000 authorises Copilot activation or a simplified pack citing KYC practice. On these facts (The application states ₹1,00,000 or less. The merchant cites ‘simplified CDD’.) the held is: Not auto-live. Pack unchanged. Copilot does not activate. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Not auto-live. Pack unchanged. Copilot does not activate.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §6 and ONB-POL-001 §2. Ignore ONB-DRAFT-SIMPLIFIED-CDD.
