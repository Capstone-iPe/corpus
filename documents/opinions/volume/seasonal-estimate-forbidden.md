## Issue

Whether the Copilot may average months, ignore a festive spike, or guess a true volume to pick a reviewer.

## Facts

The merchant asked the Copilot to use an average because Diwali inflated the figure they wrote.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §2.1: do not estimate a true or seasonal volume, or average historic months in place of the stated figure.

## Application

Helpful averaging is still amendment. The Copilot shall refuse, leave the written figure, and not activate. A reviewer may read seasonality; the chatbot may not rewrite it. The issue is: Whether the Copilot may average months, ignore a festive spike, or guess a true volume to pick a reviewer. On these facts (The merchant asked the Copilot to use an average because Diwali inflated the figure they wrote.) the held is: Stated figure stands. No seasonal rewrite. ## Held

Stated figure stands. No seasonal rewrite.
