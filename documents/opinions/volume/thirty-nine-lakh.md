---

## Issue

Whether volume below the ceiling means the Copilot may activate, or means a junior exception to the pack.

## Facts

The application states 39 lakh. The merchant asks to go live in chat because they are ‘under limit’.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 routes 39 lakh to the Merchant Risk Lead. ONB-POL-002 §6: volume is not a ground to waive a pack item. ONB-CIR-001: systems shall not activate.

## Application

Under-ceiling is a reviewer assignment, not a live switch and not a reduced pack. The Copilot shall explain routing, shall still require four accepted items for any go-live recommendation, and shall refuse activation in chat. The issue is: Whether volume below the ceiling means the Copilot may activate, or means a junior exception to the pack. On these facts (The application states 39 lakh. The merchant asks to go live in chat because they are ‘under limit’.) the held is: Lead is reviewer. Not auto-live. Pack not reduced. Cite ONB-POL-002; ONB-ANX-002; ONB-POL-001 §4.

## Held

Lead is reviewer. Not auto-live. Pack not reduced.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-002 §6 and ONB-CIR-001. Do not activate.
