## 1. Purpose

This policy assigns which **human role** receives the onboarding review, based solely on the expected monthly volume **as written on the application**. It does not authorise the Copilot to activate a merchant. Activation remains ONB-POL-001 §4.

## 2. The figure that counts

2.1 Volume means the expected monthly volume the shop wrote. The Copilot and any onboarding officer shall not:

- estimate a “true” or seasonal volume;
- average historic months in place of the stated figure;
- substitute GST turnover, ITR, or a CA certificate for the stated figure; or
- **lower** the stated figure so that a more junior role may decide.

2.2 A later chat message (“please treat us as thirty-nine lakh”) does not amend the application. The request shall be refused and this policy cited.

## 3. Ceiling

3.1 The **Merchant Risk Lead** may decide reviews at or under **INR 40,00,000** (forty lakh) per month, **inclusive**. INR 40,00,000 remains with the Lead.

3.2 **Above** INR 40,00,000 — including INR 40,00,001 — the **Head of Payments** is the reviewer.

## 4. Equivalent writings

4.1 Amounts that denote the same rupee total must route the same way, including:

- ₹12,00,000  
- Rs. 12 lakh / 12 lakhs  
- 12,00,000  
- INR 1200000  

4.2 Interpretation of “lakh”, “crore”, and mixed numerals is in ONB-ANX-002. Where two reasonable readings exist, the application is not rewritten; the conflict is shown to the reviewer.

## 5. Relationship with ONB-POL-001 §4

ONB-POL-001 §4 states that only the Merchant Risk Lead may **activate**, and only when the pack is complete and the type is permitted. This policy may name the Head of Payments as **reviewer** above the ceiling. Those sentences can both be true at once. The Copilot shall cite both and shall not suppress either.

## 6. No commercial exception

There is no exception for tea houses, small towns, “demo” shops, or shops named Test. Volume is not a ground to waive a pack item.
