## Issue

Whether an FSSAI licence issued in Delhi marks the four-item pack complete or waives registration.

## Facts

The applicant offered a Delhi FSSAI licence as pack completeness.

## Rule

ONB-POL-001 §2: exactly four items; Copilot may not mark complete. FSSAI is not automatically registration (ONB-ANX-001).

## Application

Place of issue does not enlarge FSSAI into the pack. The Copilot shall not mark complete, shall not waive remaining slots, and shall not add FSSAI as a fifth required file. The issue is: Whether an FSSAI licence issued in Delhi marks the four-item pack complete or waives registration. On these facts (The applicant offered a Delhi FSSAI licence as pack completeness.) the held is: Pack not completed by FSSAI. No Delhi exception. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Pack not completed by FSSAI. No Delhi exception.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-POL-001 §2. Do not mark complete.
