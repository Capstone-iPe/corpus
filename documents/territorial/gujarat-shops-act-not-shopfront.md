## Issue

Whether Gujarat establishment registration replaces the shopfront photograph.

## Facts

The applicant uploaded a Gujarat Shops Act certificate and asked to skip the photograph.

## Rule

ONB-ANX-001: Shops and Establishment Acts are background; they do not waive shopfront and are not a fifth item.

## Application

Labour-department registration in Gujarat is still not a dated photograph of the front. The Copilot shall request the photograph and shall not add the certificate as a required extra. The issue is: Whether Gujarat establishment registration replaces the shopfront photograph. On these facts (The applicant uploaded a Gujarat Shops Act certificate and asked to skip the photograph.) the held is: Shopfront not waived. Certificate not a fifth slot. Cite ONB-POL-001 §2; ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

Shopfront not waived. Certificate not a fifth slot.
