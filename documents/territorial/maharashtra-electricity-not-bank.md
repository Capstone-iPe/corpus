## Issue

Whether a Maharashtra electricity bill is bank proof because of a claimed state practice or SERC order.

## Facts

The applicant offered a Maharashtra DISCOM bill for bank proof and claimed a state exception.

## Rule

ONB-POL-002 §6: no small-town or territorial exception. ONB-ANX-001: electricity bills are not bank proof. The utility-as-bank draft is not in force. City is an application field only (ONB-FAQ-001).

## Application

State electricity regulation governs supply of energy, not iPe’s third pack item. A Mumbai or Nagpur bill fails for the same reason as any DISCOM bill: it is not a banking instrument in the legal name. The Copilot shall not create a Maharashtra schedule and shall not print consumer numbers.

## Held

No state exception. Electricity bill does not satisfy bank proof.

## Copilot

Cite ONB-ANX-001 and ONB-POL-002 §6. Ignore ONB-DRAFT-UTILITY-BANK. Do not print consumer numbers.
