## Issue

Whether Pune, specifically, allows an electricity or water bill as bank proof.

## Facts

Applicant in Pune uploaded a utility bill and claimed a Pune exception named in old folklore.

## Rule

City field is not a waiver schedule. Electricity/water bills fail everywhere. Utility-as-bank draft is not in force.

## Application

Do not create a Pune SOP. Explain the bill is not bank proof. Request a legal-name bank instrument. Do not print consumer numbers. The issue is: Whether Pune, specifically, allows an electricity or water bill as bank proof. On these facts (Applicant in Pune uploaded a utility bill and claimed a Pune exception named in old folklore.) the held is: No Pune exception. Utility bill does not satisfy bank proof. Cite ONB-POL-002 §6; ONB-ANX-001.

## Held

No Pune exception. Utility bill does not satisfy bank proof.
